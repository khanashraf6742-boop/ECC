## B4-01 — One year: identify the covered category

**Exact reference:** Rule 7(1), read with Rule 1(3)(i)/(iii) and Rule 2

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** For Rule 1(3)(i) civil/Defence-civilian and (iii) contract categories, one year continuous Central Government service must be completed on the journey date. Rule 2 and other category-specific conditions still apply; inclusion is not an unconditional concession.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Categories one aur three: one year continuous Central service plus other conditions.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Ravi: ten months service; leave alone enough nahi.

**Watch out:** Do not replace continuous service with probation completion or assume all categories have identical service tests.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B4-02 — Check the actual traveller’s date

**Exact reference:** Rule 7(1): employee or family journey date

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The one-year test is applied on the date of the journey performed by the employee or family, as applicable, not the application, ticket purchase or bill date. Separate family travel does not borrow the employee's later eligibility date.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Service gate actual self ya family journey date par check karo.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Family ten months par gayi; later eligibility cure nahi.

**Watch out:** For fresh recruits, personal occasion calendars are a separate Batch 5 question.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B4-03 — Casual and special casual leave count

**Exact reference:** Rule 7(2); FAQ 25; OM 03.04.2018

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** LTC is admissible during any period of leave, including casual and special casual leave; Child Care Leave is expressly recognised. The relevant kind of leave must itself be due/admissible and sanctioned as required; an LTC allowance does not create leave.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Casual aur special casual leave allowed; leave properly approved ho.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Neha casual leave par jaaye; earned leave compulsory nahi.

**Watch out:** LTC eligibility is not a direction that every leave application must be sanctioned.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B4-04 — Vacation departments: vacation counts as leave

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (1), vacation paragraph; OM 11.10.1956

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Vacation is treated as regular leave for this concession for government servants serving in a vacation department. It is a specific deeming instruction, not permission to treat every employee's ordinary weekend as vacation.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Vacation department ki vacation regular leave count hoti hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Teacher vacation par jaaye; office weekend alag hai.

**Watch out:** Department/vacation status and other entitlement conditions still need checking.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B4-05 — Weekend-only travel is not enough for self

**Exact reference:** FAQ 27; 2025 Rule 7 Note 2

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** When the employee avails LTC, weekend/holidays alone without leave are insufficient. Due-and-admissible leave is required. Do not invent a universal number of leave days, or require every travel hour to fall within leave while ignoring authorised prefix/suffix rules.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Self ka weekend only trip bina leave LTC nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Ravi Saturday Sunday ghooma, leave zero; claim assume nahi.

**Watch out:** A proper leave spell with permissible prefixed/suffixed holidays is a different factual case.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B4-06 — Family-only travel: employee need not take leave

**Exact reference:** FAQ 26; Rule 9 separate-family travel comparison

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** If only family members travel, the employee need not avail leave merely to enable their LTC. Eligibility, declaration, evidence and the employee's applicable family/block entitlement remain required.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Family only journey par employee ko leave necessary nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Neha office mein, eligible spouse hometown jaaye.

**Watch out:** Do not apply the employee-traveller weekend prohibition as a blanket family-only bar.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B4-07 — CCL does not create bonus LTC occasions

**Exact reference:** Rule 7(2); OM 13018/6/2013-Estt(L), 03.04.2018; Rule 9

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Child Care Leave is a qualifying leave setting, not an extra LTC occasion or a new block. Repeated CCL spells do not multiply entitlement. CCL's own purpose/sanction restrictions remain applicable; saying CCL has zero effect on all administration would be overbroad.

**Simple city Hinglish:** CCL valid leave hai, extra LTC occasion nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Three CCL spells se three bonus trips nahi.

**Watch out:** Normal entitlement and outward-date block accounting still apply.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B4-08 — Resigning without returning to duty

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (2): ordinary resignation after leave

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The concession is not admissible to a government servant who proceeds on regular leave and then resigns without returning to duty. This is not a blanket statement about every technical resignation, nor an absolute determination of every possible Rule 18 relaxation.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Regular leave ke baad resign, duty return nahi: concession inadmissible.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Ravi trip ke baad job chhod de; normal claim fail.

**Watch out:** Technical resignation preserving eligible service is examined separately; do not copy the master's absolute no-relaxation assertion.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B4-09 — Technical resignation is a qualifying process

**Exact reference:** DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.1.1; 17.08.2016 origin

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Applied through proper channel to a government post, selected, and required to resign for administrative reasons: qualifying resignation is technical even if the letter omits that word. Other resignations, blocked forwarding and ad hoc-post cases do not automatically earn this past-service benefit.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Proper channel selection aur administrative resignation qualify, label alone nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Letter mein technical word nahi; qualifying facts matter.

**Watch out:** This is not automatic protection for every private/PSU/State-to-Centre move.

## B4-13 — LTC entitlement can carry with technical resignation

**Exact reference:** DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.3; 2025 Rule 7 Note 4

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A Central Government servant joining another government post after technical resignation may carry LTC entitlement forward. Records of occasions already used must move too; carry-forward between offices is not a new block or a second reimbursement.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Eligible technical resignation par LTC carry, used trips bhi record hon.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** New office se same used trip repeat nahi.

**Watch out:** This is office-to-office preservation, not Rule 10 grace-year extension.

## B4-24 — Foreign hometown: limited Indian-side assistance

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (3), hometown outside India; OM 04.09.1957

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A foreign hometown may be declared subject to CO satisfaction. Assistance is limited to shortest-route travel up to/from the Indian railway station nearest hometown, or the railway station for the Indian port of embarkation/disembarkation nearest hometown. It is not foreign-side or international-airfare reimbursement.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Foreign hometown assistance specified Indian railhead ya nearest port station tak.
