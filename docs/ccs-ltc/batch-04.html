Scope, currentness and corrections

Scope: master chapters 10-15, Rule 7(1)-(2), applicable admissibility GIDs and cross-topic controls. The narration is authored in exact-reference, city-Hinglish point, immediate hypothetical example order; consult the release README for media-completion status. All authored restatements are educational, not verbatim official quotations or actual claim sanctions.

Review date 27 October 2026. Primary current substance checked in official government-hosted full rules/GIDs, 2025 FAQ and September consolidation; technical resignation additionally checked in the government-hosted 24 November 2022 DoPT consolidation. Targeted searches did not identify a verified later amendment to these admissibility clauses, but a negative search is not proof none exists. Standalone original historical OMs/Gazette were not all independently retrieved.

Evidence distinction: C07, P18 and P20 are full DoPT-document reproductions on a non-government host. The current official 2025 consolidation independently cites the combination extension and posted-staff/Emergency Passage framework; detailed 2007/2018/2020 clauses were read in those reproduced copies. Do not describe the reproduction host as a government site or claim independent authentication of the original primary-hosted scans. Verify originals with the sanctioning office for a contested case.

Master corrections: training is NOT safely reduced to automatic HQ shift. GID (20)(i) uses automatic wording, while detailed (ii) normally retains original HQ unless competent SR 59 change; 2025 repeats the tension. This edition discloses it and directs examination of actual competent orders. The broken sentence preceding the transfer-combination option in the older compilation is not silently reconstructed.

Master corrections: government spouses living together have had the operative separate-hometown option since 1990; mandatory common-hometown prose is the older position. Normal regular entitlement is NOT two hometown PLUS an extra All-India. The annual posted-staff option is IN LIEU of normal LTC; only Emergency Passage is expressly additional. An escort employee must not HAVE an adult family member under the source wording, not merely have one who happens to be busy.

Rule 7(1) expressly identifies Rule 1(3)(i)/(iii) categories. Rule 2 and distinct deputation/category terms remain necessary. Technical resignation is fact-based; correct process can qualify without a magic word, and merely writing technical does not qualify a private move. The 2022 para 2.1.2 pre-joining-application route requires all three conditions. Prior State/PSU service is not automatically continuous Central service; authorised special preservation/deputation provisions are not ruled out.

The phrase deemed availment in the master is an application, not a separately numbered sentence in Rule 9. Distinguish a declared, sanctioned and actually performed LTC with nil/no fare claim from a cancelled unperformed booking, and from an ordinary private trip. Do not promise every late claim is irretrievably lost: official delegated delayed admission exists, addressed fully in Batch 11.

The original home-and-back/actual-distance cap is reconciled with express one-leg GID (5) cases and later 1987/2017 away-family rules. Batch 2 already teaches all six GID (5) illustrations; Batch 3 covers GID (4), and Batch 1 covers GIDs (6)/(9). They are not newly renumbered or erased. Rail class uses journey-time entitlement, not the obsolete pay bands in the old compilation.

Posted-staff geographical scope is NER, UT Ladakh, A&N and Lakshadweep, not all of J&K and not merely visitors. Annual visits and Emergency Passage use the restricted spouse/dependent-child family subset. The 2020 clarification permits the declared selected family residence. These standing instructions are distinct from the time-limited special air-dispensation scheme; the reported September 2026 air extension is not authenticated here. Do not teach the master's 25 September 2026 date as the current validity of every special arrangement.

Chapters 10-15 include comparisons on fresh recruits, government spouses, cancellation/encashment, rail booking charges and statutory levies. Their applicable points are included here without claiming completion of later specialist batches 5, 6-10, 11 and 12. The 2025 FAQ jumps from Q27 to Q29: no invented Q28. Full-series remaining GIDs are still pending batchwise.

All amounts, routes, employee names and cases are invented teaching aids. An admissibility gate is not full sanction: entitlement class, route, prior permissions, actual travel, documentation, available block and competent decisions all remain necessary. Governing rules/orders prevail over compilation imprecision; this is not an error-free legal certificate.

B4-01

One year: identify the covered category

Rule 7(1), read with Rule 1(3)(i)/(iii) and Rule 2

Rule / current restatement: For Rule 1(3)(i) civil/Defence-civilian and (iii) contract categories, one year continuous Central Government service must be completed on the journey date. Rule 2 and other category-specific conditions still apply; inclusion is not an unconditional concession.

Simple city Hinglish: Categories one aur three: one year continuous Central service plus other conditions.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi: ten months service; leave alone enough nahi.

Watch out: Do not replace continuous service with probation completion or assume all categories have identical service tests.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-02

Check the actual traveller’s date

Rule 7(1): employee or family journey date

Rule / current restatement: The one-year test is applied on the date of the journey performed by the employee or family, as applicable, not the application, ticket purchase or bill date. Separate family travel does not borrow the employee's later eligibility date.

Simple city Hinglish: Service gate actual self ya family journey date par check karo.

Immediate hypothetical example: Family ten months par gayi; later eligibility cure nahi.

Watch out: For fresh recruits, personal occasion calendars are a separate Batch 5 question.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-03

Casual and special casual leave count

Rule 7(2); FAQ 25; OM 03.04.2018

Rule / current restatement: LTC is admissible during any period of leave, including casual and special casual leave; Child Care Leave is expressly recognised. The relevant kind of leave must itself be due/admissible and sanctioned as required; an LTC allowance does not create leave.

Simple city Hinglish: Casual aur special casual leave allowed; leave properly approved ho.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha casual leave par jaaye; earned leave compulsory nahi.

Watch out: LTC eligibility is not a direction that every leave application must be sanctioned.

Evidence: H; G25; F25

B4-04

Vacation departments: vacation counts as leave

Rule 7 GID (1), vacation paragraph; OM 11.10.1956

Rule / current restatement: Vacation is treated as regular leave for this concession for government servants serving in a vacation department. It is a specific deeming instruction, not permission to treat every employee's ordinary weekend as vacation.

Simple city Hinglish: Vacation department ki vacation regular leave count hoti hai.

Immediate hypothetical example: Teacher vacation par jaaye; office weekend alag hai.

Watch out: Department/vacation status and other entitlement conditions still need checking.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-05

Weekend-only travel is not enough for self

FAQ 27; 2025 Rule 7 Note 2

Rule / current restatement: When the employee avails LTC, weekend/holidays alone without leave are insufficient. Due-and-admissible leave is required. Do not invent a universal number of leave days, or require every travel hour to fall within leave while ignoring authorised prefix/suffix rules.

Simple city Hinglish: Self ka weekend only trip bina leave LTC nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi Saturday Sunday ghooma, leave zero; claim assume nahi.

Watch out: A proper leave spell with permissible prefixed/suffixed holidays is a different factual case.

Evidence: F25; G25

B4-06

Family-only travel: employee need not take leave

FAQ 26; Rule 9 separate-family travel comparison

Rule / current restatement: If only family members travel, the employee need not avail leave merely to enable their LTC. Eligibility, declaration, evidence and the employee's applicable family/block entitlement remain required.

Simple city Hinglish: Family only journey par employee ko leave necessary nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha office mein, eligible spouse hometown jaaye.

Watch out: Do not apply the employee-traveller weekend prohibition as a blanket family-only bar.

Evidence: F25; H

B4-07

CCL does not create bonus LTC occasions

Rule 7(2); OM 13018/6/2013-Estt(L), 03.04.2018; Rule 9

Rule / current restatement: Child Care Leave is a qualifying leave setting, not an extra LTC occasion or a new block. Repeated CCL spells do not multiply entitlement. CCL's own purpose/sanction restrictions remain applicable; saying CCL has zero effect on all administration would be overbroad.

Simple city Hinglish: CCL valid leave hai, extra LTC occasion nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Three CCL spells se three bonus trips nahi.

Watch out: Normal entitlement and outward-date block accounting still apply.

Evidence: G25; F25; H

B4-08

Resigning without returning to duty

Rule 7 GID (2): ordinary resignation after leave

Rule / current restatement: The concession is not admissible to a government servant who proceeds on regular leave and then resigns without returning to duty. This is not a blanket statement about every technical resignation, nor an absolute determination of every possible Rule 18 relaxation.

Simple city Hinglish: Regular leave ke baad resign, duty return nahi: concession inadmissible.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi trip ke baad job chhod de; normal claim fail.

Watch out: Technical resignation preserving eligible service is examined separately; do not copy the master's absolute no-relaxation assertion.

Evidence: H

B4-09

Technical resignation is a qualifying process

DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.1.1; 17.08.2016 origin

Rule / current restatement: Applied through proper channel to a government post, selected, and required to resign for administrative reasons: qualifying resignation is technical even if the letter omits that word. Other resignations, blocked forwarding and ad hoc-post cases do not automatically earn this past-service benefit.

Simple city Hinglish: Proper channel selection aur administrative resignation qualify, label alone nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Letter mein technical word nahi; qualifying facts matter.

Watch out: This is not automatic protection for every private/PSU/State-to-Centre move.

Evidence: T22; G25

B4-10

Pre-joining application: immediate disclosure

DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.1.2(i)

Rule / current restatement: For an application made before joining government service, the special route requires immediate intimation of those application details on joining; this is the first of three cumulative conditions.

Simple city Hinglish: Pre service application joining par immediately disclose karo.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha joining par pending application disclose kare.

Watch out: Not a universal exemption from the proper-channel system.

Evidence: T22

B4-11

Pre-joining application: specific resignation request

DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.1.2(ii)

Rule / current restatement: At resignation, specifically request treatment for taking up the other government appointment applied for before entering service. This does not dispense with the other two paragraph 2.1.2 conditions.

Simple city Hinglish: Resignation mein pre service applied Government job ka specific request do.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha new selection ka purpose clearly likhe.

Watch out: Factual qualifying circumstances, not a magic heading, control.

Evidence: T22

B4-12

Pre-joining application: authority’s satisfaction

DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.1.2(iii)

Rule / current restatement: The accepting authority must be satisfied that, had the employee already been serving when applying, the application would have been forwarded through proper channel. All three pre-service-application conditions must be assessed.

Simple city Hinglish: Authority satisfy ho ki application proper channel se forward hoti.

Immediate hypothetical example: Forwarding allowed nahi, toh automatic benefit nahi.

Watch out: Do not reduce the three conditions to employee self-certification alone.

Evidence: T22

B4-13

LTC entitlement can carry with technical resignation

DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.3; 2025 Rule 7 Note 4

Rule / current restatement: A Central Government servant joining another government post after technical resignation may carry LTC entitlement forward. Records of occasions already used must move too; carry-forward between offices is not a new block or a second reimbursement.

Simple city Hinglish: Eligible technical resignation par LTC carry, used trips bhi record hon.

Immediate hypothetical example: New office se same used trip repeat nahi.

Watch out: This is office-to-office preservation, not Rule 10 grace-year extension.

Evidence: T22; G25

B4-14

The initial appointment date does not restart

DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.3: eight-year period

Rule / current restatement: A qualifying move within eight years preserves fresh-recruit treatment for the remainder of eight years from the INITIAL government appointment, not a fresh eight years at each new department. The consolidation itself gives four years served plus four remaining as illustration.

Simple city Hinglish: Fresh recruit eight years initial appointment se; reset nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Four served, four remain; eight new nahi.

Watch out: Exact personal occasion accounting is taught in Batch 5.

Evidence: T22; G25

B4-15

Prior private work is not automatically Central service

Rule 7(1); DoPT 24.11.2022 para 2.3: lateral-entry comparison

Rule / current restatement: Do not treat previous private/PSU/State employment as continuous Central service or a preserved Central fresh-recruit cycle merely because it was called government-related. Examine actual appointment/deputation and any applicable service-preservation instruction; AIS and autonomous-body deputation have distinct rules.

Simple city Hinglish: Private service automatically Central qualifying service nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Private firm ke five years se Central one year gate satisfy nahi.

Watch out: Not a blanket denial of every specially authorised deputation/service-continuity benefit.

Evidence: H; T22

B4-16

Suspension: no self LTC

Rule 7 GID (15); FAQ 12: employee

Rule / current restatement: A suspended employee cannot avail self LTC: leave, including casual leave, is not available during suspension. Suspension is not retirement/resignation; family eligibility is separately preserved because service continues.

Simple city Hinglish: Suspension mein self LTC nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi suspended hai; apna holiday self LTC na claim kare.

Watch out: Do not invent a right to cure suspension by applying for casual leave.

Evidence: H; G25; F25

B4-17

Suspension: eligible family may still travel

Rule 7 GID (15); FAQs 12 and 26: family

Rule / current restatement: Family remains eligible during employee suspension, subject to normal family/block conditions. Employee need not personally travel or obtain leave for a family-only journey. This is not a second independent family scheme or a change of the accepted hometown.

Simple city Hinglish: Suspension mein eligible family LTC, employee ko leave nahi chahiye.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi ghar par, spouse hometown; normal family block use hoga.

Watch out: Ordinary eligibility survives; dismissal/removal and other restrictions require their own analysis.

Evidence: H; G25; F25

B4-18

Starting away from HQ does not remove the cap

Rule 7 GID (1), route/actual-distance opening; OM 11.10.1956

Rule / current restatement: The normal hometown journey is to home and back but need not start/end at HQ. Assistance for actual distance travelled is limited to the amount admissible for the HQ-home journey. Apply later specific exceptions, notably the 2017 family-to-HQ concession, rather than mechanically overriding them.

Simple city Hinglish: Normal actual fare HQ hometown cap ke andar; specific exceptions alag.

Immediate hypothetical example: Actual four thousand, cap three: ceiling three thousand.

Watch out: Figures are illustrative; no universal rupee ceiling. Rule 13 details follow later.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-19

Entitlement is time-specific

Rule 7 GID (1), rail-class paragraph; 2025 Note 1(ii)

Rule / current restatement: Rail accommodation class for employee/family is the class admissible under the normal rules WHEN THE JOURNEY IS UNDERTAKEN. Sanction date, joining date and a later promotion do not replace that test; apply the current Rule 12 framework rather than obsolete pay tables.

Simple city Hinglish: Rail class journey time ki entitlement se decide hoti hai.

Immediate hypothetical example: Later promotion se earlier travel class upgrade nahi.

Watch out: Different legs may require their own date-specific examination.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-20

Inform the CO before travelling

Rule 7 GID (1), prior-intimation paragraph; 2025 Note 1(iii)

Rule / current restatement: Inform the Controlling Officer before journeys for which LTC assistance will be claimed. This is distinct from sanctioning leave, declaring the All-India destination under Rule 6, or drawing an advance.

Simple city Hinglish: Journey se pehle Controlling Officer ko inform karo.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ticket booking ki, par prior office intimation bhi chahiye.

Watch out: A ticket booking is not by itself a declaration or approval.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-21

A booking is not proof of travel

Rule 7 GID (1), actual-journey evidence paragraph

Rule / current restatement: Produce evidence of actually performing the journey, such as ticket serial numbers and applicable records. A booked but cancelled ticket is not actual travel. Particular proof/boarding-pass instructions are addressed in the procedure batch; no new universal affidavit substitute is created here.

Simple city Hinglish: Actual journey evidence do, booking alone enough nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Cancelled email ticket se actual trip prove nahi.

Watch out: Evidence must support the actual persons, dates and route claimed.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-22

Keep an assistance record and outward dates

Rule 7 GID (1), assistance-record paragraph

Rule / current restatement: Maintain all assistance granted: the historical instruction places gazetted-officer records with the Accounts Officer and non-gazetted entries in service books/appropriate records, including outward home-journey commencement dates. Follow the applicable current record system; modern digital systems do not justify no audit trail.

Simple city Hinglish: Gazetted ka Accounts record, others ka service record; outward dates rakho.

Immediate hypothetical example: Transfer par old LTC dates new office ko dein.

Watch out: The allocation is the source wording, not a claim that every modern office uses paper only.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-23

Record both use and non-use of LTC

Rule 7 GID (1), every-leave entry; 2025 Note 1(iv)

Rule / current restatement: The service-record custodian must indicate on every occasion of leave entered in that record whether travel assistance was or was not availed. The requirement concerns leave entered in that record, not an invented service-book entry for every casual-leave day.

Simple city Hinglish: Recorded leave par LTC li ya nahi, custodian record kare.

Immediate hypothetical example: Earned leave mein LTC nahi li; non use bhi note ho.

Watch out: This helps prevent duplicate claims, but is not the only eligibility check.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-24

Foreign hometown: limited Indian-side assistance

Rule 7 GID (3), hometown outside India; OM 04.09.1957

Rule / current restatement: A foreign hometown may be declared subject to CO satisfaction. Assistance is limited to shortest-route travel up to/from the Indian railway station nearest hometown, or the railway station for the Indian port of embarkation/disembarkation nearest hometown. It is not foreign-side or international-airfare reimbursement.

Simple city Hinglish: Foreign hometown assistance specified Indian railhead ya nearest port station tak.

Immediate hypothetical example: Foreign hometown ka full international fare nahi.

Watch out: Nearest port means the Indian port nearest the hometown, not any convenient gateway.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-25

Separate family claims: do not erase one-leg exceptions

Rule 7 GID (3), separate-claims paragraph, read with GID (5)

Rule / current restatement: Employee/family travelling separately may present separate claims; the normal instruction requires each claim for outward and inward journeys. Read the express single-leg situations in GID (5), fully taught in Batch 2, rather than imposing an absolute two-leg bar.

Simple city Hinglish: Separate round trip claims possible; express one leg exceptions bhi hain.

Immediate hypothetical example: Qualifying new spouse return only claim reject nahi.

Watch out: Do not invent transfer/retirement as an automatic single-leg exemption.

Evidence: H

B4-26

Transfer via hometown: incremental distance only

Rule 7 GID (7)(i), regularisation (a); OM 19.07.1960

Rule / current restatement: Old HQ to hometown on regular leave, then to new HQ: minimum transfer TA under SR 124/126, as applicable. Additional LTC is only to the extent the combined via-hometown distance exceeds the distance for which transfer TA is admissible, subject to applicable fares/entitlement. Distance arithmetic is not a second payment for the same segment.

Simple city Hinglish: Via home excess distance beyond transfer TA hi additional LTC basis hai.

Immediate hypothetical example: Twelve hundred via home, eight hundred covered: four hundred extra.

Watch out: Illustrative kilometres, not reimbursement at an invented per-kilometre rate.

Evidence: H; G25

B4-27

You may preserve LTC instead of charging a tiny excess

Rule 7 GID (7), regularisation (a), option and advance paragraphs

Rule / current restatement: An employee may elect not to avail LTC in the combination, preserving an available occasion within its block subject to conditions. Exercise the option for self and family when preferring the transfer-TA claim. If LTC is not availed, adjust any LTC advance against TA entitlement. The old compilation has a broken sentence before this option; do not invent its missing words.

Simple city Hinglish: Transfer claim par self family option do; unused LTC advance TA mein adjust.

Immediate hypothetical example: Small excess par LTC preserve, advance settle.

Watch out: An election is not permission to claim already-paid TA again as LTC.

Evidence: H

B4-28

Tour station -> hometown -> HQ

Rule 7 GID (7)(ii)(a), regularisation (b)

Rule / current restatement: With proper prior permission, HQ-to-tour-station travel attracts tour TA. Tour station to hometown and back to HQ is considered for LTC, deeming tour station the onward starting point, subject to the usual admissible assistance ceiling referenced in the instruction.

Simple city Hinglish: Prior permission: tour to home to HQ LTC; HQ to tour official TA.

Immediate hypothetical example: Delhi tour, Jaipur home, Mumbai HQ: legs separate karo.

Watch out: Not unrestricted reimbursement of every tour detour.

B4-29

HQ -> hometown -> tour station -> HQ

Rule 7 GID (7)(ii)(b), regularisation (c)

Rule / current restatement: With proper prior permission, LTC as admissible covers HQ to hometown; tour TA covers hometown to tour station and back to HQ, regulated under the governing tour rules. Do not replace the split with two full overlapping round-trip claims.

Simple city Hinglish: Prior permission: HQ to home LTC; home to tour to HQ official TA.

Immediate hypothetical example: Mumbai Jaipur Delhi route ka double fare nahi.

B4-38
