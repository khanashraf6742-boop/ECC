## B1-01 · The legal name

**Anchor:** Rule 1(1)

**Spoken reference — audio chapter 2:** Rule one, sub rule one.

**Provision — close restatement:** These rules may be called the Central Civil Services (Leave Travel Concession) Rules, 1988.

**Explanation:** Naam mein LTC ka matlab Leave Travel Concession hai. Yeh pension rules, TA on tour, ya income-tax LTA exemption ka substitute nahi hai. Pehle governing scheme identify karo, phir entitlement decide karo.

## B1-18 · Two years, reckoned from joining

## B1-24 · Scope includes the Government servant

**Anchor:** Rule 3: employee

**Spoken reference — audio chapter 8:** Rule three, employee coverage.

**Provision — close restatement:** LTC covers the Government servant himself or herself and the family.

**Explanation:** Rule 3 beneficiaries ki boundary batata hai. It does not prescribe amounts, classes or frequency. Self-only travel ko sirf isliye reject nahi karna ki family saath nahi hai. Other provisions determine whether that specific journey is admissible.

## Active recall — with reasoned answers

### 1. A Defence civilian clerk and an Armed Forces officer work at the same establishment. Are both excluded?
No. Civilian Defence Government servants are expressly included under Rule 1(3)(i); Armed Forces members are excluded under Rule 1(4)(e), subject to their separate schemes.

### 2. A contract officer has a certified four-year tenure from joining but only six months of completed service. Does duration alone satisfy Rule 2?
No. The completed one-year continuous-service gate is separate; assume no earlier countable continuity.

### 3. After one year is complete, must a two-year contract officer still have two years remaining for Hometown LTC?
No. The two-year certified duration is reckoned from joining the Central Government post, not afresh from the journey date. Other conditions still apply.

### 4. A one-year initial contract is continuously extended to four years. Is the original one-year duration frozen?
No. Rule 2(2) takes total extended contract duration into account.

### 5. No-break re-employment follows an already-used All India LTC. Does a fresh appointment letter refill the same block?
No. Rule 2(3), its proviso and official illustration preserve continuity but do not duplicate the exhausted occasion.

### 6. Must every autonomous-body deputationist obtain the Rule 2 certificate merely because the note says temporary Government servant?
Do not infer that solely from the note. Verify the actual appointment category, applicable service conditions and deputation terms; the note itself grants temporary-servant treatment during deputation.

### 7. Does a conditional Railway All India facility establish ordinary Hometown LTC?
No. The official consolidation acknowledges a limited conditional All India window, not full ordinary coverage.

### 8. Is an exact 21 May 1988 commencement day officially authenticated in this batch?
No. The publication-date trigger is checked; the original Gazette issue and exact publication day remain pending.
