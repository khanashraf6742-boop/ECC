## Corrections applied to the uploaded master

1. **Normal frequency:** two Home Town OR Home Town + All India, not two Home Town plus a third trip and not compulsory All India. Each relevant traveller's adjusted quota still applies.
2. **Rule 10 proviso:** where Home Town entitled, the second two-year Home Town entitlement must be unused/carried into grace. It need not have been travelled first before the previous four-year block ended. A used second Home Town cannot support the old All-India carry.
3. **Rule 9:** each group's outward date matters. A January departure can use valid old-block grace; it is not necessarily current-block-only. Return may cross year-end without changing the charge. Other return/leave/claim conditions remain.
4. **Automatic extension:** no special order is needed for ordinary one-year grace; normal sanction/application requirements are not removed.
5. **FAQ 66:** a normal-scheme election excludes fresh-recruit LTCs in **that four-year block**, not an invented permanent career-long prohibition or an automatic right to reset/switch at will.
6. **Table 4 explanation (ii):** if eighth All India forgone/not availed, following 2024–25 sub-block permits Home Town OR All India. This is not annual fresh carry. The source's “2024–45” is visibly corrected to 2024–25.
7. **Two clocks:** initial appointment is not reset by job change, but eligible calendar occasions start after the service gate. Table 5 has occasion eight in **2033** after joining 1 January 2025, not a cutoff at the eighth joining anniversary. Tables govern the examples.
8. **31 December:** eligibility is next year's 31 December, not joining day. The first slot's outward occasion can be one day; no rule requiring same-day return is invented.
9. **Current label:** 2022–25 was current at FAQ issue; **2026–29** is current on review. Previous eligible normal carry has outward deadline **31 December 2026**, with the All-India condition. Fresh annual slots have no grace.
