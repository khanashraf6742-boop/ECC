## Source corrections and limits

See [the full source notes](batch-03.md):

1. The master's property-first mnemonic is wrong: the legal order is **obligations/visits → residential/joint-family property → near relations → pre-service residence**, proceeding only if the previous criterion is not satisfied. It is not four compulsory conditions.
2. The master's Ministry-only shorthand for Rule 6 relaxation omits **HoD**. Ordinary hometown-change authority, own-CO communication, destination approval and advance-money sanction are different roles.
3. The **03.09.2025 consolidation's Rule 6 sentence compresses incompatible before-commencement timing phrases**. The full government-hosted Rule 6 separately states normal pre-start CO approval and exceptional post-start relaxation. Consolidation paragraph 2 gives precedence to governing OMs/orders over imprecision. We flag this discrepancy, use the complete rule text and do **not** claim a newly authenticated amendment correcting the summary.
4. Rule 5's ordinary one-change ceiling is not an automatic entitlement, nor a full determination of all broader Rule 18 relaxation powers. Rule 18 is a later batch.
5. Regular block **2026–29**, sub-blocks **2026–27/2028–29**, and conditional previous-block carryover through **31.12.2026** are reminders, not full block-accounting or fresh-recruit instruction. Rule 10 conditions remain applicable; fresh-recruit personal cycles differ.
6. Primary substance was reviewed in the government-hosted rules/GIDs, July 2025 FAQ and September 2025 consolidation. Original standalone 1958 OMs/Gazette and the entire original 2015 OM are not claimed independently authenticated. Targeted searches found no verified later amendment to these provisions; absence of a search result is not proof that none exists.

All examples are invented learning cases, not actual sanctioned claims. The governing rule/order and competent departmental decision prevail. This review is not a guarantee of legal certainty or sanction for a particular journey.
