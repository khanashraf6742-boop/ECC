## Opening lessons: reference → explanation → practical example

### Important dates | Origins and legal cut-offs

**D1-01 — Rule 7 GID (1); Rule 19**

Source: BASE.

**Explanation:** yeh purana LTC instruction hai. Rule nineteen ke hisaab se, purane consistent instructions sirf old hone se obsolete nahi hote.

**Example:** aaj ki claim mein purana order dikhe, toh later changes check karo; sirf date dekhkar reject mat karo.

**On-screen memory point:** 11 Oct 1956: foundational instruction. Rule 19 preserves consistent older instructions.

**D1-02 — Rule 1(2), 1988**

Source: BASE.

**Explanation:** Rules ki start date Gazette publication hai, signature date nahi. Uploaded master ki May three aur May twenty one dates original Gazette se abhi authenticate nahi hui hain.

**Example:** do dates alag hon, toh commencement ke liye publication wala record check karo.

**On-screen memory point:** 1988 Rules: Gazette publication triggers commencement; exact calendar day not authenticated.

**D1-03 — Rule 4(d), Explanation 1(i)-(ii)**

Source: BASE.

**Explanation:** October twenty, nineteen ninety seven, two-child restriction ka cutoff hai. Existing larger families aur agle one year mein born children ke exceptions bhi padho.

**Example:** Third child nineteen ninety six mein born tha? Cutoff exception check karo; automatic rejection nahi.

**On-screen memory point:** 20 Oct 1997: two-child restriction cut-off; protected cases and one-year exception matter.

**Caution:** Issue date, effective date and historical cut-off are different. Gazette day remains pending.

### Important dates | Entitlement and air booking

**D1-04 — Rule 12; DoE 13.07.2017; DoPT 19.09.2017**

Source: GUIDE.

**Explanation:** Pay-level based entitlement padho. Levels six to eight ki tour air entitlement ordinary LTC mein automatically nahi aati.

**Example:** Level seven employee ko tour par flight allowed hai; usi wajah se ordinary LTC flight manzoor mat maano.

**On-screen memory point:** 13 Jul / 19 Sep 2017: pay-level framework; TA air entitlement does not automatically mean LTC air.

**D1-05 — Rule 1(4)(d); OM 27.03.2018**

Source: GUIDE.

**Explanation:** Railway employees ke liye conditional All India LTC window hai, once in four-year block. Full pass-surrender procedure Railway batch mein aayega.

**Example:** Railway employee ko isse ordinary Hometown LTC automatically nahi milti.

**On-screen memory point:** 27 Mar 2018: conditional Railway All India LTC window; not ordinary Hometown LTC.

**D1-06 — OM 29.08.2022; OM 10.08.2023**

Source: FAQ.

**Explanation:** Authorized Travel Agent rules ko later exception ke saath padho. Non-air-entitled ki ordinary LTC aur Special Dispensation booking alag hain.

**Example:** Ordinary LTC wali optional ATA booking ko special regional air scheme par apply mat karo.

**On-screen memory point:** 29 Aug 2022 booking rules + 10 Aug 2023 qualification: ordinary and special schemes differ.

**Caution:** Dates identify instructions, not automatic entitlement. Detailed conditions follow in topic batches.

### Important dates | Blocks, carry-forward and outward journey

**D1-10 — Rules 8, 9; FAQ 13-18**

Source: FAQ.

**Explanation:** Regular four-year block January one, twenty twenty six se December thirty one, twenty twenty nine hai. Two-year sub-blocks twenty twenty six to twenty seven aur twenty twenty eight to twenty nine hain.

**Example:** Regular employee ki twenty twenty six journey mein sahi block debit karo, twenty twenty two to twenty five ko current mat bolo.

**On-screen memory point:** Regular block: 1 Jan 2026-31 Dec 2029. Sub-blocks: 2026-27 and 2028-29.

**D1-11 — Rule 10; FAQ 16-18**

Source: BASE.

**Explanation:** Eligible unused previous-block LTC, next block ke first year mein li ja sakti hai. Twenty twenty six grace year hai. Hometown-entitled employee ki All India carry-forward par second sub-block wali condition bhi hai.

**Example:** Unused twenty twenty four to twenty five occasion ho, toh eligibility aur previous usage check karke twenty twenty six mein charge karo.

**On-screen memory point:** 31 Dec 2026: regular previous-block grace-year end, subject to Rule 10 conditions.

**D1-12 — Rule 9; FAQ 64-69**

Source: BASE.

**Explanation:** Block counting mein outward journey ki start date dekho, return ki nahi. Fresh-recruit annual LTC par regular one-year carry-forward nahi lagta.

**Example:** Eligible grace-year journey December thirty one ko start, January mein return ho, toh sirf return date se wrong block mat lagao.

**On-screen memory point:** Outward commencement fixes the block, including Rule 10 carry-forward. Fresh recruits differ.

**Caution:** Regular-block carry-forward is conditional. Fresh-recruit annual occasions have different rules.

### Important dates | Booking guidance and claim deadlines

**D1-13 — OM 29.08.2022 para 1(iv); Cash circular 16.07.2026**

Source: CASH.

**Explanation:** Twenty one days pehle ticket booking encouraged hai. DoPT staff circular thirty days pehle advance application plan karne ko kehta hai. ise universal automatic claim forfeiture mat banao.

**Example:** Twenty days pehle booking hui? sirf one day kam hone se automatic rejection mat karo; applicable instructions check karo.

**On-screen memory point:** 21-day booking guidance; 30-day advance-application planning in the DoPT-staff circular.

**D1-14 — Rules 14, 15; FAQ 35**

Source: FAQ.

**Explanation:** Return journey complete hone ke baad, advance liya tha toh one month mein claim; advance nahi tha toh three months mein claim.

**Example:** do colleagues same day laute, lekin ek ne advance liya tha. dono ki normal claim limit same nahi hai.

**On-screen memory point:** After return: advance drawn = 1 month; no advance = 3 months.

**D1-15 — OM 21.12.2023; FAQ 35, 71**

Source: FAQ.

**Explanation:** Ministry, Financial Advisor concurrence se limited delayed claims admit kar sakti hai. No advance mein six months tak; advance mein three months tak, full advance plus penal interest one lump sum refund ki condition hai.

**Example:** Late claim ko automatic extension mat samjho. Authority, refund aur interest conditions pehle check karo.

**On-screen memory point:** Limited delegated admission: no advance up to 6 months; advance up to 3 months with conditions.

**Caution:** Calendar months are not automatically 30/90/180 days. Relaxation is not an automatic extension.

**Caution:** GID numbering is source-specific. These are Rule 7 cross-references, not invented Rule 1 sub-clauses.

## Dates-versus-deadlines traps

- The 2026–2029 regular block is not the special air scheme validity period. Fresh-recruit annual occasions are different again. See Rules 8–10 and FAQ 13–18, 64–69. [2](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)
- Rule 9 counts the **outward commencement** date, including carried-forward LTC. Do not invent a universal requirement to finish the return journey by 31 December 2026. Other time-limited schemes and leave conditions still require their own checks. [1](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)
- The one/three/six-month claim limits are **calendar-month wording**, not automatic 30/90/180-day replacements. Normal deadlines and discretionary delegated delayed admission must be taught separately. Advance cases require the applicable full-refund and penal-interest conditions; FA concurrence is not optional. FAQ 35 and 71. [2](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)

## Full-series GID teaching contract

For each topic batch: identify the rule, source-specific GID number and original authority/date; break out every operative clause, proviso, note and illustration; compare later primary orders; then teach **reference → simple city-Hinglish explanation → immediate practical example → exception/trap**. Use Roman-script subtitles, English legal terms/numbers/dates, and the already-selected voices.

The register is a **coverage checklist, not a current-law certificate**. `covered-in-updated-batch-1` applies to GIDs 7(6) and 7(9); `pending-topic-batch` means not yet taught in full; `expired-one-time-window` is retained only for audit. Mixed/modified annotations require clause-level reconciliation before inclusion.
