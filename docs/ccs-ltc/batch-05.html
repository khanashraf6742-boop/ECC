Scope, corrections and evidence limits

Scope: master chapters 16–18; Rules 8(a), 8(b) and proviso, 8(c), 9, 10 and proviso, 11; FAQ 13–20 and 64–70; every row and explanation in Tables 1–5. FAQ 6–7 and Rule 6 recalled only for destination/family context.

Corrections to master: normal HQ-different entitlement is two Home Town LTCs OR one Home Town plus one All India, not an extra third LTC or a compulsory All-India trip. Table 2 illustrates the mixed option, not an exhaustive menu.

Rule 10 proviso: where Home Town entitled, the SECOND two-year Home Town entitlement must itself be unused/carried into grace to permit old All-India carry. It is NOT a requirement to have completed a carried Home Town journey before the old four-year block ended. If the second Home Town has already been used, old All India cannot replace it in grace.

Automatic extension is the ordinary one-year carry window, not an exemption from normal sanction, leave, destination and documentary requirements. Block accounting follows commencement of the outward journey, not booking/bill or return date. Ordinary return/claim conditions remain; no indefinite journey right is implied.

Fresh recruits use eight calendar-year occasions after the one-year service gate. FAQ shorthand “first eight years” must be read with its own tables; do not cut Table 5 off at the eighth joining anniversary. Initial appointment and continuous-service eligibility are related but distinct clocks; technical resignation does not reset the initial-service clock.

Table 4 explanation (ii) expressly permits Home Town OR All India in 2024–25 if the eighth-year All-India occasion was forgone/not availed. This is a transition choice, NOT carry-forward of the expired annual occasion. Table 4 source typo “2024–45” is transparently normalized to 2024–25.

FAQ 66 normal-scheme election excludes fresh-recruit LTCs in THAT four-year block. Do not invent a permanent career-long ban, an automatic reset or unrestricted switching; record and check the applicable election.

B5-01 — Home Town: once per two calendar years

Rule 8(a): Home Town frequency

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Home Town LTC is available once in a block of two calendar years, subject to eligibility; it is not once every calendar year under the normal scheme.

Simple city Hinglish: Normal Home Town: two calendar years ke block mein one occasion.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi ne 2026 mein li; same 2026–27 block mein repeat nahi.

Watch out: Calendar blocks, not two years counted from the last trip.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-02 — Distance is not a minimum-kilometre test

Rule 8(a), read with FAQ 19/70: distance

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Home Town LTC is irrespective of distance, but headquarters and accepted Home Town must be different places. A short distance is not itself a bar.

Simple city Hinglish: Distance chhota ho sakta hai; HQ aur Home Town alag places hon.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha ka Home Town nearby hai; distance alone se reject nahi.

Watch out: Same HQ and Home Town is not a zero-kilometre reimbursable journey.

Evidence: H; F25

B5-03 — All India: once per four calendar years

Rule 8(b): Any Place in India

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Any Place in India LTC is available once in a four-calendar-year block, irrespective of distance, subject to the Home Town adjustment proviso where applicable.

Simple city Hinglish: All India: four calendar years mein one occasion, distance chahe jitna ho; India ke andar.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi 2026 mein Goa gaya; 2028 ka second All India nahi.

Watch out: No automatic annual All-India right; fare/class conditions still apply.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-04 — All India replaces one Home Town occasion

Rule 8(b), proviso: in lieu and adjustment

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Where Home Town LTC is available, All India is in lieu of and adjusted against the Home Town LTC available at commencement of the outward journey.

Simple city Hinglish: All India extra third occasion nahi; one Home Town ko replace karta hai.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha: 2026–27 All India li; usi slot ki extra Home Town nahi.

Watch out: Do not add two Home Town plus a third All India.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-05 — Two Home Town is also a valid normal choice

Rule 8(a)/(b); 3 September 2025 consolidation introduction

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): For normal HQ-different employees, two Home Town LTCs (one per two-year sub-block), OR one Home Town and one All India in the four-year block, are available. All India is not compulsory.

Simple city Hinglish: Two Home Town bhi allowed; All India lena compulsory nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi: 2026–27 aur 2028–29, dono mein Home Town choose karta hai.

Watch out: FAQ Table 2 mixed options do not prohibit two Home Town LTCs.

Evidence: H; D25

B5-06 — Do not borrow a future Home Town slot

Rule 8(b), proviso: available at outward commencement

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): The relevant Home Town entitlement must be available when the All-India outward journey commences; exhausting the current entitlement does not permit borrowing the next sub-block in advance.

Simple city Hinglish: Outward date par Home Town slot available ho; future slot advance nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha ne 2026–27 Home Town use ki; 2027 mein usi slot par All India nahi.

Watch out: A new calendar year is not necessarily a new two-year sub-block.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-07 — Self-only annual option: qualifying situation

Rule 8(c): family living away at Home Town

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): An employee whose family is living away at the Home Town may elect self-only Home Town LTC every year in place of all other concessions under the scheme.

Simple city Hinglish: Family Home Town par away rehti hai? Self-only annual option dekho.

Immediate hypothetical example: Delhi-posted Ravi ki family Jaipur mein; khud yearly visit option leta hai.

Watch out: This is an election with a full trade-off, not an added entitlement.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-08 — Annual self visits replace family and All India

Rule 8(c): in lieu of ALL concessions

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): The self-only annual Home Town election substitutes for all other employee/family LTC concessions, including Any Place in India; family trips cannot be stacked on it.

Simple city Hinglish: Self annual choose kiya, toh family LTC aur All India saath nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha annual self Home Town leti hai; extra family Goa claim nahi.

Simple city Hinglish: Ye normal annual option hai, posted-staff package nahi; blanket annual grace nahi.

B5-10 — Family need not travel together

Rule 9: family groups and different times

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Employee and eligible family may travel in different groups at different times within the applicable block; the separation does not multiply their individual entitlement.

Simple city Hinglish: Employee aur family alag groups, alag time travel kar sakte hain.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi March mein, wife June mein same available occasion use karti hai.

Watch out: Different groups are not extra annual occasions for the same person.

B5-11 — Outward date controls the charged block

Rule 9: commencement of outward journey

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): The block is determined by commencement of the outward journey, even if the return is in the next block; booking, billing and return dates do not choose the block.

Simple city Hinglish: Block outward journey se count hota hai, return ya booking se nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha 30 December 2025 nikli, January return; charge old block.

Watch out: A later return does not automatically shift the trip to the new block.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-12 — Each group has its own outward date

Rule 9: separate outward dates, read with Rule 10

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Separate groups require their own outward dates and entitlement accounting. A January group may use an eligible old-block carry or current block, depending on valid sanction and ledger; not automatically current only.

Simple city Hinglish: Har group ki outward date dekho; January mein eligible old carry bhi possible.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi December gaya; family January, office old carry ya current charge check kare.

Watch out: The master example must not erase the Rule 10 grace route.

Evidence: H; G25

B5-13 — Grace also follows outward commencement

Rule 9, final sentence; Rule 10 grace

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): The outward-commencement principle also applies to Rule 10 carry-over. Start the eligible outward journey within grace; return crossing year-end alone does not change its block.

Simple city Hinglish: Grace deadline outward start ki hai; return next year ho sakta hai.

B5-14 — Family can make different permitted choices

Rule 9; FAQ 7: mixed family Home Town / All India

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Eligible family members may avail Home Town and Any Place in India in the same two-year block, within each traveller’s available adjusted entitlement. Separation does not replenish quota.

Simple city Hinglish: Same sub-block: kisi ka Home Town, kisi ka All India allowed, quota ke andar.

B5-18 — All India carry needs unused second Home Town

Rule 10, proviso: where Home Town entitled

B5-38 — Do not cut off at eighth joining anniversary

FAQ 64–65; Tables 3–5: two different clocks

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): Read “first eight years” with the eight eligible calendar occasions after the service gate. Table 5 explicitly puts occasion eight in 2033 for joining 1 January 2025; do not truncate that table at 2032.

B5-39 — Normal-scheme election: no same-block hybrid

FAQ 66: opting for the normal LTC scheme

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): A fresh recruit may choose the normal LTC scheme, but then cannot avail fresh-recruit LTCs in that four-year block. This is not stated as an automatic permanent career-long forfeiture; do not invent unrestricted switching.

Simple city Hinglish: Normal scheme choose ki, toh us four-year block mein fresh pattern nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Neha normal option leti hai; same block mein extra fresh annual slot nahi.

Watch out: Record the choice and verify later applicable election; do not promise a reset.

Evidence: F25

B5-40 — Unused annual slot lapses, not postponed

FAQ 67: no fresh-recruit carry-over

Rule / current restatement (not verbatim): A fresh-recruit annual occasion not used in its calendar year lapses; Rule 10 ordinary block carry is unavailable. Skipping a Home Town slot does not bring the fourth All India year forward.

Simple city Hinglish: Fresh annual slot unused? Year-end lapse; next year extra slot nahi.

Immediate hypothetical example: Ravi 2026 Home Town skip karta hai; 2027 mein two slots nahi milte.

Watch out: No one-year fresh-recruit grace and no pushing the whole pattern forward.

Evidence: F25

B5-45 — First slot can be a single eligibility day
