## Scope, currentness and corrections

Scope: master chapters 4-8; every Rule 4 limb, the three child-number exceptions, operative Rule 4 GIDs, family FAQs 1-12 and 74-75. Reference first, then city-Hinglish explanation and an immediate hypothetical example. All examples are invented teaching cases, not actual departmental sanctions.

Evidence review: 27 October 2026. The current family position is checked against the official July 2025 FAQ and September 2025 consolidation, with the original numbered GIDs cross-mapped. A publication/upload folder is not an amendment effective date. No verified later family amendment was identified in this review; this is not a guarantee that no later instrument exists.

Critical correction to the uploaded master: parental/step-parent no-co-residence and minimum-pension-linked dependency were already in the 23 September 2008 instruction, not newly created in 2025. Current official sources confirm the substance. The standalone primary 2008 instrument was not retrieved; a secondary full-text reproduction corroborates the origin. Its claimed 1 September 2008 effective date is not certified in this edition.

Second master correction: multiple births qualify for the numerical exception when the SECOND childbirth produces the excess; not every later set of twins. The normal child count is family-wide, not two per wife.

Third master correction: the separate-hometown/independent-claims option for government spouses residing together dates to the 8 May 1990 decision, from block 1990-91 onwards. The old mandatory common-hometown/one-claim paragraph is the pre-decision position, not the operative conclusion.

Fourth master correction: a declared valid All India option can have hometown as its destination. Destination alone does not mechanically decide HT versus All India. The family residence-to-HQ-and-back 2017 instruction cannot be reduced to a zero HQ-to-HQ ceiling, nor extended to unlimited fares for every other destination.

Numbering crosswalk: historical Rule 4 Explanation 4 (sisters' children) is Explanation 3 in the 2025 consolidation. Historical Explanation 3 (away-family fare cap) is reconciled with Notes 5(i)-(ii), the 2017 HQ-visit instruction and FAQs 74-75. Historical Explanation 5's Rs.1500 ceiling is obsolete; no old DR/stipend exclusion is silently carried into current FAQ 2.

Calendar warning: the 2025 sources call 2022-25 current because that was their issue-year context. On 27 October 2026 the regular block is 2026-29. The 2022-25 carryover deadline is conditional: for a person eligible for HT LTC, carrying forward All India requires carryover of HT from the second two-year sub-block. Full Rule 8/9/10 mechanics and fresh-recruit personal blocks follow in the planned block batch.

The assumed 50% DR in B2-17 is arithmetic ONLY, not today's notified DR. Always obtain the applicable minimum family pension and DR for the relevant date. A new pay commission's announcement does not by itself change the minimum used here.

Training/study-leave cards compare family eligibility and fare anchors. Detailed domestic-training clauses, missions abroad, special-posting concessions, fresh recruits, general fare/ATA rules, claims/advance deadlines, encashment and spouse administration remain in their topic batches. Special Dispensation air segments require a valid applicable permission; this batch does not authenticate the reported September 2026 scheme extension.

Coverage is batchwise, not a claim that all non-obsolete GIDs in the full series are already complete. GID (2) teaches the operative printed clauses (i), (ii), (vi); starred/deleted historical matter is not invented. GID (5) teaches the opening principle and all SIX illustrations. The register distinguishes actual coverage, partial comparisons and remaining work.

The governing OM/order prevails if a consolidation is imprecise. This is educational material; the sanctioning office must verify journey facts, the applicable order, entitled class/route, documentation and available occasion.

## B2-01 — India includes Indian islands

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(a); Rule 4 GID (3)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Any place within Indian territory, mainland or overseas territory, is a place in India. Local restrictions on visits remain the traveller's responsibility; foreign territory is not covered by this definition.

**Simple city Hinglish:** India ki territory mein mainland aur Indian islands dono aate hain; restricted area ki permission khud leni hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Andaman India hai; foreign island sirf paas hone se India nahi banta.

**Watch out:** Indian territory, not every overseas destination. Local permits still matter.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-02 — Who is the Controlling Officer?

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(b)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Controlling Officer means an officer declared as such under Supplementary Rule 191. A line manager is not automatically the designated officer.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Controlling Officer wahi hai jo Supplementary Rule one ninety one ke under declared hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Team leader hona enough nahi; designated officer ka record dekho.

**Watch out:** Designation under SR 191 matters.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-03 — Disciplinary Authority

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(c)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The meaning is imported from CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 2(g). Rule 4(c) does not create a new disciplinary authority.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Disciplinary Authority ka meaning CCS CCA Rules nineteen sixty five, Rule two, clause G se aata hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** LTC fraud case mein sirf bill pass karne wala officer automatically disciplinary authority nahi.

**Watch out:** Definition is cross-referenced; detailed fraud procedure belongs to Rule 16.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-04 — Declared AND accepted hometown

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(e)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Hometown is the town, village or other place declared by the employee and accepted by the Controlling Officer. Rule 5 change/acceptance criteria are reserved for Batch 3.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Town, village ya place ko employee declare kare aur Controlling Officer accept kare, tab hometown hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Ravi ne Jaipur likha, par acceptance pending hai; declaration akela enough nahi.

**Watch out:** Rule 5 change-of-hometown procedure follows in Batch 3.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-05 — Shortest direct route

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(f)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Shortest direct route has the meaning in Supplementary Rule 30 and orders issued under it from time to time; personal preference does not redefine the reimbursable route.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Shortest direct route ka meaning Supplementary Rule thirty aur uske orders se aata hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Goa ghoomne ka detour khud choose kiya, toh poora detour automatically reimbursable nahi.

**Watch out:** Definition is not a complete Rule 13 fare calculation.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-06 — Spouse: no income or co-residence test

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(i); Rule 4 Note 2; FAQ 3;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Husband or wife is family without a dependency-income test and need not reside with the employee. This does not give two employee spouses duplicate independent claims.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Spouse par income test nahi; saath rehna bhi zaroori nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Wife private job mein achha earn karti hai aur Pune rehti hai; spouse eligibility phir bhi hai.

**Watch out:** Income exemption applies to spouse, not all relatives.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-07 — Two eldest surviving unmarried children

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(i); FAQ 1;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The normal category is the two eldest surviving unmarried children, including stepchildren and legally adopted children, wholly dependent, whether co-resident or not. Explanation 1 supplies three exceptions to the numerical restriction.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Normally two eldest surviving unmarried dependent children; step aur legally adopted children included, residence test nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Three unmarried bachche hain; exception nahi toh youngest ko choose karke eldest ko replace mat karo.

**Watch out:** Not two children per wife, per trip or per employee spouse.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-08 — Age 25 is not a cut-off

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(i); FAQ 4;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** An unmarried wholly dependent son or daughter above 25 is not disqualified by age alone; other family and numerical conditions remain.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Unmarried dependent child ke liye twenty five years ka upper age cut off nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Twenty eight year old unmarried dependent daughter eldest two mein hai; age se reject nahi hogi.

**Watch out:** Adult child is different from the expressly minor-brother category.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-09 — Pre-existing more-than-two children

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d), Explanation 1(i)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The two-surviving-children restriction does not apply to employees who already had more than two children before 20.10.1997; other eligibility tests continue.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Twenty October nineteen ninety seven se pehle already more than two children the, toh numerical exception hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Nineteen ninety six mein three children the; sirf two child cap se third reject nahi hoga.

**Watch out:** 20.10.1997 is the cut-off, not 20.10.1998.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-10 — One-year birth window

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d), Explanation 1(ii)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Children born within one year of the restriction coming into force are protected from its numerical restriction. This is historical grandfathering, not a rolling one-year concession today.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Restriction start hone ke one year ke andar born children ko numerical protection hai; fresh yearly window nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Third child March nineteen ninety eight mein born hua; historical window check hoga.

**Watch out:** Use the actual birth date and historical window; do not invent a current grace year.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-11 — Second childbirth resulting in multiple births

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d), Explanation 1(iii)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The exception applies where the number exceeds two because the second childbirth results in multiple births. It is not an unrestricted exception for twins at any childbirth.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Second childbirth mein twins ya triplets se total two se badha, toh exception hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** First childbirth one baby, second childbirth twins: three children ka numerical exception; third childbirth twins automatically nahi.

**Watch out:** Second childbirth is an essential qualifier.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-12 — One wife: narrow express exception

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d), Explanation 2

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Normally not more than one wife. The express exception requires two legally wedded wives and specific Government permission for the second marriage. General FAQ shorthand does not erase this express exception.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Normally one wife; two legally wedded wives aur second marriage ki specific Government permission ho, tab exception.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Sirf second marriage certificate enough nahi; specific Government permission bhi chahiye.

**Watch out:** No claim of automatic permission or automatic lawful second marriage.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-13 — Daughters: all status and residence tests

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(ii); FAQ 1;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Divorced, abandoned, separated-from-husband or widowed daughters must both reside with and be wholly dependent on the employee. An ordinarily married daughter is not included by this limb. No separate numerical/age ceiling is stated in this limb.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Divorced, abandoned, separated ya widowed daughter ko dependent aur saath rehna dono chahiye.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Dependent divorced daughter alag flat mein rehti hai, toh residence test fail; maa ke liye rule alag hai.

**Watch out:** Do not transfer the parents' no-residence test to daughters.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-14 — Children of daughters or sisters excluded

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d), Explanation 3 (2025) / Explanation 4 (historical); FAQ 1;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The historical Explanation 4 is current consolidated Explanation 3: children of divorced/abandoned/separated/widowed sisters are excluded. FAQ 1 also expressly excludes children of daughters in those categories. An independently qualifying legal adoption must be assessed as adoption, not as a grandchild entitlement.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Eligible sister ya daughter ke bachche automatically family nahi; current Explanation three, old four tha.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Widowed daughter eligible hai, par uska son sirf grandson hone se covered nahi.

**Watch out:** Relative's eligibility does not flow down to her children.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-15 — Parents and both kinds of step-parent

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(iii); FAQ 3;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Parents and/or step-parents, expressly stepmother and stepfather, must be wholly dependent; co-residence is not required. This is not a new 2025-only liberalisation: the 2008 family instruction already states the change.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Dependent parents, stepmother aur stepfather included; saath rehna zaroori nahi. Yeh sirf twenty twenty five ka naya change nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Dependent mother Kanpur mein rehti hai, employee Delhi mein; eligibility residence se fail nahi.

**Watch out:** 2008 instruction predates the 2025 restatement; old parental residence wording is not current.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [D08](https://www.referencer.in/LTC_Rules/31011_4_2008_Estt.(A).aspx)


## B2-16 — Dependency: income from all sources

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 Note 1; FAQ 2; OM 31011/4/2008-Estt.(A), 23.09.2008

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Income from all sources, including pension and temporary increase in pension, must not exceed the prescribed minimum family pension plus DR thereon. FAQ 2 quotes Rs.9000 per month as the Seventh CPC minimum; apply the legally applicable minimum and DR for the relevant date. Spouse is exempt.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Income all sources jodo, pension aur temporary increase bhi. Limit minimum family pension plus DR; FAQ base nine thousand hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Parent ki pension, rent aur interest jodkar applicable limit se compare karo.

**Watch out:** Do not teach old Rs.1500/Rs.3500 or carry old exclusions over without current authority.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-17 — Calculation: equality still qualifies

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 Note 1; FAQ 2; equality at the ceiling

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Does not exceed includes equality. Worked arithmetic uses assumed 50% DR, not the actual current DR. Rs.9000 + Rs.4500 = Rs.13500 per month; income Rs.13500 meets the income limb; Rs.13501 exceeds it.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Assumed DR fifty percent, current rate nahi: nine thousand plus forty five hundred equals thirteen thousand five hundred.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Income thirteen thousand five hundred par pass; one rupee extra par income test fail.

**Watch out:** 50% is deliberately hypothetical. Other relationship conditions still apply.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-18 — Brothers must be unmarried AND minor

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(iv); FAQ 1; minor brothers

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** An unmarried minor brother must reside with and be wholly dependent on the employee; his parents must be dead or themselves wholly dependent on the employee. An adult unmarried brother is not included by this limb.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Brother unmarried minor ho, saath rahe, dependent ho; parents dead ya khud employee par dependent hon.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Twenty two year old unmarried dependent brother ko minor brother category se LTC nahi.

**Watch out:** Every listed condition is cumulative, not optional.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-19 — Sisters: no minor-only condition

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d)(iv); FAQ 1; sisters

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Unmarried, divorced, abandoned, separated-from-husband or widowed sisters must reside with and be wholly dependent on the employee, and their parents must be dead or themselves wholly dependent on the employee. The word minor qualifies brothers, not sisters.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Sister unmarried, divorced, abandoned, separated ya widowed ho; saath aur dependent ho; parents dead ya dependent. Minor test nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Thirty year old unmarried sister saari conditions meet kare toh age se reject nahi.

**Watch out:** Parents-status condition applies to sisters too.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-20 — In-laws, grandparents and unlisted relatives

**Exact reference:** Rule 4(d); FAQ 5; FAQ 1; excluded relations

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Parents-in-law, grandparents and relatives not included in Rule 4(d) do not qualify merely because they are dependent or co-resident. Each government employee spouse may instead claim for their own qualifying parents under the independent-option rules.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Parents in law, grandparents aur unlisted relations sirf dependent hone se family nahi bante.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Mother in law saath rehti aur dependent hai; employee ki own mother wali category use nahi hogi.

**Watch out:** Spouse's own independent employee entitlement is a different question.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-21 — New husband after hometown marriage

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 GID (1); Rule 4 Note 3; OM 48/2/65-Ests.(A), 02.04.1965

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** If a female government servant travels to her hometown on LTC and marries there, LTC may also be allowed for her husband's journey from that hometown to her headquarters.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Female employee hometown LTC par gayi, wahin shaadi hui; new husband ka hometown se HQ leg allow ho sakta hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Neha Jaipur mein shaadi karti hai; husband Jaipur se uske Delhi HQ aata hai.

**Watch out:** Specific marriage-linked journey, not a new benefit for every relative.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-22 — Who may sanction the LTC advance?

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 GID (2)(i); OM 31011/1/77-Ests.(A), 01.10.1977

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The Head of Office may sanction the LTC advance instead of the Controlling Officer. This is an authority provision, not a waiver of current advance limits, application checks or settlement requirements.

**Simple city Hinglish:** LTC advance Head of Office sanction kar sakta hai; sirf Controlling Officer tak authority limited nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Employee advance file Head of Office ke paas bhejta hai; amount aur settlement checks phir bhi lagenge.

**Watch out:** Detailed Rule 15 advance limits and deadlines remain for the advance batch.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-23 — Shortest route disrupted

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 GID (2)(ii); OM 31011/1/77-Ests.(A), 01.10.1977

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** If the required shortest route is disrupted by accident or other cause, Controlling Authority may exercise the power to permit reimbursement by the actual route travelled, replacing the old central DPAR/Finance referral. It is not automatic reimbursement for a chosen detour.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Shortest route accident ya doosre cause se disrupted ho, Controlling Authority actual route reimbursement approve kar sakti hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Flood se route band hai; alternate route ka proof aur approval lo, personal detour mat jodo.

**Watch out:** Actual disruption plus competent approval, not unrestricted route choice.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-24 — LPR: complete the return before leave expires

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 GID (2)(vi); Rule 4 Note 4; OM 31011/1/77-Ests.(A), 01.10.1977

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Both directions of admissible hometown LTC in a two-year block or any-place-in-India LTC once in four years may be allowed during leave preparatory to retirement, provided the return journey is completed before that leave expires. These are normal occasions, not additional retirement occasions.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Leave preparatory to retirement mein eligible LTC dono ways hai; return leave expire hone se pehle complete karo.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** LPR thirty September end hoti hai; twenty nine ko return okay, two October return condition fail.

**Watch out:** Not merely an outward-journey-start test; no extra HT plus AI occasions.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-25 — Family staying away: ordinary ceiling

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 GID (4); historical Explanation 3; Rule 4 Note 5(i); OM 31011/14/86-Est.(A), 08.05.1987; FAQ 8;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Spouse and dependent children living away may start from their residence to hometown in the applicable two-year block or an any-place destination in the four-year entitlement. Ordinary reimbursement is limited to actual eligible travel or the HQ-to-destination eligible fare, whichever is less. Read the 2017 HQ-visit exception separately.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Away spouse aur dependent children residence se travel kar sakte hain; ordinary case mein actual ya HQ destination ceiling, jo kam.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Actual eligible fare four thousand, HQ ceiling six thousand: reimbursement four thousand, extra two thousand nahi.

**Watch out:** 2017 HQ-and-back instruction is a distinct route-specific modification.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-26 — Family to HQ and back: distinct 2017 instruction

**Exact reference:** Rule 4 Note 5(ii); OM 31011/5/2015-Estt.(A-IV), 31.10.2017; FAQ 75;

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Family members travelling on LTC from any place in India to the employee's headquarters/place of posting and back are allowed full reimbursement as per employee entitlement. FAQ 75 classifies the away-family any-place option, including HQ, as All India LTC. An available valid LTC occasion and ordinary eligibility/booking rules are still needed.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Family residence se HQ aur back aaye toh full entitled reimbursement; HQ to HQ zero ceiling mat lagao. Valid All India occasion chahiye.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Pune family Mumbai HQ aati aur Pune lautti hai; valid All India LTC par entitled fare check hoga.

**Watch out:** Not unlimited actual fare, free private commuting or an additional LTC occasion.

**Evidence:** [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-27 — At HQ: HT and All India counting

**Exact reference:** FAQ 74; family residing at headquarters

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A hometown-occasion visit to hometown counts as HT LTC. On an All India occasion the chosen destination may be any place, including hometown, and counts as All India LTC. Destination alone does not override the valid option recorded.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Family HQ par ho: hometown option par hometown trip HT; All India option mein hometown bhi choose kar sakte ho.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Delhi se Patna, accepted hometown hai; valid All India option declared ho toh counting All India hogi.

**Watch out:** Do not automatically label every visit to hometown as HT LTC.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-28 — Away family: count first, calculate second

**Exact reference:** FAQ 75; family residing away from headquarters

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Away-family HT travel has the lesser actual/HQ-to-hometown ceiling. The All India option covers any place, including HQ or hometown. Ordinary non-HQ any-place claims still have normal entitled-fare limits; the FAQ is not an unrestricted removal of all HQ-based ceilings.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Away family ki HT claim par lesser ceiling; All India destination HQ ya hometown bhi ho sakta hai, normal fare limits ke saath.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Kanpur family Patna HT jaaye: eligible actual three thousand versus HQ ceiling five thousand, claim three thousand.

**Watch out:** 2017 HQ-specific relief does not make all other away-family trips unlimited.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-29 — Separate times, groups and places

**Exact reference:** FAQ 6; Rule 9

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Employee and family may travel separately or in different groups, at different times and to different destinations within the relevant two/four-year block, subject to available entitlement. This does not create a new block or extra occasion for each departure.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Different groups, times aur destinations allowed; relevant block aur available occasion phir bhi check karo.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Employee May mein Goa, eligible spouse June mein Kochi jaaye; separate travel se extra block nahi banta.

**Watch out:** Separate claims/travel do not equal unlimited occasions.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-30 — Mixing HT and All India within the sub-block

**Exact reference:** FAQ 7; Rule 9

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Some family members may use hometown LTC while others use any-place-in-India LTC in the same two-year block. Track each person's valid occasion and the overall scheme, rather than forcing identical family destinations.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Same two year sub block mein kuch members HT aur kuch All India use kar sakte hain.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Eligible spouse HT Jaipur, employee All India Goa choose kare; individual occasion record clear rakho.

**Watch out:** Availability must be checked; not a second All India trip for one person.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-31 — Private employer LTA is not a blanket bar

**Exact reference:** FAQ 9; private-sector spouse

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A private-sector spouse may avail LTA/travel reimbursement from their own employer; there is no blanket LTC prohibition on that basis. This answer does not create permission to submit false declarations or treat the same expense as two genuine out-of-pocket expenses.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Private job wala spouse apne employer ka LTA le sakta hai; blanket ban nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Wife employer se LTA leti hai; husband ki Government LTC sirf isi wajah se automatically barred nahi.

**Watch out:** Follow truthful declarations and employer/accounting conditions; no invented universal NOC bar.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-32 — Both government spouses: independent choice

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (18); OM 31011/8/89-Est.(A), 08.05.1990

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Even when residing together, two government employee spouses may opt for separate hometowns and claim under normal rules for their respective qualifying families. The mandatory common-hometown/only-one-claim description is the superseded pre-decision position, not a change first made in 2025. Effective for journeys against blocks 1990-91 onwards.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Dono Government spouses saath rehkar bhi separate hometowns aur respective family claims choose kar sakte hain; option nineteen ninety se hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Husband apne parents, wife apne parents ka eligible claim kare; forced common hometown rule mat lagao.

**Watch out:** Full spouse/encashment administration is expanded in the later spouse batch.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-33 — No double self claim; children under one parent

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (18); spouse self-claim and children conditions

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A spouse availing LTC as the other's family member is not entitled to an independent self-claim for that occasion. Children may receive the benefit in one particular block as members of one parent's family only. Other normal conditions continue.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Spouse doosre ki family member bankar LTC le toh independent self claim nahi; children particular block mein one parent se only.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Child ka same block benefit husband aur wife dono offices se claim mat karo.

**Watch out:** One-unit income exemption does not cancel the independent-choice option or anti-duplication controls.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-34 — Suspended employee versus eligible family

**Exact reference:** FAQ 12; Rule 7 GID (15)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The suspended employee cannot avail LTC personally, but eligible family members remain entitled because the employee continues in service. Suspension does not dispense with family eligibility, available blocks or claim requirements.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Suspended employee khud LTC nahi le sakta; eligible family le sakti hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Suspended Ravi ki eligible wife LTC jaaye; Ravi ka personal ticket family claim mein mat jodo.

**Watch out:** Family eligibility is not personal permission for the suspended employee.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf); [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-35 — Assess outward and return legs separately

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), opening principle; OM 43/10/58-Ests.(A), 11.12.1958

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Admissibility of family travel is assessed independently against facts at the forward and return journeys. The following six illustrations are specific single-leg cases; they do not dispense with current fare rules.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Outward aur return journey ki facts separately check karo; family status beech mein badal sakta hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Dependent child jaate waqt eligible tha, return se pehle job mil gayi; dono legs ka answer same mat assume karo.

**Watch out:** Six illustrations follow; child-age fares must be read with 2016/2018 clarifications.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-36 — Outward only: employment, marriage or staying for studies

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), I(i)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Outward-only illustration: a dependent son/daughter gets employment or marries after reaching hometown, or remains there for studies. Employment must be assessed with actual dependency facts; the illustration is not a rule that every token receipt destroys dependency.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Dependent son ya daughter hometown ke baad job ya marriage kare, ya studies ke liye wahi rahe: outward only case check karo.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Daughter eligible hokar gayi, hometown mein shaadi karke wahi rahi; uska return fare claim nahi.

**Watch out:** Assessment uses actual leg facts, not a fictional return journey.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-37 — Outward only: written surrender of later return

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), I(ii)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Where family travels to hometown with no intention to complete return travel, outward-only reimbursement is allowed provided the employee foregoes in writing the concession for any return journey later performed by those members.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Family return intend nahi karti, employee later return concession written mein forego kare toh outward only claim.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Parents hometown settle ho gaye; employee written return surrender deta hai, baad mein wahi return claim nahi.

**Watch out:** Written relinquishment is an express condition.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf)


## B2-38 — Return only: new wife or wife long at hometown

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), II(i); Rule 4 Note 3

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Return-only illustration: a newly married wife comes from hometown to HQ, or a wife living long at hometown who did not avail LTC for the outward journey comes to HQ. The separate 1965 instruction covers the new husband of a female employee marrying on hometown LTC.

**Simple city Hinglish:** New wife hometown se HQ aaye, ya wife long time hometown par thi aur outward LTC nahi li: return only case.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Wife hometown par pehle se rehti thi; usne outward LTC nahi li, ab HQ aati hai.

**Watch out:** Do not invent an outward claim for travel not undertaken or not availed.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-39 — Return only: child studying or living with grandparents

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), II(ii)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A dependent son/daughter returns with parents or comes alone from hometown where studying or living with grandparents. The child must satisfy the current family conditions; the grandparents do not thereby become eligible family.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Dependent child hometown mein studies ya grandparents ke saath tha; parents ke saath ya alone return case allowed.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Eligible son grandparents ke ghar padh raha tha, ab HQ aata hai; grandparents ka ticket include nahi.

**Watch out:** Living with grandparents does not make grandparents eligible.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-40 — Birthday between journeys: 5 or 12

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), II(iii)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The historical illustration permits the return-leg fare where the child was previously below 5/12 and completes 5/12 by return. Apply current carrier fare and 2016/2018 LTC reimbursement rules to each leg, not an invented automatic full adult fare.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Child return tak five ya twelve complete kare toh us leg ki age aur current fare rule check hoga.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Outward par four years, return se pehle five complete; eligible return rail fare assess karo.

**Watch out:** This historic age illustration does not override FAQ 10/11.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-41 — Legal adoption while at hometown

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (5), II(iv)

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A child legally adopted while the employee is staying at hometown is a return-only illustration; legal adoption, dependency and the applicable child-number restriction/exceptions must be established.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Hometown stay mein child legally adopt kiya toh return leg consider hoga; normal child conditions bhi lagenge.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Legal adoption Jaipur mein hui; eligible child employee ke Delhi HQ aata hai, imaginary outward fare nahi.

**Watch out:** Legal adoption, not informal care alone.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-42 — Under 5: separate train seat is not reimbursed

**Exact reference:** FAQ 10; OM 31011/3/2016-Estt.(A-IV), 16.05.2018

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** A child under 5 who travels by train with an optional separately purchased seat/berth is not entitled to LTC reimbursement of that seat/berth fare, even if money was actually paid.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Under five child ki separate train seat ya berth ka paid fare LTC mein reimburse nahi.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Four year old ke liye extra berth kharidi; payment proof alone reimbursement nahi banata.

**Watch out:** Actual purchase is not the same as admissibility.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-43 — Under 5: non-air-entitled employee

**Exact reference:** FAQ 11; OM 31011/3/2016-Estt.(A-IV), 16.05.2018; air restriction

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** No airfare reimbursement for an under-5 child travelling by air with a non-air-entitled employee under the ordinary capped-fare route. The exception applies only to segments where air travel is permitted to such employees/families under the Special Dispensation Scheme.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Non air entitled employee ke under five child ka airfare ordinary route par nahi; allowed Special Dispensation air segments exception hain.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Airline full fare leti hai, phir bhi ordinary case mein no; scheme segment ki valid permission alag check karo.

**Watch out:** No blanket exemption just because the destination is in a special region; current scheme validity is separate.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-44 — Ages 5 to under 12: actual rail fare

**Exact reference:** FAQ 11; OM 31011/3/2016-Estt.(A.IV), 29.04.2016; rail fare

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** The actual rail fare paid for children aged 5 and under 12 is reimbursable for LTC within the employee's otherwise admissible entitlement. Do not mechanically cap it at an assumed half fare.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Five se under twelve child ka actual paid rail fare eligible entitlement ke andar reimburse hota hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Eight year old ki permitted rail berth ka actual fare paid hai; automatically half fare cut mat karo.

**Watch out:** Entitled class/route and documentary checks still apply.

**Evidence:** [F25](https://documents.doptcirculars.nic.in/D2/D02est/FAQs%20on%20LTC5hctT.pdf)


## B2-45 — Study leave: eligibility versus fare anchor

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (17)(a), (b)(i)-(ii); OM 31011/1/88-Ests.(A), 30.03.1988

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** Self may travel from the study-leave place to hometown/any place, capped at actual expenditure or normal HQ-to-destination entitlement, whichever less. Family staying with the employee at the study station uses that cap; family not staying there uses normal LTC terms. Family membership does not change due to study leave.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Study leave par self aur saath wali family ka fare actual ya normal HQ ceiling, jo kam; baaki family normal terms par.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Chennai HQ, Pune study, Goa destination: actual eight thousand, HQ ceiling six thousand; self aur saath family par six thousand ceiling.

**Watch out:** Full leave/training administration follows in Batch 4; no fare for a relative making no journey.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)


## B2-46 — Training abroad: Indian-HQ liability ceiling

**Exact reference:** Rule 7 GID (20)(iii); OM 43/10/65-Estt.(A), 07.08.1965; family comparison

**Rule / current restatement (not a verbatim quotation):** For the employee on overseas training, LTC liability is limited to the fare liability from the Indian HQ from which training began, or HQ declared under SR 59, to hometown and back. For family, the HQ from which the employee proceeded on training is the onward starting point. The overseas point does not create an automatic Government-funded international LTC leg. Detailed training conditions follow in Batch 4.

**Simple city Hinglish:** Overseas training se foreign flight LTC mein automatically funded nahi; Indian HQ ya SR fifty nine HQ ceiling relevant hai.

**Immediate hypothetical example:** Mumbai HQ, London training, husband Mumbai mein: London flight ko spouse ke Mumbai LTC claim mein mat jodo.

**Watch out:** This is a family/anchor comparison, not full coverage of every GID (20) overseas subclause.

**Evidence:** [H](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30f46c64b74a6c964c674853a89796c8e/uploads/2022/11/2022112351.pdf); [G25](https://www.csir.res.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/guidelines_on_ltc.pdf)
