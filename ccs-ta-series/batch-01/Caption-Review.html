Batch 01 / cue-by-cue listening

Human caption review

Not yet human-verified. These cues are automatic transliterations of the speech-synthesis input. A person must listen to every cue before calling the recording verbatim-verified. Check names, numbers, words and timing; forced alignment and speech recognition can be wrong. The English subtitles are a separate translation and are not being certified here.

1. Listen and compare

Use headphones, play each segment and compare it with the Roman-Hinglish text. Correct the words and timestamps if needed, then tick that cue only after you have listened. The original subtitle is burned into the video and cannot change in this player.

Each cue's Play button starts slightly before its proposed start and pauses just after its proposed end. Times are seconds from the beginning of the video. Boundary playback is approximate.

2. Record the review

Review status is stored in this browser when available. Export a backup before closing or switching devices. A tick is a reviewer's assertion, not automatic proof of accuracy.
