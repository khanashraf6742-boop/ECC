ch10-004

Very High-YieldKey definitions — Headquarters, Tour, Family (which dependents qualify)

ProvisoExplanation
Source L2138–2180
Exact-Source Fact Card

Under Supplementary Rules (SR) and DoPT/DoE guidelines, key definitions for Travelling Allowance (TA) purposes are strictly defined:

  • Headquarters (SR 8, SR 116): The station declared to be such for a Government servant by the competent authority (usually where their office is located). TA on tour/transfer is reckoned from this notified station. The signed 12.09.2018 DoE letter uses a beyond-8-km test from the normal place of duty for the stated local-journey TA, irrespective of journey origin; it does not settle every fare/vehicle claim at or below that distance.
  • Tour (SR 61): A journey performed on public duty away from headquarters under proper sanction/authorization by a competent authority. A tour begins when the employee leaves headquarters and ends when they return.
  • Family (SR 2(8)): For TA purposes, "family" comprises the following members who are residing with and wholly dependent on the Government servant:
    • Spouse: Wife or husband of the Government servant. Note that only one spouse is recognized for TA claims. If both husband and wife are Government servants and are transferred from the same old station to the same new station within 6 months, only one can claim TA for the family, and the other will be treated as a dependent family member.
    • Children: Legitimate children and step-children.
      • Sons: Up to the age of 25 years, or until they get married, or until they start earning, whichever is earliest. However, for sons with physical or mental disabilities, there is no age limit, provided they are wholly dependent.
      • Daughters: Until they get married or start earning, whichever is earlier, regardless of age.
      • Divorced/Abandoned/Widowed Daughters: Residing with and wholly dependent, regardless of age.
    • Parents: Dependent parents (including step-parents) residing with the Government servant.
    • Brothers: Dependent minor brothers (under 18 years) residing with the Government servant.
    • Sisters: Dependent unmarried, widowed, or divorced sisters residing with the Government servant, regardless of age.

Dependency Income Ceiling: A family member (other than the spouse) is deemed wholly dependent if their total monthly income from all sources (including pension, temporary increase in pension, and other earnings) does not exceed ₹9,000/- per month plus Dearness Relief (DR) thereon (as per latest 7th CPC standards). If the income exceeds this ceiling, they do not qualify as "family" for TA/LTC/Medical reimbursement.

Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Headquarters kya hai? Wo station jo aapka office officially aapka "base" notify karta hai. Signed 12.09.2018 letter ke mutabik qualifying local-journey threshold normal place of duty se 8 km se zyada hai, chahe aap residence se travel karo. Isse at/below-8-km fares ya vehicle rules decide nahi hote; full current SR 71/GIO abhi open hai.
  • Tour kya hai? Office ke proper sanction/authorization se public duty ke liye HQ se bahar jaana. Departure se return tak tour chalta hai. Bina sanction ke gaye to koi TA nahi milega — chahe kitna bhi zaroori kaam kyun na ho.
  • Family — kaun-kaun shamil hai? Ye bohot detailed definition hai TA/LTC ke liye:
    • Spouse (Husband/Wife): Hamesha dependent maana jaata hai — koi income limit nahi. Lekin sirf ek spouse recognize hota hai. Agar dono govt servants hain, to dono alag-alag claim kar sakte hain (separate rules).
    • Sons (Bete): 25 saal ki age tak ya shaadi tak ya earning tak — jo sabse pehle aaye. Lekin disabled sons ke liye koi age limit nahi — hamesha cover rahenge. Example: 30 saal ka disabled beta jo aapke saath rehta hai aur dependent hai — wo family mein count hoga.
    • Daughters (Betiyaan): Jab tak unmarried hain aur earning nahi — koi age limit nahi! 35 saal ki unmarried beti bhi family mein count hogi. Divorced/widowed/abandoned beti bhi cover hai.
    • Parents: Dependent parents (step-parents bhi) — income ₹9,000/month + DR se kam honi chahiye.
    • Brothers: Sirf minor (18 saal se kam) bhai — dependent aur saath rehne wale.
    • Sisters: Unmarried, widowed, ya divorced behenein — koi age limit nahi — dependent aur saath rehne wali.
  • Dependency Income Ceiling — ₹9,000 + DR: Spouse ko chhod ke, baaki sab family members ki monthly income ₹9,000 + Dearness Relief (DR) se kam honi chahiye. Agar kisi parent ki pension ₹10,000/month hai (DR mila ke), to wo "family" mein nahi aayenge — unka TA claim nahi hoga. Exam mein ye ₹9,000 figure bohot test hota hai — purana 6th CPC figure ₹3,500 tha, ab 7th CPC mein ₹9,000+DR hai.
Memory Hook

"Son-25-Cutoff: Sons leave the TA nest at 25, daughters only upon marriage or earning. Parents are family only if their pocket is under ₹9,000+DR."

Exam Angle / PYQ

Examiners frequently design questions around the dependency age and income limits. Watch out for the ₹9,000/- plus DR ceiling — questions often omit the "plus DR" or use outdated 6th CPC figures (₹3,500). Also, remember that a disabled son has no age limit, which is a common MCQ trap.

ch10-008

Very High-YieldThe Crucial LTC vs. TA/DA Entitlement Contrasts (High-Yield Exam Distinctions)

O.M.ProvisoExceptionExplanation
Source L2288–2345
Exact-Source Fact Card

Under DoPT and Department of Expenditure circulars (clarified via DoPT O.M. F. No. 31011/8/2017-Estt.A-IV dated 19th September 2017 and O.M. dated 04.02.2021), the entitlements for Leave Travel Concession (LTC) and Travelling Allowance (TA) on Tour/Transfer exhibit critical, heavily tested differences:

  • 1. Air Entitlement Difference for Pay Levels 6 to 8 (Classic Exam Trap):
    • On Tour/Training/Transfer: Government servants in Pay Levels 6 to 8 are entitled to travel by Air (Economy Class) within India.
    • On LTC: Government servants in Pay Levels 6 to 8 are NOT entitled to travel by Air! Their normal LTC entitlement is restricted to rail as specified in CCS (LTC) Rule 12, subject to any current special-dispensation or later order.
    • Exception: Air travel is allowed for Levels 6 to 8 only under the "Special Dispensation Scheme" (for travel to Northeast Region, J&K, Ladakh, and Andaman & Nicobar) from designated railheads, or with special relaxation.
  • 2. Daily Allowance (DA) & Incidentals:
    • On Tour: Daily Allowance (Hotel, Food, Local conveyance) is fully admissible under standard ceilings.
    • On LTC: No Daily Allowance (DA) of any kind is admissible. Incidental expenses and local journeys (stay, boarding, taxis within the city) are non-reimbursable under the cited LTC rule.
  • 3. Travel in Private/Hired Vehicles (Own Car / Hired Taxi):
    • On Tour/Transfer: Road mileage is fully reimbursable for journeys performed in own car, hired taxi, or public transport.
    • On LTC: Travel is admissible only in vehicles operated by the Government or Public Sector Corporations (Central/State/Local body), subject to the stated exceptions. Private transport is not admissible except where a specific rule or exception applies.
    • Exceptions on LTC:
      • Disability exception (LTC only): DoPT’s 01.07.2025 LTC FAQ, Q30, says own/hired taxi may be used where disability of the employee or dependent makes authorized modes infeasible, subject to a competent-authority medical certificate, an undertaking that authorized travel is not feasible and the claimant actually used the own/hired vehicle, and a reimbursement cap no higher than entitled rail/air fare by the shortest route. Do not extend this to local official-tour travel.
      • Unconnected Places (DoPT O.M. dated 04.02.2021): If the relevant places are not connected by public transport, the stated private-transport exception is limited to a maximum of 100 km each side, subject to the applicable order and evidence requirements.
  • 4. Tour Packages:
    • On LTC: Tour packages run by private travel operators are not allowed. Only the tour packages permitted by the applicable CCS (LTC) instructions may be claimed; verify the allowed operator and fare component.
    • Reimbursement Scale: Only the travel fare component is reimbursable. The organizing agency must separately indicate and certify the fare component from the boarding/lodging package.
  • 5. Claim Submission Timelines (Contrast with TA):
    • If LTC advance is drawn: The claim for reimbursement is normally due within one month after completion of the return journey (CCS (LTC) Rule 15(vi)). A later, limited relaxation may allow up to three months with FA concurrence if the entire advance plus penal interest is refunded in one lump sum.
    • If no LTC advance is drawn: The claim is normally due within three months after completion of the return journey (Rule 14). A later, limited FA-concurred relaxation may admit it within six months.
    • TA Contrast: The ordinary 60-day TA claim-submission limit applies whether or not a TA advance was drawn; advance adjustment is separate. The inspected 2005 Compendium states 15 days for tour advances, but its currency is unverified and no general 30-day transfer deadline was located.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • LTC vs Tour/Transfer — 5 bade farq jo exams mein aate hain:
  • 1. Level 6-8 Air Ticket: Tour pe → Flight allowed (Economy). LTC pe → Flight NOT allowed (sirf AC-II Tier train)! Exception: Special dispensation scheme under J&K, NER, Ladakh, A&N jaane ke liye. Ye sabse commonly tested difference hai.
  • 2. Daily Allowance: Tour pe → Hotel + Food + Travel DA milta hai. LTC pe → Koi DA nahi — sirf aane-jaane ka ticket fare. LTC personal vacation hai — sarkar khana/hotel nahi degi.
  • 3. Private Taxi: Tour TA and LTC have different rules. For LTC, a disability-related own/hired taxi is only a conditional exception (medical certificate, undertaking, actual journey, fare cap); the public-transport-unconnected route is a separate exception with its own 100 km each-side limit. Neither exception establishes a pay-level-independent local AC taxi claim on official tour/training.
  • 4. Tour Packages: LTC pe sirf ITDC, STDC (State Tourism), ya IRCTC ke tour packages allowed hain. Private tour operators (Thomas Cook, Cox & Kings, etc.) ke packages se LTC claim nahi hoga.
  • 5. Claim Deadlines: LTC advance drawn → the bill is normally due within one month after the return journey; no advance → three months. The DoPT O.M. dated 21.12.2023 provides limited FA-concurred relaxation (up to three months with advance, subject to full refund plus penal interest; up to six months without advance). Ordinary TA claims have a 60-day submission window whether or not an advance was drawn; TA advance adjustment is a separate, not-fully-established question.
Memory Hook

"LTC is Government-Only, Train-Only (for Level 6-8). No DA, no private taxi (unless <100km unconnected), and no private tour packages!"

High-YieldCancellation-charge reimbursement rules

ProvisoExceptionExplanation
Source L2346–2369
Exact-Source Fact Card

Under the TA/DA rules, cancellation and rescheduling charges for air/rail/bus tickets booked on Government account are reimbursable subject to strict conditions:

  • Official Exigency: The cancellation or postponement of the official tour, training, or transfer must be necessitated by public/official interest under the order of the competent authority.
  • Supporting Certificates: The claim for reimbursement must be accompanied by:
    • A certificate from the Controlling Officer stating that the cancellation/postponement was due to official exigencies of service.
    • Receipts/vouchers issued by the authorized travel agents (or airlines/railways) indicating the actual cancellation fee or deduction made.
  • Personal Reasons: If the ticket is cancelled due to personal reasons of the employee, the cancellation charges must be borne by the employee themselves, unless specifically condoned under emergency medical circumstances by the Head of Department.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Tour cancel hua to cancellation charges kaun dega? Agar aapne official tour ka ticket book kiya aur phir tour cancel/postpone ho gaya, to cancellation charges reimbursable hain — lekin conditions ke saath:
  • Official reason se cancel hua: Agar public/official interest mein tour cancel hua (boss ne bola "meeting postpone ho gayi" ya "budget nahi hai"), to poora cancellation charge sarkar degi. Conditions: (1) Controlling Officer ka certificate chahiye ki "ye cancellation official exigency mein hua," (2) Airline/railway ka cancellation receipt/proof chahiye.
  • Personal reason se cancel hua: Agar aapne apne personal reason se tour cancel kiya (jaise "meri tabiyat kharab nahi hai, bas man nahi hai"), to cancellation charges aapko khud bharna padega — sarkar nahi degi. Exception: Emergency medical circumstances mein HoD condone kar sakta hai.

ch10-010

High-YieldRules on journeys by a longer / circuitous route

ProvisoExceptionExplanation
Source L2370–2399
Exact-Source Fact Card

Under SR 30 and SR 31, Travelling Allowance must be calculated based on the shortest and cheapest route usually open to the public:

  • Shortest Route Rule (SR 30): The shortest route is that by which the traveler can most speedily reach their destination. If two routes are equally short, but one is cheaper, the cheaper route must be used.
  • Personal Convenience (SR 31): If a Government servant performs a journey by a longer or circuitous route for personal reasons or convenience:
    • Their TA/fare reimbursement is strictly restricted to the fare and road mileage of the shortest route.
    • The excess time spent on travel via the longer route is treated as leave (Casual Leave/Earned Leave) and cannot be treated as duty.
  • Public Interest Exception: A journey by a longer/circuitous route may be allowed with full TA reimbursement and treated as duty ONLY IF:
    • The longer route is specifically prescribed or approved by the competent authority in public interest.
    • The shortest route is blocked or disrupted due to natural disasters (landslides, floods), civil unrest, or suspension of train/road services, and proof of such blockage is furnished.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Ghum ke gaye to kya hoga? (SR 30 & SR 31): Rule simple hai: hamesha shortest route se travel karo. Agar aap apni marzi se lamba rasta (circuitous route) choose karte ho personal convenience ke liye, to 2 consequences hain:
  • 1. Fare restriction: Sarkar sirf shortest route ka fare degi — jo extra paisa aapne lambe raste pe kharcha kiya, wo aapki jeb se jaayega. Example: Delhi-Mumbai shortest route = ₹3,000, lekin aap Delhi-Jaipur-Ahmedabad-Mumbai gaye = ₹5,000. Sarkar sirf ₹3,000 degi — baaki ₹2,000 aapka personal kharcha.
  • 2. Extra time = Leave: Jo extra din lambe raste pe lage — wo duty nahi, leave maane jayenge (CL/EL). Example: Shortest route se 1 din lagta, lekin aapne ghum ke 3 din lagaye — to 2 extra din aapki CL/EL se katenge.
  • Exception — Public Interest: Agar (1) competent authority ne written order diya hai ki "is route se jaao" (public interest mein), ya (2) shortest route blocked hai (floods, landslides, civil unrest, rail roko) aur proof hai — to lambe raste ka full fare + full duty days milenge.
Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

SR 30/31: shortest and cheapest route; longer route for personal convenience → fare/mileage of shortest route, excess time = leave. Exceptions (full TA, duty): route prescribed/approved in public interest; shortest route blocked by disaster/unrest/suspension with proof.

ch10-011

Moderate-YieldDDO & Controlling Officer certificates required on a TA bill

OrderProvisoExplanation
Source L2400–2425
Exact-Source Fact Card

To ensure financial propriety and prevent double billing under Treasury Rules and G.A.R. 14-A (Tour) / G.A.R. 14-B (Transfer), specific certificates must be recorded and signed by the Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO) and the Controlling Officer before passing a TA bill:

  • 1. Proper Sanction: "Certified that the journeys/mileage claims made in this bill were performed under proper sanction and strictly in the interest of public service."
  • 2. Class & Fare Verification: "Certified that I have checked the claimant’s travel class and, where the claim includes an air ticket, compliance with the applicable Government-account ATA instructions."
  • 3. Shortest Route: "Certified that the journeys were performed by the shortest and most economical route usually open to the public."
  • 4. No Free Transport: "Certified that the claimant was not provided with free Government transport/conveyance for any part of the journey for which road mileage has been claimed." (Or if provided, the mileage has been proportionately deducted/not claimed).
  • 5. Original Order Endorsement: "Certified that the original tour/transfer/LTC order has been produced and a note of this TA claim has been endorsed thereon to prevent duplicate claims."
  • 6. Scrutiny of Vouchers (Transfer/Retirement): "Certified that the details of actual expenditure for the transportation of personal effects and conveyance have been scrutinized and found to be correct, reasonable, and supported by authentic vouchers."
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • DDO (Drawing & Disbursing Officer) ko TA bill pass karne se pehle kya-kya check karna hai? Ye financial propriety ke 6 certificates hain jo DDO ko sign karne padte hain:
  • 1. Proper Sanction: "Ye journey properly sanctioned thi — boss ne permission di thi — aur public interest mein thi." Bina sanctioned tour ke koi TA nahi.
  • 2. Class & Fare Verification: Verify the admissible class for the journey. If the claim includes an air ticket, check the applicable ATA channel and any authorized relaxation; rail-ticket evidence follows its separate applicable rules.
  • 3. Shortest Route: "Journey shortest route se hui" — ghum ke nahi gaye.
  • 4. No Free Transport: "Employee ko koi free sarkari gaadi nahi mili thi is journey ke liye" — agar mili thi to mileage claim nahi kar sakte.
  • 5. Original Order Endorsement: "Original tour/transfer order pe is TA claim ka note laga diya hai — taaki double billing na ho." Matlab ek hi order pe do baar claim nahi kar sakte.
  • 6. Voucher Scrutiny (Transfer/Retirement): "Baggage transport ke vouchers maine check kiye hain — sahi hain, reasonable hain, authentic hain."
Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Six DDO/Controlling Officer certificates: proper sanction; class & fare (ATA compliance for air); shortest route; no free transport for mileage; original order endorsed; vouchers scrutinised for personal effects/conveyance.

Study aid · Practice MCQs (written for this guide; answers cite the source card)
Q1. Dependency income ceiling for a family member (other than spouse)?
  1. ₹3,500
  2. ₹9,000 plus DR
  3. ₹9,000 flat
  4. ₹25,000

Answer: (b) ₹9,000 plus DR — “does not exceed ₹9,000/- per month plus Dearness Relief (DR)”. The 6th CPC figure ₹3,500 is the trap. Rule citation: ch10-004 (L2138–2180)

Q2. A disabled, wholly dependent son — age limit for TA ‘family’?
  1. 18
  2. 21
  3. 25
  4. No age limit

Answer: (d) No age limit — “for sons with physical or mental disabilities, there is no age limit”. Rule citation: ch10-004

Q3. How many Class-X cities does the guide list?
  1. 4
  2. 6
  3. 8
  4. 97

Answer: (c) 8 — “Class X (8 Cities)”. Rule citation: ch10-005 (L2181–2227)

Q4. On SCI island ships, PML 6–8 officers are entitled to:
  1. Deluxe
  2. First Class / ‘A’ Cabin
  3. Second / ‘B’ Cabin
  4. Bunk

Answer: (b) First Class / ‘A’ Cabin — PML 9+ Deluxe; 6–8 First/‘A’; 4–5 Second/‘B’; 1–3 Bunk. Rule citation: ch10-006 (L2228–2258)

Q5. Pay Levels 6 to 8 on LTC (normal entitlement) as against tour:
  1. Economy air on both
  2. Not entitled to air on LTC; rail under CCS (LTC) Rule 12, subject to later orders/special dispensation
  3. No travel entitlement
  4. AC-I only

Answer: (b) Not entitled to air on LTC; rail under CCS (LTC) Rule 12, subject to later orders/special dispensation — Classic exam trap — tour air yes, LTC air no. Rule citation: ch10-008 (L2288–2345)

Q6. Journey by a longer route for personal convenience — TA admissible?
  1. Full fare
  2. Fare/mileage of shortest route only; excess time treated as leave
  3. Nil
  4. Average of both routes

Answer: (b) Fare/mileage of shortest route only; excess time treated as leave — SR 31. Rule citation: ch10-010 (L2370–2399)
