Source L3297–3311
DoPT O.M. No. DOPT-1669266628362 (NESTS-hosted Government reproduction; direct DoPT record not retrieved) dated 24.11.2022, Para 2.1

As per the Ministry of Finance O.M. No. 3379-E.III(B)/65 dated 17th June 1965, technical resignation is a resignation treated as a technical formality where a Government servant has applied through proper channel for a post in the same or some other Department, and is on selection, required to resign the previous post for administrative reasons.

Critical Point: The resignation will be treated as technical resignation if these conditions are met, even if the Government servant has not mentioned the word "Technical" while submitting his resignation.

Simple Explanation (Hinglish)
  • Technical Resignation kya hai? Maan lo aap ek sarkari naukri mein ho (jaise SSC se select hue, Level 5 pe kaam kar rahe ho). Aapne dusri sarkari naukri ke liye apply kiya — "through proper channel" (matlab apne current office ke through, NOC leke, forwarding letter ke saath). Aap select ho gaye. Ab nayi department mein join karne ke liye purani naukri se resign karna padega. Ye resignation "technical" hai — ye koi voluntary leaving nahi hai, ye sirf ek administrative formality hai taaki aap nayi post join kar sako.
  • "Through Proper Channel" ka matlab: Ye sabse important requirement hai. Aapka application current office se forward hona chahiye — NOC (No Objection Certificate) ke saath, proper forwarding letter ke saath. Agar aapne directly apply kiya bina office ko bataye, to technically ye "proper channel" nahi hai — lekin DoPT ne 2022 ke OM mein ek relaxation di hai (para 2.5): agar authority satisfy ho jaaye ki "agar employee service mein hota to application forward ki jaati," to bhi technical resignation treat ho sakta hai.
  • Word "Technical" likhna zaroori nahi: Agar aapne resignation letter mein "technical resignation" nahi likha — bas normal resign kiya — lekin conditions meet hoti hain (proper channel se apply kiya tha, usi post ke liye resign kar rahe ho), to automatically technical resignation maana jaayega.

ch12-004Micro-note — Three Essential Conditions for Technical Resignation: 1. Applied through proper …
Source L3312–3320

Three Essential Conditions for Technical Resignation:

1. Applied through proper channel — Application forwarded by current employer with NOC

2. Selected for a Government post — Central/State Government, PSU, or autonomous body

3. Required to resign for administrative reasons — Not voluntary leaving, but necessity to join new post

NO minimum service requirement for technical resignation itself — it can be done at any stage (even during probation).
