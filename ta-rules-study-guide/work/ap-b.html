Q1. The O.M. No.19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 13.07.2017 is effective from which date?
(a) 13.07.2017   (b) 01.07.2017   (c) 01.01.2017   (d) 01.04.2017
Answer: (b) — the O.M. is explicitly backdated to 01.07.2017; (a) is a trap using the issue date instead of the effective date; (c) and (d) are generic fiscal-year-start distractors with no textual basis.

Q2. Under the current (post DA-50%) rates, what is the Mileage Allowance for own car where no state rate is prescribed?
(a) ₹24/km (2017 base)   (b) ₹27/km   (c) ₹30/km   (d) ₹36/km
Answer: (c) — ₹30/km is the currently applicable rate since DA ≥ 50%; (a) is the 2017 base rate (₹24/km); (b) and (d) are arithmetic distractors.

Q3. For a station-to-station transfer of 18 km with no actual change of residence, Composite Transfer Grant is:
(a) 100% of basic pay   (b) 80% of basic pay   (c) Nil   (d) 50% of basic pay
Answer: (c) — 18 km is below the 20 km threshold and the stem specifies no actual change of residence, so CTG is nil; (b) is the trap for candidates who forget the 20 km cutoff.

Q4. Was a fresh Office Memorandum required to raise TA/DA rates by 25% when DA crossed 50% in January 2024?
(a) Yes, O.M. dated 01.01.2024   (b) Yes, O.M. dated 13.07.2024   (c) No — automatic under the 2017 O.M.'s standing clause   (d) No revision occurred
Answer: (c) — this is the single most important nuance of Chapter 8; (a) and (b) invent plausible-sounding but non-existent dedicated TA O.Ms; (d) is factually wrong.

Q5 (Assertion-Reason). Assertion (A): Levels 6–8 officials cannot travel by air on tour. Reason (R): Only Level 9 and above were given air entitlement under the 7th CPC.
(a) Both A and R are true, R explains A   (b) Both A and R are true, R does not explain A   (c) A is false, R is false   (d) A is true, R is false
Answer: (c) — both statements are false: Levels 6–11 (not just 9+) are entitled to Economy air travel under 7th CPC.

Q6 (Statement-based). Consider the following statements about DA on tour: (1) Food component requires a voucher. (2) Hotel stays in Class-X cities always need a voucher for Level ≤8. (3) DA is reckoned midnight-to-midnight. How many statements are correct?
(a) Only one   (b) Only two   (c) All three   (d) None
No validated key — unscored. Statement (1) is false: the food component is a lump sum, not a separate voucher claim. Statement (3) is supported: absence from Headquarters is reckoned midnight-to-midnight on a per-day basis. Statement (2) remains unresolved: the 13.07.2017 DoE O.M. allows Level 8 and below to self-certify hotel claims up to the ceiling, while separately setting a Class-X ceiling of ₹1,000/day against relevant vouchers. The exact interaction for a Level 8-or-below Class-X claim within the ordinary ceiling is not established by this targeted review. The source-snapshot key (b) is withdrawn; do not score this item until the applicable instruction and voucher scope are confirmed.

Q8. A Level 8 employee is transferred within the same city, which involves an actual change of residence. Their entitlement for Composite Transfer Grant (CTG) is:
(a) Nil   (b) 80% of Basic Pay   (c) 1/3 of the normal rate (26.67% of Basic Pay)   (d) 50% of Basic Pay
Answer: (c) — For same-station transfers involving change of residence, the CTG is restricted to 1/3 of the normal rate (which is 1/3 of 80% = 26.67%). If there was no change of residence, it would be Nil (Option a).

Q13. Under the LTC rules, if a Government servant travels by private taxi on a stretch not connected by any public transport, what is the maximum distance for which they can claim reimbursement?
(a) 50 km each side
(b) 100 km each side (to-and-fro combined 200 km)
(c) No limit, actual distance is reimbursed
(d) Private taxi is completely prohibited under all circumstances on LTC
Answer: (b), subject to the order’s scope. DoPT O.M. 31011/3/2015-Estt.(A-IV), 04.02.2021, clarifies that the 100-km cap in the unconnected-stretch LTC route is each side (200 km to-and-fro). The underlying exception is conditional: the journey is to the declared LTC place after using authorized transport to the nearest airport/railway station/bus terminal, no public transport is available on the relevant stretch, and the prescribed self-certification and entitled-transfer-journey rate cap apply. This targeted source check does not verify the whole MCQ package.

Q17. An officer is posted from Delhi to Gurugram (within the same Urban Agglomeration) for an indefinite period, and changes residence. Their Composite Transfer Grant (CTG) entitlement is:
(a) Nil — same city, no TA
(b) Full CTG (80% of Basic Pay) — it is a permanent transfer
(c) 1/3rd of normal CTG (26.67% of Basic Pay) — same UA with change of residence
(d) 50% of normal CTG
Answer: (c) — A transfer within the same Urban Agglomeration involving an actual change of residence attracts a restricted CTG of 1/3rd of the normal rate (1/3 × 80% = 26.67% of Basic Pay). Option (a) would apply only if there was no change of residence. Option (b) applies only for inter-station transfers ≥20 km.
