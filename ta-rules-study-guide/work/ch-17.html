Q: I'm on probation (1.5 years completed). Can I apply for another post through proper channel?

Q: What if my department refuses to forward my application through proper channel?
The department can refuse only on valid grounds (e.g., disciplinary proceedings, bond obligation, shortage of staff). If the refusal is arbitrary, you can appeal to higher authorities. If you apply directly without proper channel and get selected, it will NOT be treated as technical resignation — you'll lose all past service benefits.

Department can refuse forwarding only on valid grounds (e.g. disciplinary proceedings, bond, staff shortage); direct application without proper channel → not technical resignation.

Q: I'm on deputation to a PSU. Can I apply for another Central Government post through proper channel?
Yes. Your application should be forwarded by your parent department (not the PSU). If selected, you submit technical resignation from the parent department post. TTA is calculated from your deputation station (current place of duty) to the new post.

Q: What happens to my GPF/NPS account on technical resignation?
Your GPF account is transferred to the new office under Rule 35 of GPF (CS) Rules, 1960. Your NPS account (PRAN + PRA balance) is also transferred to the new office. Both continue uninterrupted.

Q: Can I retain lien if I join a State Government post?
Yes. A permanent Central Government employee appointed to a State Government post retains lien on the parent post for 2 years (extendable to 3 in exceptional cases). You must revert within this period or the lien ceases.

Source L4035–4039
Q: What if I don't mention "technical resignation" in my resignation letter?
It doesn't matter. Under DoPT O.M. Para 2.1, if all conditions are met (applied through proper channel, selected for Government post, required to resign), the resignation WILL be treated as technical resignation even if you didn't use the word "technical." However, it's advisable to mention it explicitly to avoid confusion.
Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Word “technical” not needed under O.M. para 2.1 if conditions are met; advisable to mention it.
