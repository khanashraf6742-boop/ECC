ch13-004Micro-note — Use this decision aid instead of the old yes/no chart: 1. First assess technical…Explanation
Source L3345–3362

Use this decision aid instead of the old yes/no chart:

1. First assess technical-resignation status under the applicable DoPT conditions (including proper-channel / prior-application rules where relevant).

2. Separately check whether the appointment is within Rule 4(4) as substituted in 2015, including its conditions and service-counting option.

3. Separately read DoPT O.M. para 3.6.3 and check for later binding clarification or department-specific instructions.

4. If the two provisions affect the case, obtain a written decision from the competent authority. This study aid does not decide an employee's TTA or Joining Time Pay.

Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Technical resignation aur TTA/Joining Time Pay alag sawal hain. Technical resignation valid ho sakta hai, lekin usse TTA ya joining-time pay automatically decide nahi hota.
  • 2015 ke Rule 4(4) amendment mein qualifying competitive appointments ke liye conditions hain, including past service ko Central Government mein all purposes ke liye count karne ka option; us substituted wording mein explicit 3-year cutoff nahi dikhta.
  • DoPT ke 24.11.2022 OM, para 3.6.3, temporary Central Government employees with under 3 years ke TTA ko restrict karta hai.
  • In dono texts ka interaction yahan unresolved hai. Sirf "confirmed" ya "temporary + under 3 years" se final yes/no mat nikaalo; competent authority se applicable rule aur written decision lo.

Q1. Does the substituted Rule 4(4) (G.S.R. 229(E), 27.03.2015) contain an express three-year cutoff, per the guide?
  1. Yes
  2. No — conditional wording with an option to count past service
  3. Only for temporary employees
  4. Only for State posts

Answer: (b) No — conditional wording with an option to count past service — “the substituted wording contains no express three-year cutoff.” Rule citation: ch13-003 (L3336–3344)

Q2. What does DoPT O.M. 24.11.2022 para 3.6.3 state?
  1. All confirmed staff get TTA
  2. Temporary Central Govt employees with <3 years’ regular continuous service are not entitled to TTA
  3. Technical resignation requires 3 years
  4. Lien lasts 3 years

Answer: (b) Temporary Central Govt employees with <3 years’ regular continuous service are not entitled to TTA — As stated in the authority comparison. Rule citation: ch13-003

Q3. The guide’s safe approach where the two provisions both affect a case?
  1. Apply the three-year rule
  2. Apply Rule 4(4) only
  3. Obtain a written decision from the competent authority
  4. Pay TTA by default

Answer: (c) Obtain a written decision from the competent authority — Decision aid step 4. Rule citation: ch13-004 (L3345–3362)
