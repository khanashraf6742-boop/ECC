RED-FLAG TRAP

Flag: "Levels 6–8 are too junior for air travel." Fix: Under the 7th CPC rules, Levels 6 to 11 are entitled to Economy Class air travel on tour — a major liberalisation from the 6th CPC regime. Fix-phrase: "6 through 13 all fly economy or better; only 5-and-below stays on the ground (by air)."

Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Level 14 aur usse upar (Secretary / Addl. Secretary level): Ye sabse senior officers hain — inko flight mein Business Class ya Club Class milti hai (sabse mehngi class), aur train mein AC First Class (AC-I). Matlab agar ye Delhi se Mumbai ja rahe hain to Business Class ka ticket fully reimburse hoga — koi ceiling nahi hai flight fare pe. Train mein poora private cabin milta hai AC-I mein. Ye level typically Secretary, Additional Secretary, ya equivalent rank ke officers ka hota hai (basic pay ₹2,25,000+).
  • Level 12, 13 aur 13A (Director / Joint Secretary level): Inko flight mein Economy Class milti hai (Business Class nahi — ye Level 14+ ke liye reserved hai), aur train mein AC First Class (AC-I). 7th CPC se pehle (6th CPC mein) Level 13A ka koi separate mention nahi tha TA table mein — 4 September 2017 ko ek alag O.M. nikla jisne clear kiya ki Level 13A = Level 13 same entitlements. Ye bohot commonly tested point hai exams mein. Note: Level 13A ka Grade Pay pehle ₹8,900 tha — ye Director (Senior) ya equivalent posts ke liye hai.
  • Level 9 se 11 (Under Secretary / Deputy Secretary level): 7th CPC ka sabse bada gift — in levels ko pehli baar flight mein travel karne ka right mila! Pehle (6th CPC mein) sirf Level 12+ hi flight le sakte the. Ab Level 9 se 11 wale bhi Economy Class flight le sakte hain official tour pe. Train mein AC-II Tier milti hai. Example: Agar aap Under Secretary (Level 11) ho aur Delhi se Guwahati jaana hai training ke liye, to aap Indigo/Air India ki Economy class flight le sakte ho — poora paisa reimburse hoga. Hotel ceiling (current): ₹2,812.50/day, Food lump sum: ₹1,125/day.
  • Level 6 se 8 (Section Officer / Inspector level): Ye bhi Economy Class flight + AC-II train ke entitled hain — bilkul Level 9-11 jaisa! Ye 7th CPC ka revolutionary change tha. Pehle (6th CPC mein) Level 6-8 walon ko flight bilkul nahi milti thi — sirf train se travel karna padta tha. Ab ek Inspector (Level 6) bhi official tour pe flight le sakta hai! LEKIN EK BADA EXCEPTION: Ye flight entitlement sirf official tour/transfer/training pe hai. LTC (Leave Travel Concession) pe Level 6-8 ko flight nahi milti — sirf AC-II Tier train milegi (special dispensation ko chhod ke — jaise J&K, NER, Ladakh, A&N jaane ke liye). Ye sabse commonly tested trap hai!
  • Level 5 aur usse neeche (LDC, MTS, Peon etc.): Inko flight nahi milti (normally). Train mein AC-III Tier ya AC Chair Car milta hai. Koi special relaxation se (jaise medical emergency ya extreme urgency) flight mil sakti hai, lekin ye rare hai aur competent authority ki specific approval chahiye. Inka hotel ceiling (current) ₹562.50/day hai, food lump sum ₹625/day hai — ye amounts DA≥50% enhanced hain (pehle ₹450 aur ₹500 the).
  • Real-life comparison: Socho ek office mein 4 log hain — Secretary (L14), Director (L13), Section Officer (L7), aur Peon (L1). Sabko Delhi-Chennai tour pe bheja. Secretary Business Class flight lega, Director Economy flight lega, Section Officer bhi Economy flight lega (7th CPC ka gift!), aur Peon train se AC-III mein jayega. Sabka hotel bhi alag level ka hoga — Secretary ₹9,375/day tak, Director ₹5,625/day tak, SO ₹937.50/day tak, Peon ₹562.50/day tak.

ch02-005

High-YieldPremium / Special trains & international travel

Explanation
Source L898–910
Exact-Source Fact Card

Officials may travel by Premium Trains/Premium Tatkal Trains/Suvidha Trains, with reimbursement of Premium Tatkal charges. Dynamic/Flexi-fare in Shatabdi/Rajdhani/Duronto trains is reimbursable while on official tour/training. Tatkal Seva Charges (fixed fare) continue to be allowed. The 12.09.2022 Tejas O.M. allows Tejas travel for tour, training, transfer and retirement, with class entitlement as for Shatabdi trains. For international air travel, the entitlement cited here is based on SR 48-B and remains outside this targeted source check; confirm the operative primary text before use.

Train typePay Level entitlement
Rajdhani / Shatabdi / Duronto / Tejas (dynamic-fare)All eligible levels, as per their base class entitlement (12 & above → Executive/AC-I; 6–11 → AC-II/Chair Car; 5 & below → AC-III/Chair Car)
Analogy

Think of "dynamic fare" trains like Uber's surge pricing — the government doesn't cap your reimbursement to the "normal" fare; it reimburses whatever the surge price was, since you had no choice on an official tour.

Exam Angle / PYQ

Trap: Tatkal Seva Charge (fixed, small fee) vs Premium Tatkal (dynamic, can be large) — both are reimbursable, but examiners test whether you know both exist and are treated alike (reimbursable), unlike ordinary "convenience fee" add-ons which are not.

Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

International air basis (SR 48-B) is outside the targeted source check — confirm primary text.
