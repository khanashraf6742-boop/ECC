High-YieldIllustration 1 — Composite Transfer Grant calculation

Source L2072–2083
Worked Example

Scenario: A Level 10 officer with last basic pay ₹56,100 is transferred from Chennai to Coimbatore (over 400 km apart).
Step 1: Distance ≥ 20 km → CTG applicable.
Step 2: Destination is not A&N/Lakshadweep → standard 80% rate applies.
Step 3: CTG = 80% × ₹56,100 = ₹44,880.
Result: ₹44,880 payable as CTG, in addition to travel fare and personal-effects transportation cost.
Counter-example: If the same officer were transferred instead from Chennai HQ to a suburban office 12 km away, CTG would be nil — only actual conveyance charges (if any) would be considered.

ch09-005

High-YieldIllustration 2 — Mixed-mode Mileage Allowance

Source L2084–2094
Worked Example

Scenario: A Level 4 official travels 15 km by auto-rickshaw (no state rate notified) then walks the last 2 km to a remote field office.
Step 1: Auto-rickshaw leg: 15 km × current rate ₹15/km = ₹225.
Step 2: On-foot leg: 2 km × current rate ₹15/km = ₹30.
Step 3: Total Mileage Allowance = ₹225 + ₹30 = ₹255.
Takeaway: Mixed-mode journeys are billed leg-by-leg at each mode's applicable rate — they are not averaged.

ch09-006

High-YieldIllustration 3 — Short tour, DA timing-band edge case

Source L2095–2105
Worked Example

Scenario: A Level 13 officer leaves HQ at 8:00 AM and returns the same day at 1:45 PM (5 hours 45 minutes away), on a same-day tour with no hotel stay.
Step 1: Duration = 5h45m → falls in the "<6 hours" band.
Step 2: Applicable % = 30% of the lump-sum food component. Current Level-12/13 food rate = ₹1,250/day.
Step 3: DA = 30% × ₹1,250 = ₹375.
Counter-example: Had the officer returned 20 minutes later (6h05m), the absence would cross into the "6–12 hours" band, paying 70% × ₹1,250 = ₹875 — more than double, for 20 extra minutes. This edge-case is a favourite in numerical MCQs.

Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 9

Source L2115–2122
  • CTG = 80%/100%/0% of last basic pay depending on distance/destination.
  • Mixed-mode mileage journeys are billed leg-by-leg, never averaged.
  • DA timing bands create sharp cliff-edges (30%→70%→100%) — minutes can double your claim.
