TA/DA and LTC Rules: 54 Important FAQs on LTC Eligibility, Travel Entitlements, Leave Encashment and Reimbursement

Isha Gupta 0 November 16, 2026 9:37 am

TA/DA and LTC Rules: 54 Important FAQs on LTC Eligibility, Travel Entitlements, Leave Encashment and Reimbursement. Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS), in Section 7 of its FAQs on Procurement and Other Financial Matters, addresses 54 questions on Travelling Allowance (TA), Daily Allowance (DA), and Leave Travel Concession (LTC). Topics include eligibility, authorised ticket booking, permissible travel routes, family entitlement, leave encashment, reimbursement, and supporting documents.

KVS employees and other Central Government personnel covered by the applicable TA/DA and LTC provisions, including eligible family members. Generally, LTC requires one year of continuous service and admissible leave; reimbursement remains subject to prescribed travel entitlements and conditions.

Employees should obtain necessary approvals, book tickets through authorised channels where required, retain tickets and receipts, and submit complete claims within prescribed deadlines. They should check applicable rules before changing destinations, claiming leave encashment, or travelling by alternative routes.

FAQs on Procurement and Other Financial Matters | Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan

SECTION 7 TA/DA and LTC Rules

Q1. Whether encashment can be availed during Casual Leave while availing LTC?
Yes.

Q2. Whether Air Tickets can be booked through private agents on TA/DA & LTC?
No, as per para 3(viii) of MOF, Dept. of Exp. OM No. 19024/1/E-IV/2005 dt. 24.3.2026 24.03.2006 only from authorized travel agents that are M/s Balmer Lawrie, IRCTC and M/s Ashoka Tours & Travels.

Q3. What is the minimum service for availing LTC?
Government servant who have completed one year of continuous service on the date of journey can avail this concession. {LTC Rules}

Q4. Which kind of leave is necessary for availing LTC?
Travel on LTC is admissible during any kind of leave, including casual leave, special casual leave and child care leave.

Q5. Whether LTC can be availed during weekend/holidays alone?
No. It is necessary to avail of any kind of leave due and admissible when the LTC is availed of by the Government employee.

Q6. When both husband and wife are government servants, can they declare separate home town independently?
Yes

Q7. Whether a Government employee can travel by longer route?
Yes, however reimbursement for his/her journey will be restricted to shortest direct route.

Q8. Whether LTC can be availed during study leave?
Yes, however reimbursement of fare should be restricted to the fare admissible for travel between his headquarters station to any place in India/home town or actual expenditure, whichever is less.

Q9. Whether LTC can be availed under suspension?
No, Government servant under suspension cannot avail of LTC as he/she cannot get any leave including casual leave during the period of suspension; however his family is entitled to LTC.

Q10. What is the entitlement of fresh recruits?
Fresh recruits of Central government may be allowed to travel to their home town with their families on three occasions in a block of four years and to any place in India on the fourth occasion. This facility shall be available to the Government officers only for the first two blocks of four years applicable after joining the Government for the first time. The blocks of 4 years shall apply with reference to the initial date of joining the Government even though the employee changes the job within Government subsequently. The existing blocks will remain the same but the entitlements of the new recruit will be different in the first eight years of service.

Q11. Home town of an employee is connected by train but is not directly connected by Rajdhani/Shatabdi Express trains. In such a case, can the employee be entitled to travel partly by Rajdhani/Shatabdi Express trains and partly by other trains?
Yes, If the journey is actually performed by Rajdhani/Shatabdi trains up to an en route railway station by direct shortest route and thereafter the journey is completed in a train other than Rajdhani/Shatabdi trains, fare for both the types of trains by the entitled class would be admissible for the respective portion of journey.

Q12. If the originating and terminating points fall on the routes of Rajdhani/Shatabdi express trains but these trains do not halt at these stations, can an employee travel by Rajdhani/Shatabdi Express?
The employee can travel by Rajdhani/Shatabdi Express up to the nearest en route station which should not be beyond the destination i.e., Home town or the declare place of visit.

Q13. If a Government employee/his family member is entitled to concessional train fare such as senior citizen, student concession, children, etc., whether in case of air travel, the reimbursement would be restricted to such concessional fare by train in entitled class?
If full air fare has been charged by the airlines and paid by the Government servant, the reimbursement would be restricted to the full train fare in entitled class including Rajdhani/Shatabdi.

Q14. Are parents-in-law covered under LTC?
No. As per Rule 4(d) of the CCS (LTC) Rules, 1988, read with DOPT O.M. No. F. No. 31011/07/2025-PP.A-IV dated 01.07.2025, parents-in-law are not included in the definition of family for LTC purposes.

Q15. Whether DA is admissible on Sunday, holiday, casual leave and RH to Government Servant on temporary duty at out station?
1. Government servant availing CL or RH while on tour is not entitled to draw DA.
2. DA is not admissible for any day whether Sunday or holiday unless the officer is actually and not merely constructively in camp.

Q16. How mileage allowance is admissible when journey on tour is performed by longer route by rail partly by lower class and partly by the entitled class?
In such case claim shall be regulated on proportionate basis, by calculating mileage allowance for different modes/classes by the shortest route in the ration of distance covered by such modes/classes by the longer actually used route.

Q17. Is TATKAL SEVA charges reimbursable?
Yes

Q18. Whether DA to Government servant on tour returning to Headquarters daily is admissible?
Yes, a Government servant who returns to his Hqrs while on tour at outstation due to non-availability of proper accommodation etc. to suite his convenience, may draw DA at the rate applicable at temporary duty station, but on intervening Sundays and holidays no DA will be admissible.

Q19. Whether hotel/ guest house should be registered/recognized?
For any hotel the Government servant must have stayed in registered/licensed as a hotel by proper authority.

Q20. Whether cash receipt/ cash memo will be mandatory in support of the claims?
Yes actual expenditure incurred within the prescribed limit will be reimbursed as per normal procedure and rules.

Q21. Whether encashment of leave is allowed after LTC is availed?
Sanction of leave encashment should, as a practice, be done in advance, at the time of sanctioning the LTC. However, ex-post-facto sanction of leave encashment on LTC may be considered by the sanctioning authority as an exception in deserving cases within the time limit prescribed for submission of claims for LTC.

Q22. Whether encashment of leave with LTC can be availed at the time when the LTC is availed by the Government servant only or can leave be encashed at the time when LTC is availed by family members?
A Govt. servant can be permitted to encash earned leave upto 10 days either at the time of availing LTC for himself or when his family avails it provided other conditions are satisfied.

Q23. Whether leave encashment should be revised on retrospective revision of pay/D.A?
In terms of 38-A of CCS (Leave) Rules, encashment of EL alongwith LTC is to be calculated on pay admissible on the date of availing LTC and DA admissible on that date. If pay or DA admissible has been revised with retrospective effect, going by the rule the Govt. servant would be entitled to encashment of Leave on the revised rates.

Q24. Whether Destination can be changed during LTC?
It depends upon the certain condition, where change of destination is permissible as per LTC Rule.

Q25. Whether Date of journey is important during LTC?
YES, Date of journey decide the LTC Block Year and kind of Leave availed, period of journey by employee.

Q26. Whether age of the children effects the LTC Claim?
YES, the age of the children effects the family claim eligibility.

Q27. Whether Break/ Interruption is allowed during LTC Journey?
Break/ Interruption depends upon the certain conditions as per LTC Rule/ Provision.

Q28. Whether Govt. servant who has already availed one Home Town LTC in the current block can avail LTC to visit NER?
Yes, against All India LTC.

Q29. Whether a Govt. servant avail the benefit of visiting NER/J&K twice in particular block of 4 years?
Yes, a Govt. servant can visit NER/J&K by conversion of his Home Town LTC and also by availing All India LTC subject to validity period of the scheme and fulfilling of other conditions.

Q30. What is the dependency criterion for the purpose of LTC?
A member of family whose income from all sources, including pension, temporary increase in pension does not exceed minimum pension (presently Rs. 9000/- per month as per 7th CPC) and Dearness relief (DR) thereon, is deemed to be wholly dependent on the Government employee. Condition of dependency is not applicable to spouse of the government employee. (O.M. No. 31011/4/2008-Estt. (A) dated 23.09.2008)

Q31. Whether Carry forward of LTC is allowed to fresh recruits?
NO, as fresh recruits are eligible for every year LTC for the first 8 years of service.

Q32. Whether parents/children residing at other places avail LTC to visit the Govt. servant at Headquarters and go back?
No, reimbursement of LTC claims being restricted to the entitlement for journey between Headquarters and place of visit. (O.M. No. 31011/14/86-Estt. (A) dated 07.05.1987)

Q33. Whether leave encashment is allowed in case the Government employee does not avail of LTC in a particular block year but his family member(s) avail of LTC?
Yes. Government employee may apply for leave encashment before the commencement of the journey in respect of his/her family member in a particular sub-block/block year. Employees are entitled for one leave encashment only in a particular Block/ Sub-Block year, as the case may be. [O.M. No. 14028/2/2012-Estt. (L), dated 09.02.2015]

Q34. Whether reimbursement is allowed in case the journey is performed by private ferry after utilizing the authorized mode of transportation?
In cases where last part of the journey from the nearest airport/railway station is performed by private ferry, reimbursement may be restricted to the entitled Government ship fare from the nearest airport/station to the declared place of destination provided public transport/Government ferry is available in that particular area. [OM No. 31011/3/2015-Estt. (A.IV) dated 09.02.2017]

Q35. Whether Government employees can travel by Helicopter?
No, Government employees can travel only in economy (Y) Class in a passenger plane of airlines.

Q36. Whether a Government employees visiting Sikkim can travel by air up to Bagdogra (West Bengal) which is not situated in NER.
Yes. A Government employee is entitled to travel by air from their place of posting (or nearest airport) to a city in the NER (or nearest airport). [OM No. 31011/4/2007-Estt.A-IV dated 14.05.2008

Q37. Are incidental expenses and expenditure incurred on local journeys allowed under LTC?
No, Reimbursement under LTC scheme does not cover incidental expenses and expenditure incurred on local journeys. Reimbursement for expenses of journey is allowed only on the basis of a point to point journey on a through ticket over the shortest direct route. (Rule 13 of CCS(LTC) Rules, 1988)

Q38. What will be the rates for the place where no specific rates have been prescribed either by the Directorate of Transport of the concerned State or of the neighboring States?
(I) For journeys performed in own car/taxi- Rs.24/- Per KM (II) For Journeys performed by auto rickshaw/ Own Scooter etc.- Rs. 12/- Per KM [OM 19030/1/2017-E IV Date 13.07.2027 13.07.2017]

Q39. What are the key documents required in complete TA/DA Claims?
The following documents are required with TA/DA Claims: Movement Order, Relieving Order/ Attendance Certificate, Accommodation bill (wherever applicable), Self-Declaration for claims (Hotel & Local Conveyance within the city), Tickets, Boarding pass, vouchers, etc., as per rules.

Q40. What is the timing restrictions for reimbursement of Food charge?
Timing restrictions: If absence from headquarter is <6 hours- 30% of Lump sum amount. If absence from headquarter is between 6-12 hours- 70% of Lump sum amount. If absence from headquarter is >12 hours-100% of Lump sum amount. Note: Absence from Head Quarter will be reckoned from midnight to midnight and will be calculated on a per day basis. [OM 19030/1/2017−E19030/1/2017-E19030/1/2017−E IV Date 13.07.2027 13.07.2017]

Q41. Whether submission of Boarding Pass is compulsory along with TA Bill?
As per the Ministry of Finance, Department of Expenditure, O.M. No. 19030/3/2014-E.IV dated 23.06.2020, if a Government employee is unable to submit the boarding pass along with the TA bill, a Self-Declaration Certificate in lieu of the boarding pass may be submitted. The certificate should confirm that the employee actually performed the onward and return journeys and provide the relevant ticket/PNR details. The certificate must be countersigned by the Controlling Officer in the case of officials below the rank of Under Secretary (Pay Level 10 and below). However, this OM specifically relates to Travelling Allowance (TA) claims; its applicability to LTC claims should be verified with reference to the relevant LTC instructions.

Q42. Whether Tatkal Seva charge is reimbursable?
YES

Q43. Whether LTC journey is allowed on tour packages offered by various travel agents
Travel on tour packages is not allowed. However, the tours conducted by Indian Tourism Development Corporation (ITDC), State Tourism Development Corporation (STDC) and Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC) can be considered and only the fare component for a fixed destination by the shortest route shall be reimbursable provided ITDC/STDC/IRCTC separately indicate the fare component and certify that the journey was actually performed by the Government employee and his family members for which he/she is claiming the Leave Travel Concession. [O.M. No. 31011/6/2002-Estt. (A), dated 30.07.2002 and 26.3.2008]

Q44. Is the taxi/ auto fare from the residence of the Government service/ other eligible family member to the nearest airport/ railway station/bus depot during the outward/ inward journey, is reimbursable
No. Local travel cost is not covered under LTC Rules (Rule 13 of CCS(LTC) Rules, 1988)

Q45. Is concessional circular trip tickets offered by the Railways allowed?
There is no objection to a Government employee or his family members availing themselves of concessional circular trip tickets offered by the Railways in conjunction with the leave travel concessions. In such cases also, the official will be entitled to reimbursement of the fare for the entitled/lower class actually travelled by the shortest route. [O.M. No.31011/277/-Ests. (A), dated 03.02.1979

Q46. Are catering charges allowed while booking train tickets?
Wherever employees opt for catering services while booking the tickets for the eligible trains for the purpose of LTC, the reimbursement of catering charges shall be allowed. [Ο.Μ. 3101117//2023-Estt.A-IV dated 10.08.2023]

Q47. Is the element of Tax, Education Cess and other levies being charged by Government on travel by Air/Road /Rail/Steamer on LTC reimbursable?
Yes. [M.F., O.M. F. No. 190231//2006-E. IV, dated 18.07.2007]

Q48. Who is the competent authority to grant relaxation in case air ticket is booked from unauthorized travel agent or website of the airlines?
(a) The Financial Advisors of the Ministry/Department in case the Government employee is working directly under Ministry/Department; and (b) In case of the employees working under subordinate/attached offices, Head of Department not below the rank of Joint Secretary. [O.M. No. 31011/12/2022-Estt. (A-IV), dated 29.08.2022]

Q49. State whether the DA entitled of hotel accommodation/guest house is inclusive of taxes/GST.
No. The entitlement prescribed is exclusive of all Taxes/GST. Taxes incurred will be reimbursed over and above the prescribed entitlement.

Q50. What is the condition for grant of one-third of Composite Transfer Grant in cases of transfer within the same city?
There should actually be a change of residence.

Q51. Can a printout or confirmation copy of the ticket be submitted as supporting document for an TA or LTC claim?
No. The employee should submit the actual travel tickets as supporting documents along with the TA or LTC claim. A mere ticket confirmation printout should not be treated as a substitute for the actual tickets, wherever submission of the tickets is required under the applicable LTC rules and instructions.

Q52. Does the provision of a working lunch during official duty automatically require deduction of Daily Allowance?
A working lunch is not equated with TA/DA rules.

Q53. Can a TTA claim be rejected merely because the employee paid the expenses in cash?
The claim should be examined with reference to the applicable TTA rules, admissibility of the expense, proof of Goods transportation and supporting documents. However, applicable rules for reimbursement requires documentary evidence(s) and if the employee fails to provide it, the unsupported component may be disallowed.

Q54. How should TA/DA claims be regulated for teachers escorting students to events?
When teachers accompany students to participate in sports, cultural or other official events, the TA/DA claims of the accompanying teachers should be submitted separately from the students’ reimbursement claims. The teachers’ admissible travelling and daily allowances should be regulated under the applicable TA/DA Rules and reimbursed from the School Fund account, subject to the prescribed limits and sanction.
