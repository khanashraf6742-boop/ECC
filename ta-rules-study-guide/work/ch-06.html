ch06-004

Very High-YieldTime limits — general vs retirement vs death-in-service

O.M.Exception
Source L1416–1429
Exact-Source Fact Card

Ordinary tour/transfer/training TA claims must be submitted within 60 days succeeding the applicable trigger under GFR Rule 290 and DoE O.M. 13.03.2018, whether or not an advance was drawn; para 2 sets separate triggers for relevant family journeys and delivered personal effects/conveyances. TA on retirement has a distinct 180-day window under DoE O.M. 15.06.2021. These are claim-submission limits, not advance-adjustment deadlines. Where a Government servant dies in service, SR 148 separately addresses a family TA advance; do not import the ordinary tour-advance rules.

ch06-005

Very High-YieldRetirement TA Exclusions & Station Boundaries (SR 147, SR 148)

ExceptionExplanation
Source L1431–1453
Exact-Source Fact Card

Under Swamy's Handbook-2024 and GIOs below SR 147 and SR 148, Travelling Allowance on Retirement is governed by strict boundary and eligibility rules:

  • 1. Strict Forfeiture on Resignation, Dismissal, or Removal:
    The retirement TA concession is admissible only to employees who retire on retiring pension, superannuation, invalid, or compensation pension.
    The Restriction: It is strictly NOT admissible to employees who quit service by resignation, or who are dismissed or removed from service as a disciplinary penalty.
  • 2. Last Station of Duty Boundary Definition:
    For the purpose of determining "same-station" or "change-of-residence" boundaries on transfer or retirement, the "last station of duty" strictly means and includes all areas falling within the Urban Agglomeration (UA) of that station. Moving within the UA without a change of residence does not attract CTG.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Retirement TA kis-kis ko milega aur kis-kis ko nahi? Ye bohot clearly defined hai — aur exams mein bohot test hota hai:
  • KO MILEGA (Eligible): (1) Superannuation pe retire hone wale (normal retirement at 60 years), (2) Retiring pension pe retire hone wale (voluntary retirement after qualifying service), (3) Invalid pension pe retire hone wale (medical grounds pe), (4) Compensation pension pe retire hone wale (post abolition etc.). Basically agar aapko pension mil rahi hai, to retirement TA bhi milega.
  • NAHI MILEGA (Not Eligible — Strictly): (1) Resignation (istifa) dene wale — chahe 20 saal service ho, agar resignation diya to retirement TA nahi milega. Reason: Resignation voluntary hai — aap khud chhod rahe ho, sarkar nahi nikaal rahi. (2) Dismissal ya Removal — disciplinary action se naukri se nikalne wale. (3) Temporary employees with <3 years service — agar temporary ho aur 3 saal se kam service hai, to nahi milega.
  • Station Boundary — Urban Agglomeration (UA): "Last station of duty" ka matlab sirf office ka building nahi — poori Urban Agglomeration shamil hai. Example: Agar aapka office Delhi ke North Block mein hai, to "last station" mein poora Delhi UA aata hai — Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad sab shamil hain. Agar aap retirement ke baad North Block se Gurugram shift hote ho (same UA mein), to CTG tab tak nahi milega jab tak actual ghar na badla ho.
  • Real-life scenario: Ek officer 30 saal service ke baad resign de deta hai (kisi private company mein jaane ke liye). Kya usko retirement TA milega? → Nahi! Resignation = no retirement TA, even with 30 years of service. Agar wahi officer superannuation pe retire hota (60 years complete karke), to full TA milta. Ye distinction bohot important hai.
Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Retirement TA only for retiring pension, superannuation, invalid or compensation pension — NOT resignation, dismissal or removal. “Last station of duty” includes the whole Urban Agglomeration; moving within the UA without change of residence attracts no CTG.

Revision of TA Bills — The 3-Year Limit (GFR Procedural Rule)

After a TA bill has been passed and payment made, it may still be revised under certain circumstances:

• Grounds for Revision: A passed TA bill can be revised for (a) arithmetic errors discovered post-payment, (b) subsequent increase in DA that retrospectively affects the rates applicable at the time of the journey, or (c) supplementary claims that were inadvertently omitted from the original bill.

• Time Limit: Under GFR provisions, a revised/supplementary TA claim can be processed within three (3) years from the date of the original claim. Beyond 3 years, no revision or supplementary claim is entertained.

• Supplementary Claim: An official who has already drawn TA on a bill may draw a supplementary claim even after the original bill has been passed — for example, if a DA rate revision occurred after the original bill was settled, or if a component was missed in the original claim.

Exam Trap: "A TA bill passed in March 2023 can be revised in June 2026 for a DA enhancement." → Yes, because 3 years and 3 months have passed, this would be time-barred. But if the revision is sought in February 2026 (within 3 years), it is admissible.

TA for Pensioners / Retired Employees Called Back for Duty

When a retired Government employee (pensioner) is summoned by a Central Government office — not for re-employment, but for specific purposes like:

• Testifying in a departmental inquiry or vigilance case,
• Appearing before a Medical Board for disability reassessment,
• Providing evidence or deposition in a court case related to their official service,

they are entitled to TA and DA as per their original pay level at the time of retirement for the duration of the visit. The travel fare (by the class they were entitled to at retirement) and Daily Allowance for the journey and halt days are reimbursable.

Important: This entitlement is distinct from re-employment (covered under O.M. 24.01.2022). A pensioner called for a one-off inquiry is not "re-employed" — they are summoned as a witness/deponent and are reimbursed accordingly.

Note: Pensioners do NOT get LTC (Leave Travel Concession) — LTC is a benefit for serving employees only.

Exam Trap: "A retired Level 11 officer is called to depose in a CBI inquiry. What TA do they get?" → Answer: TA/DA as per Level 11 (their retirement pay level), not as per any re-employment scale.

Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Pensioner summoned (inquiry, medical board, court deposition) — not re-employment: TA/DA at the pay level at retirement; no LTC for pensioners.
