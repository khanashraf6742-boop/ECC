pack-004

2. 5-Step TA Audit & Claim Settlement Flowchart

Source L4099–4107
  1. Sanction: Confirm the tour/transfer order issued by the Controlling Officer.
  2. Entitlement: Check Pay Level, mode entitlement and (if air) the applicable booking channel.
  3. DA and hotel: Apply the absence band (30%/70%/100%) and check hotel, food and local-travel documentation separately.
  4. Transfer/retirement: Check CTG conditions and personal-effects weight/rate separately.
  5. Settlement: Apply the 60-day / 180-day claim-submission window; verify any advance-adjustment deadline from a separate current authority.

3. Common TA Audit Objections & How to Reply

Proviso
Source L4108–4114
  • Objection: Level 7 officer flew on official tour. Reply: Valid! DoE OM 13.07.2017 entitles Level 6–8 to Economy Air on TA (restriction is only on LTC).
  • Objection: Transfer TA submitted after 75 days. Reply: It is outside the ordinary 60-day window. Do not promise routine HOD/FA condonation; check the applicable DoE O.M. and any valid specific relaxation. The 13.03.2018 O.M. para 5 addresses claims kept in abeyance for more than one year after HOD investigation; it is not a general late-claim extension.
  • Objection: Same-station transfer CTG claimed at 80%. Reply: Restricted to 1/3rd of CTG only if actual change of residence is proved.

4. TA Numerical & File-Noting Lab

Source L4115–4120

Numerical: Basic Pay ₹60,000, Transfer >20 km (mainland) → CTG = 80% × ₹60,000 = ₹48,000. If same-station with residence change → 1/3rd × ₹48,000 = ₹16,000.
Model Note: "Shri [Name], Level 7, performed official tour from [Date] to [Date] (absence 14 hrs). Claim submitted within 60 days with hotel/food vouchers. Passed for ₹[Amount] under DoE OM 13.07.2017."
