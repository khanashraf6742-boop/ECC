2.1 SR 30 / 31 — Route for Mileage Allowance

SR Secondary summary (not primary text)
SR 30 For calculating mileage allowance, a journey between two places is held to have been performed by the shortest of two or more practicable routes or by the cheapest of such routes as may be equally short; provided that, when there are alternative Railway routes and the difference between them in point of time and cost is not great, mileage allowance should be calculated on the route actually used 2. A secondary summary in the same terms states the journey is held to have been performed by the shortest of two or more practicable routes 5.
SR 31 A competent authority may, for special reasons which should be recorded, permit mileage allowance to be calculated on a route other than the shortest or cheapest, provided the journey is actually performed by such route 2.

Caveat: Secondary summary only. Primary text of SR 30/31 not verified in this compilation.

2.6 SR 71–73 — Local Journeys / 8 km Rule

SR Secondary summary
SR 71 Daily allowance may not be drawn for any day on which a Government servant does not reach a point outside a radius of eight kilometers from the duty point (i.e. the place/office of employment) at his headquarters or return to it from a similar point 2. The same wording is reproduced in another secondary summary 5.
SR 71 — Note The term “radius of eight kilometers” should be interpreted as meaning a distance of eight kilometers by the shortest practicable route by which a traveller can reach his destination by the ordinary modes of travelling 2.
SR 71(2) — Local journey TA for a local journey shall be admissible if the temporary place of duty is beyond 8 km from the normal place of duty, irrespective of whether the journey is performed by the Government servant from his residence or from the normal place of duty 2. The same position is stated in a secondary discussion of SR 71(2) 1.
SR 71 — 7th CPC clarification After the 7th CPC, the Finance Ministry clarified that Daily Allowance on tour comprises 3 components — hotel accommodation, travel within the city and food charges — and that for local journey beyond 8 kms, the prescribed components may be admissible 2.
SR 71 — Local journey practice For journeys beyond 8 km within the limits of the Urban Agglomeration/Municipality or contiguous Municipality etc. in which the headquarters is located, the journey should normally be performed in the same way as the journey to the duty point, i.e. by bus, local trains or own conveyance 3.
SR 71 — G.I.O.(4) Daily allowance on local journeys admissible up to 180 days 3.
SR 72–73 (as reproduced in secondary source) (iv) If a Government servant combines a Railway journey with a journey by steamer or road, he may, if he travels to a place distant at least eight kilometers from the point where he leaves the Railway or returns to the Railway from a place similarly distant, draw mileage allowance for the journey by steamer or road, in addition to daily allowance, if any, admissible under this rule; provided the time spent on the steamer/road journey is deducted in calculating absence from headquarters 2. (iii) If the court in which he gives evidence is situated within eight kilometers of his headquarters and no TA is therefore admissible, he may, if he is not in receipt of permanent travelling allowance, accept such payment of actual travelling expenses as the court may make 2.

Caveat: All above are secondary summaries. The at-or-below-8-km position is unresolved — see Section 4 below.

2.7 SR 107 — TA on Re-appointment

Item Secondary summary
SR 107 When a pensioner, or a Government servant who has been thrown out of employment owing to a reduction of establishment or the abolition of his post, is reappointed to Government service, the authority which sanctions his re-appointment may in cases other than those covered by Rule 105–A permit him to draw travelling allowance for so much of his journey to join his new post as falls within India 2. The same wording is reproduced in another secondary summary 5.

Caveat: Secondary summary only. Primary text not verified.

2.11 SR 164 — TA on Training

Item Secondary summary
SR 164 When a Government servant or student not already in Government service is selected to undergo a course of training, a competent authority may decide the scale, if any, on which he shall draw: (a) Travelling allowance for the original journey to and the last journey from the place of training, and for halts at such place; (b) in the case of training at a school, college or similar institution, travelling allowance for similar journeys on the occasion of holidays and vacations; and (c) travelling allowance for journeys during the course of training. Provided that the scale so fixed shall not exceed that admissible to Government servants of similar status on duty at the place of training 5.
SR 164-A A Military officer in civil employ, while detailed to attend a military course of instruction, is entitled to draw mileage and daily allowance at rates admissible to a military officer in military employ in similar circumstances 5.

Caveat: Secondary summary only. The source itself holds the training-TA position as unresolved — see Section 3 below.

3. Training TA under SR 164 — Unresolved

  • Position: The source itself holds the training-TA position under SR 164 as unresolved, and the compiler has no primary text.
  • Secondary discussion (not a rule): A secondary discussion notes the interaction between SR 71(2) (local journey beyond 8 km) and SR 164, and records differing views on whether TA/DA is admissible for training at a place beyond 8 km from the duty point, including for fresh entrants sent for training at the headquarters station 1.
  • Action required: Obtain the primary text of SR 164 and any DoE clarification on training TA before treating any position as settled.

4. Local-Journey Rules (SR 71) — At-or-Below-8-km Position Unresolved

  • What is summarised: SR 71 bars DA for any day on which the Government servant does not reach a point outside a radius of eight kilometers from the duty point, with “radius” interpreted as distance by the shortest practicable route 2. SR 71(2) allows TA for a local journey if the temporary place of duty is beyond 8 km from the normal place of duty 2.
  • What is unresolved: The at-or-below-8-km position — i.e. the treatment of journeys at or below the 8 km threshold, including the interaction with the duty-point shift for temporary duty — is unresolved in this compilation.
  • Action required: Verify against the primary text of SR 71–73 and the latest DoE clarification on local journeys.

6. Joining Time and TTA — Rule 4(4) as Substituted in 2015 Unverified

  • Position: Rule 4(4) of the Central Civil Services (Joining Time) Rules, 1979, as substituted in 2015, is unverified, because G.S.R. 229(E) dated 27th March, 2015 was unreachable during compilation.
  • Secondary summaries (not primary text):
  • The Joining Time Rules, 1979 were notified vide GSR 695 dated 8th May, 1979, amended vide GSR 197 dated 10th March, 1989 and again vide GSR 229(E) dated 27th March, 2015 2.
  • A Government servant on joining time is regarded as on duty and entitled to joining time pay equal to the pay drawn before relinquishment of charge in the old post, plus DA if any appropriate to the joining time pay, and compensatory allowances / HRA as applicable to the old station; Conveyance Allowance or permanent travelling allowance is not allowed 2.
  • For appointment to posts under the Central Government on the basis of a competitive examination and/or interview open to Government servants and others, Central Government employees and permanent/provisionally permanent State Government employees are entitled to Transfer Travelling Allowance (TTA); temporary Central Government employees with less than 3 years of regular continuous service are not entitled to TTA, as they are not entitled to joining time pay under the Joining Time Rules 2.
  • The same TTA position is recorded as “[Rule 4 and Notification dated 27th March, 2015]” in another secondary summary 4.
  • A secondary summary of joining-time entitlements records joining time pay, DA, compensatory allowance/HRA as applicable to the old station, and that Conveyance Allowance or Permanent Travelling Allowance is not allowed 5.
  • Action required: Obtain G.S.R. 229(E) dated 27.03.2015 and verify Rule 4(4) as substituted before citing the TTA position.
