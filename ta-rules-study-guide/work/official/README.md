# Official evidence (downloaded 26 Nov 2026)

PDFs downloaded from the Government hosts cited in the study guide (doe.gov.in, cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in, dgt.gov.in, nests.tribal.gov.in) and converted with markitdown 0.1.9 into `md/` (text-layer PDFs only; image-only scans have empty conversions and were read from page images).

`dl.txt` and `urls.txt` list the source URLs of the first batch; the other files were fetched from the doe.gov.in listings named in the guide's "Official verification" chapter (evidence register). Not retrieved: DoPT LTC FAQ, DoPT O.M. 29.08.2022, Joining Time Rules consolidation, G.S.R. 229(E).
