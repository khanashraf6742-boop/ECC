Sub:
Air travel on official tour where the cost of air passage is not borne by the Government of
lndia - clarification reg.
The undersigned is directed to refer this Department's O.M. dated 07.06.2016 and 26.07.2016
regarding delegation of powers to Financial Advisors (FAs) of the Ministries/Departments to accord
relaxation for travel by airlines other than Air lndia both domestic and international including individual
cases of oflicials of Central Govemment and aulonomous bodies. Several references have been received
in this Deparlment seeking clarification as to whether relaxation is required in case the cost of air passage
is not bome by the Government of lndia
2.
The matter has been examined in this Department and it is clarified that in case the cost of air
passage is not borne by the Government of lndia, relaxation is not required to be obtained from the
Financial Advisors of the mncerned Ministry/ Department.
3.
Further clariflcation, if any, in this regard may be handled by Financial Advisers of the concerned
Ministry/Department.
4.
