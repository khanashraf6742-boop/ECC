Subject: Booking of air tickets for LTC travel and timely submission of LTC
advance applications – reg.

  Attention of all oﬃcers and oﬃcials of the Department is invited to DoPT O.M. No.
instructions
31011/12/2022-Estt.A.IV  dated  29.08.2022  containing  consolidated
regarding booking of air tickets for journeys on Leave Travel Concession (LTC).

2.  As per Para 1(iv) of the aforesaid O.M., Government employees are encouraged
to book ﬂight tickets at least 21 days prior to the intended date of travel on LTC so as to
avail the most competitive fares and minimize the burden on the Government exchequer.

3.    Further,  under  the  provisions  relating  to  LTC  advance,  Government  employees
entitled to travel by air may apply for LTC advance at least 30 days before the intended
date of journey, along with a printout of the concerned webpage of the authorized travel
agency indicating suitable flight and fare details.

4.  It has been observed during scrutiny of LTC claims that in a number of cases air
tickets are being booked only a few days before the journey and, in some cases, during
the same week in which the journey is undertaken. Such last-minute bookings generally
result  in  higher  air  fares  and  are  not  in  consonance  with  the  spirit  of  the  instructions
issued by DoPT vide O.M. No. 31011/12/2022-Estt.A.IV dated 29.08.2022.

5.  All oﬃcers and oﬃcials are, therefore, advised to plan their LTC journeys well in
advance  and  book  air  tickets,  as  far  as  possible,  at  least  21  days  before  the  proposed
date  of  travel.  Oﬃcers/oﬃcials  intending  to  avail  LTC  advance  may  submit  their
applications  at  least  30  days  before  the  intended  date  of  journey  in  compliance  of  the
extant instructions.

6.  The above extant instructions may be noted for strict compliance.
