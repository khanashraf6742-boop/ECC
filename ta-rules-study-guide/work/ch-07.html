Reference — status variesOM 1: DoE O.M. dated 04.09.2017 — Pay Level 13A Entitlement

O.M.Explanation
Source L1556–1576
Exact-Source Fact Card

Consequent upon the implementation of the 7th CPC, the newly introduced Pay Level 13A (carrying a pre-revised Grade Pay of ₹8,900, applicable to certain senior administrative grades like Directors/Directors-equivalent) was omitted from the baseline TA entitlements table of the 13.07.2017 O.M.

The Clarification: Department of Expenditure vide O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 04.09.2017 clarified that the TA/DA travel and Daily Allowance entitlements of officers in Pay Level 13A are identical in all respects to those in Pay Level 13.

  • Travel Entitlement: Economy Class by Air, AC-I Class by Train.
  • Hotel Ceilings: Up to ₹5,625 per day.
  • Food Lump Sum: Up to ₹1,250 per day.
  • Local Travel within City: AC Taxi up to 50 km per day.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Level 13A ka confusion kyun hua? 7th CPC ne Pay Matrix mein ek naya level introduce kiya — Level 13A (Grade Pay ₹8,900 wala, jo Director-level posts ke liye hai). Lekin jab 13 July 2017 ko base TA O.M. nikla, to uski entitlement table mein Level 13A ka naam galti se chhut gaya — table mein sirf 14+, 12-13, 9-11, 6-8, aur 5- ye 5 bands the. Officers confuse ho gaye: "Hum Level 13A wale hain, to hume kya milega — Level 12 jaisa ya Level 13 jaisa?"
  • 4 September 2017 ko clarification aaya: Department of Expenditure ne ek short O.M. nikaal ke clear kar diya: Level 13A = Level 13 (same entitlements in all respects). Matlab Level 13A wale officers ko wo sab facilities milti hain jo Level 13 ko milti hain: Flight mein Economy Class, Train mein AC-I, Hotel ceiling ₹5,625/day (current, DA≥50% enhanced), Food lump sum ₹1,250/day, Local travel AC taxi up to 50 km/day.
  • Exam mein kaise puchhte hain? Examiners often puchhte hain: "Level 13A ki TA entitlement kya hai?" — Options mein Level 12 jaisa, Level 14 jaisa, Level 11 jaisa — ye sab traps hain. Correct answer: "Same as Level 13" — per O.M. dated 04.09.2017. Ye date aur ye fact dono yaad rakhna zaroori hai.

    • Travel: Economy Class by Air, AC-I Class by Train.

ch07-008

Reference — status variesOM 3: DoE O.M. dated 01.02.2018 — Daily Allowance under Free Boarding and Lodging

O.M.ProvisoExceptionExplanation
Source L1616–1642
Exact-Source Fact Card

Consequent upon the restructured Daily Allowance (DA) components under the 7th CPC (Hotel, Food, Local Travel), Department of Expenditure vide O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 01.02.2018 clarified the admissibility of Daily Allowance when free boarding and/or lodging is provided by the Government or host organization:

  • Component-wise Zeroing Rule: Since DA is purely a reimbursement of actual expenses, if any component is provided free, that specific claim is zeroed out:
    • Free stay (Lodging) provided: Hotel stay component is reduced to NIL.
    • Free meals (Boarding) provided: Food bill component is reduced to NIL.
    • Both stay and meals provided free: Both Hotel and Food components are reduced to NIL. No Daily Allowance for stay/meals can be claimed.
  • The 25% Flat DA Abolition: Under 5th & 6th CPC, if both boarding and lodging were free, employees were paid a flat 25% of the single-rate DA for out-of-pocket expenses. This O.M. completely abolished the 25% flat DA rule.
  • Local Travel Admissibility: Even if stay and meals are free, the employee is still entitled to claim actual or capped local travelling charges under Para 2E(i) & (iii) for local journeys actually performed on official duty (e.g. from airport to the guest house).
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Free Boarding/Lodging pe kya hoga DA ka? (O.M. 01.02.2018): Ye bohot practical situation hai — jab aap kisi sarkari training ya conference mein jaate ho jahan rehna aur khana dono free hai (jaise LBSNAA Mussoorie ya any sarkari training institute).
  • Purana rule (6th CPC): Pehle agar stay aur food dono free the, to employee ko flat 25% DA milta tha "out-of-pocket expenses" ke naam pe. Matlab agar aapka DA ₹1,000/day tha, to ₹250 milta tha chahe aapka kuch kharcha nahi hua.
  • Naya rule (7th CPC, O.M. 01.02.2018): Ab ye 25% flat DA completely band ho gaya hai! Naya system component-wise hai: (1) Agar stay free hai → Hotel component = NIL (zero). (2) Agar food free hai → Food component = NIL (zero). (3) Agar local transport free hai (jaise official gaadi) → Travel component = NIL. (4) Agar dono stay aur food free hain → dono NIL — sirf local travel ka actual kharcha claim kar sakte ho (jaise airport se guest house tak ka auto).
  • Example: Level 9 officer training pe gaya jahan hostel + mess dono free hain. Uska normal DA hota: Hotel ₹2,812.50 + Food ₹1,125 + Travel ₹422.50 = ₹4,360/day. Ab kya milega? Hotel = NIL, Food = NIL, Travel = ₹422.50 (agar actually use kiya) = sirf ₹422.50/day. Purana 25% rule hota to ₹1,090 milta — ab kam milta hai!
RED-FLAG TRAP

Flag: "If both boarding and lodging are free, a 25% Daily Allowance is paid to the employee."
Fix: This is an outdated 6th CPC rule. Under the latest 01.02.2018 O.M., the 25% flat DA is completely discontinued. Hotel and Food components are reduced to NIL, leaving only local city travel admissible under Para 2E. Fix-phrase: "Free stay + food = Zero stay/food DA; only local travel."

Reference — status variesOM 4: DoE O.M. dated 13.03.2018 & 15.06.2021 — TA Claim Time Limits

O.M.ExceptionExplanation
Source L1643–1673
Exact-Source Fact Card

To align Travelling Allowance claims with the procedural discipline of the General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017, the submission timelines were drastically modified:

  • 1. Tour, Transfer, and Training claims (O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 13.03.2018):
    • The ordinary tour/transfer/training claim window is 60 days succeeding the applicable trigger, whether or not an advance was drawn. For the journey itself the trigger is completion; para 2 specifies separate triggers for relevant family journeys and actual delivery/arrival of personal effects or conveyances.
    • This includes road mileage and the transport of personal effects/conveyances (which must be submitted within 60 days of their actual delivery/arrival at the destination).
    • Relinquishment / delayed-claim handling: The O.M. says an unpreferred claim is forfeited/deemed relinquished at the 60-day limit. Para 5 separately says that a TA claim allowed to remain in abeyance for more than one year should be investigated by the Head of Department; if genuineness and valid reasons for delay are established, it may be paid after usual checks. This is a limited stated procedure—not automatic or general condonation for any late claim.
  • 2. TA on Retirement claims (O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 15.06.2021):
    • Recognizing the practical disruptions and settlement delays faced by retired employees and their families, the time limit for submitting Retirement TA claims was relaxed to 180 days (6 months) succeeding the date of completion of the journey.
    • This 180-day window applies only to retirement claims; ordinary tour, transfer and training claims remain due within 60 days of the applicable trigger, whether or not an advance was drawn.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Bill jama karne ki deadlines — 3 important numbers: Ye bohot practical aur commonly tested topic hai:
  • 1. Tour/Transfer/Training ka bill — 60 din (O.M. 13.03.2018): Deadline is 60 days succeeding completion of the journey, whether or not a TA advance was drawn. A late claim is treated as forfeited/deemed relinquished under the O.M.; para 5 is a specific HOD review route for a claim kept in abeyance for more than one year, not a blanket extension for any valid reason. Retirement TA has its separate 180-day order.
  • 2. Retirement TA ka bill — 180 din (O.M. 15.06.2021): Retired employees ke liye deadline 3 guna zyada — 180 din (6 mahine) hai. Reason: Retirement ke baad settlement mein time lagta hai — naya ghar dhundho, bank accounts transfer karo, family ko relocate karo. 15 June 2021 ko ye relaxation specifically retirement claims ke liye di gayi.
  • TA advance adjustment: The inspected 2005 Compendium states a 15-day tour-advance adjustment period (currency unverified); its Rule 43 transfer-advance text gives no general 30-day deadline. Check the current departmental order.
  • Comparison: The ordinary 60-day TA claim-submission rule applies whether or not an advance was drawn; it is distinct from advance adjustment. The inspected 2005 Compendium says 15 days for tour advances, but its currency is unverified and no general 30-day transfer deadline was located.

ch07-010

Reference — status variesOM 5: DoE O.M. dated 29.06.2018 — GST/Taxes on Hotel Entitlement

O.M.Explanation
Source L1674–1700
Exact-Source Fact Card

Following the introduction of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in July 2017, numerous references were made to the Ministry of Finance regarding whether hotel tax is inclusive of or exclusive of the pay-level-based hotel accommodation ceilings.

The Rule (DoE O.M. No. 19030/2/2017-E.IV dated 29.06.2018):

  • GST is Exclusive: The hotel staying accommodation ceilings prescribed under the revised TA Rules (Para 2E(i) of the 13.07.2017 O.M.) are strictly exclusive of all taxes and GST.
  • Reimbursement Over and Above the Ceiling: The GST/taxes levied by hotels shall be reimbursed to the Government servant over and above the prescribed ceiling/entitlement!
  • The Proportionate Restriction: The reimbursement of GST/taxes is calculated only on the actual room tariff paid within their prescribed ceiling.
    • Case A (Within Ceiling): A PML 9 officer (entitled up to ₹2,812.50/day) stays in a room costing ₹2,500/night with ₹450 GST. Since ₹2,500 is within the ceiling, they get reimbursed the full ₹2,500 + ₹450 GST = ₹2,950.
    • Case B (Exceeding Ceiling): The same officer stays in a room costing ₹3,500/night with ₹630 GST. Their room rent is restricted to the ceiling of ₹2,812.50, and their GST reimbursement is restricted to the GST rate calculated on ₹2,812.50 (e.g. ₹506.25 GST), resulting in ₹2,812.50 + ₹506.25 = ₹3,318.75. The remaining excess tariff and excess GST must be borne personally.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Hotel pe GST ka paisa alag se milta hai! (O.M. 29.06.2018): Ye bohot employee-friendly rule hai. Hotel ke room rent ke upar jo GST (Goods and Services Tax) lagta hai (usually 12% ya 18%), wo aapki hotel ceiling ke alag se (over and above) reimburse hota hai.
  • Case A — Room rent ceiling ke andar: Level 9 officer (ceiling ₹2,812.50) ne ₹2,500 ka room liya + ₹450 GST. Total bill = ₹2,950. Kya milega? Poora ₹2,950 milega! Room rent (₹2,500) ceiling ke andar hai, to GST (₹450) bhi poora milta hai — ceiling se upar jaake bhi!
  • Case B — Room rent ceiling se upar: Wahi Level 9 officer ne ₹3,500 ka room liya + ₹630 GST. Total bill = ₹4,130. Kya milega? Room rent ceiling pe restrict hoga: ₹2,812.50. GST bhi sirf ceiling pe calculate hoga: 18% of ₹2,812.50 = ₹506.25. Total = ₹2,812.50 + ₹506.25 = ₹3,318.75. Baaki ₹811.25 aapko apni jeb se dena hoga.
  • Key insight: Ceilings strictly exclusive of GST hain — ye exam ka favourite calculation trap hai. Jab question mein hotel rent + GST diya ho, to pehle dekho rent ceiling ke andar hai ya nahi — phir GST accordingly calculate karo.
Exam Angle / PYQ

A high-yield MCQ calculation trap! "A Level 9 officer stays in a hotel costing ₹2,500/night + ₹450 GST. Their entitlement ceiling is ₹2,812.50. How much is reimbursed?" — many candidates restrict the total to the ₹2,812.50 ceiling. The correct answer is ₹2,950 (₹2,500 actual rent + ₹450 GST), because GST is paid over and above the ceiling when the room tariff is within the limit.

Reference — status variesTA & Pay Responsibilities during Deputation, Foreign Service, and Joining Time under CCS Rules

ExceptionExplanation
Source L1902–1941
Exact-Source Fact Card

Under the Central Civil Services (CCS) rules — specifically the CCS (Joining Time) Rules, 1979 (Rule 7), standard DoPT Deputation Guidelines (OM No. 6/8/2009-Estt.(Pay-II)), and Supplementary Rules (SR 206) — the financial responsibility for Travelling Allowance (TA), transit pay, and joining time pay is regulated with absolute procedural clarity:

  • 1. During Deputation (Within Central Government / PSUs / Autonomous Bodies):
    • Onward Journey (Joining Deputation): The Transfer TA (including fares, CTG, and transportation of personal effects/conveyance) is borne and paid entirely by the borrowing department/organization.
    • Return Journey (Reversion to Parent Cadre): Upon repatriation/reversion, the return Transfer TA is also borne and paid entirely by the borrowing department/organization (the organization they are leaving).
    • On-Tour TA/DA: All Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance for official tours performed during the deputation tenure are borne and sanctioned by the borrowing department/organization.
    • The Absorption Exception: If the employee is permanently absorbed into the borrowing organization, NO return TA is payable, as no repatriation journey takes place.
  • 2. During Foreign Service (Transfer to State Govts / International Bodies / PSUs on Foreign Terms):
    • Onward & Return Journeys: Under SR 206, both the onward Transfer TA (to join) and the return Transfer TA (on reversion) are borne and paid entirely by the foreign employer.
    • On-Tour TA/DA: All TA/DA for official tours performed during the foreign service period are paid entirely by the foreign employer, governed by the foreign employer's rules (or parent-cadre rules if specified in the terms of deputation).
  • 3. Joining Time Pay & Salary Responsibility (Rule 7, CCS (Joining Time) Rules, 1979):
    • Core Mandate: A Government servant on joining time is regarded as on duty and is entitled to Joining Time Pay (equal to the pay drawn in the old post before relinquishing charge, along with DA, HRA, and CCA appropriate to the old station).
    • Who Pays for Joining Time?: Under Rule 7, the joining time pay and allowances shall be paid for strictly by the new administrative authority (the borrowing department / new organization) where the Government servant joins on transfer.
    • On Reversion: Upon repatriation/reversion back to the parent cadre, the borrowing organization (the department being left) bears the joining time pay, allowances, and transit pay for the return journey.
Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • Deputation pe TA kaun deta hai? Simple rule: "The Guest Never Pays!"
  • Deputation (within Central Govt/PSUs): Jab aap apne parent department se kisi dusre department/PSU/autonomous body mein deputation pe jaate ho, to aane-jaane ka poora Transfer TA borrowing department pay karega — dono directions mein! Joining ka bhi, reversion (wapas aane) ka bhi. Aur deputation ke dauran jo bhi official tours hain — unka TA/DA bhi borrowing department dega.
  • Foreign Service (State Govt/International bodies): SR 206 ke under, foreign employer aane-jaane ka poora TA pay karega. Example: Agar aap World Bank ya UN mein deputation pe ja rahe ho, to World Bank/UN aapka TA dega.
  • Absorption exception: Agar aap deputation ke dauran borrowing department mein permanently absorb ho jaate ho, to return TA nahi milega — kyunki aap wapas ja hi nahi rahe! Aap ab permanently wahin ke ho gaye.
  • Joining Time Pay (Rule 7, CCS Joining Time Rules 1979): Transfer ke dauran jo joining time milta hai (travel days + joining days), uska pay naya administrative office dega — purana nahi. Joining Time Pay = purane post ki pay + DA + HRA. Reversion pe bhi same rule: joining time pay borrowing office dega.
Memory Hook

The correct answer is (b) New office / new administrative authority. Another tested point is reversion return TA: it is always borne by the borrowing department, NOT the parent cadre.

Study aid · Practice MCQs (written for this guide; answers cite the source card)
Q1. Hotel tariff ₹2,500 + ₹450 GST; Level 9 ceiling ₹2,812.50. Reimbursement per O.M. 29.06.2018 (as illustrated)?
  1. ₹2,812.50
  2. ₹2,500
  3. ₹2,950
  4. ₹3,318.75

Answer: (c) ₹2,950 — Tariff within ceiling → rent + full GST = ₹2,950. Rule citation: ch07-010 (L1674–1700)

Q2. Under O.M. 01.02.2018, if both boarding and lodging are free:
  1. 25% flat DA is paid
  2. Hotel and Food components are NIL; local travel under Para 2E(i)/(iii) remains admissible
  3. Full DA is paid
  4. Only food is paid

Answer: (b) Hotel and Food components are NIL; local travel under Para 2E(i)/(iii) remains admissible — The 25% flat DA is discontinued. Rule citation: ch07-008 (L1616–1642)

Q3. O.M. 22.12.2020 extends the no-voucher/self-certification route for local travel to:
  1. Levels 12 and above
  2. Levels 9–11
  3. Level 5 and below only
  4. All levels

Answer: (b) Levels 9–11 — “removes the receipt/voucher condition for Pay Levels 9–11”. Rule citation: ch07-012 (L1720–1739)

Q4. Retirement CTG after O.M. 06.01.2022, settling at the last station with actual change of residence?
  1. One-third
  2. Nil
  3. Full 80% of last month’s basic pay
  4. 100%

Answer: (c) Full 80% of last month’s basic pay — 20-km restriction abolished; full 80% if actual change of residence; else nil. Rule citation: ch07-013 (L1740–1761)

Q5. How many Authorized Travel Agents are specified for Government-funded air travel (31.12.2021)?
  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Three
  4. Five

Answer: (c) Three — Balmer Lawrie, Ashok Travels & Tours, IRCTC. Rule citation: ch07-017 (L1842–1866)

Q6. Per the guide, who pays joining time pay under Rule 7, CCS (Joining Time) Rules?
  1. Old office
  2. New administrative authority
  3. Shared equally
  4. Government servant

Answer: (b) New administrative authority — “paid for strictly by the new administrative authority”. Rule citation: ch07-019 (L1902–1941)
