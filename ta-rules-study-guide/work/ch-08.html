ch08-005

Very High-YieldThe trigger mechanism — why rates rose automatically

O.M.OrderExplanation
Source L1965–1982
Exact-Source Fact Card

The 7th CPC's TA Rules (O.M. 13.07.2017) carry a standing instruction: rates of Mileage Allowance and all Daily Allowance components (hotel, travelling, food) will increase by 25% each time Dearness Allowance rises by 50 percentage points. Dearness Allowance crossed 50% effective 01.01.2024 (confirmed vide DoE, MoF O.M. No.1/1/2024-E.II(B) dated 12.03.2024). Per the precedent set for HRA on 04.08.2021 — where the Department of Expenditure clarified that "this Department's O.M...is very clear and no separate order is being issued" — no fresh dedicated O.M. was necessary to activate the TA/Daily Allowance rate revision either; it took effect automatically by operation of the 2017 O.M.'s own clause. As of 22.11.2026, Dearness Allowance stands at 63% effective 01.07.2026 (DoE O.M. No. 1/2(i)/2026-E.II(B), dated 29.10.2026) — below the next stated 100% trigger. The rate table below reflects the 25% enhancement triggered when Dearness Allowance crossed 50%; verify each underlying rate against the latest applicable order before use.

Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • DA 50% cross hua → Rates automatically 25% badh gaye! Kaise?
  • 2017 ke O.M. mein hi likha tha: Jab 13 July 2017 ko TA Rules ka master O.M. nikla, to usme ek standing clause tha: "Jab bhi DA 50 percentage points badhega (i.e., 50% cross karega, phir 100% cross karega), to Mileage Allowance aur teeno DA components (Hotel/Travel/Food) automatically 25% badh jayenge — koi naya O.M. nikalne ki zaroorat nahi."
  • 1 January 2024 ko trigger hua: DA 50% ho gaya → usi din se saari rates 25% badh gayi. Example: Own car mileage pehle ₹24/km tha → ab ₹30/km. Hotel ceiling Level 9-11 pehle ₹2,250 thi → ab ₹2,812.50. Food Level 9-11 pehle ₹900 tha → ab ₹1,125.
  • Koi naya O.M. nahi nikla! Ye sabse important point hai. Jaise HRA 2021 mein automatically revise hua tha jab DA 25% cross kiya — same logic yahan apply hui. Sarkar ne kaha "humara O.M. clear hai, alag se order nahi chahiye." Exam mein ye bohot puchha jaata hai: "Was a separate O.M. required?" → Answer: NO!
  • Current status (updated 22 November 2026): DA 63% hai, effective 01.07.2026. The previously stated 60% / 01.01.2026 figure was stale. Check current orders; the 25%-enhanced rates in this chapter trace to the earlier 50% trigger.
Memory Hook

"DA 50 → rates +25%, automatically — no separate order, just like HRA in 2021."

Exam Angle / PYQ

Very high-probability question: "Was a separate O.M. required to enhance TA/DA rates when DA crossed 50%?" — Answer: No. This is the single most-tested nuance in this chapter; examiners try to trick candidates into thinking a specific 2024 TA O.M. exists (it doesn't — the mechanism is self-executing).

Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Standing clause of O.M. 13.07.2017: mileage and all three DA components rise 25% each time DA rises 50 points. DA crossed 50% on 01.01.2024 (O.M. 1/1/2024-E.II(B), 12.03.2024). No fresh dedicated TA O.M. needed (HRA precedent 04.08.2021).

Worked Example

Scenario: A Level 9 officer (Delhi HQ) tours to Mumbai for 2 nights, staying at a hotel actually charging ₹2,600/night (voucher produced), takes local taxis totalling ₹400/day for 3 days, and is away >12 hours each day.
Step 1 — Hotel: Ceiling for Level 9-11 (current) = ₹2,812.50/night. Actual ₹2,600 < ceiling → reimbursed ₹2,600 × 2 = ₹5,200 (voucher-based, actual expenditure within ceiling).
Step 2 — Travel-within-city: Ceiling (current) = ₹422.50/day. Actual ₹400 < ceiling → reimbursed ₹400 × 3 = ₹1,200.
Step 3 — Food (lump sum): ₹1,125/day (current) × 100% (each day >12 hrs away) × 3 days = ₹3,375.
Result: Total DA on tour = ₹5,200 + ₹1,200 + ₹3,375 = ₹9,775 (plus separately claimable air/rail fare).

Q1. On what date did Dearness Allowance cross 50% (triggering the 25% TA rate rise)?

Q2. Was a fresh dedicated O.M. required to enhance TA/DA rates when DA crossed 50%?
  1. Yes, 01.01.2024
  2. Yes, 13.07.2024
  3. No — automatic under the standing clause
  4. No revision occurred

Answer: (c) No — automatic under the standing clause — “no fresh dedicated O.M. was necessary”. Rule citation: ch08-005

Q3. Current DA per the 22.11.2026 update?
  1. 50%
  2. 60%
  3. 63% w.e.f. 01.07.2026

Answer: (c) 63% w.e.f. 01.07.2026 — O.M. 1/2(i)/2026-E.II(B) dated 29.10.2026. Rule citation: ch08-009 (L2036–2048)
