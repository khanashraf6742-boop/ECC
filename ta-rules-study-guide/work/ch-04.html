ch04-004

Very High-YieldOwn car/taxi/scooter — default rates where no state rate exists

Source L1188–1201
Exact-Source Fact Card

At places where no specific rate has been prescribed either by the Directorate of Transport of the concerned State or of a neighbouring State, Mileage Allowance for road journeys by own car/taxi/auto-rickshaw/own scooter is payable at currently applicable (25%-enhanced) rates of ₹30 per km (own car/taxi) and ₹15 per km (auto/own scooter etc.). Where a state-specific Road Mileage Allowance (RMA) rate does exist (e.g., defence services use PCDA-notified state-wise RMA rates), that state rate governs instead.

ModeCurrent Rate (Applicable Today)
Own car / taxi₹30 per km
Auto-rickshaw / own scooter₹15 per km
Journey on foot₹15 per km (additional allowance)
Trap note: "on foot" and "auto/scooter" share the same default rate (₹15/km) — easy to mix up in MCQs, but they are conceptually different entitlements (one is a mode of local travel reimbursement, the other a foot-travel-specific add-on).

The "First Kilometre" Rule — Dead Kilometre for Road Mileage

Under the FR/SR rules (carry-over from the 6th CPC regime, still applicable in most departments), no mileage is payable for the first kilometre of a road journey performed by own car or hired taxi. The calculation of Mileage Allowance begins from the 2nd kilometre onward.

Worked Example: An officer travels 10 km by own car for an official tour.
• Billable distance: 10 − 1 = 9 km (the first km is a "dead kilometre").
• Mileage Allowance: 9 km × ₹30/km = ₹270 (NOT ₹300).
• Trap: If the question says "10 km by own car at ₹30/km" — don't blindly compute 10 × 30 = ₹300. Deduct the dead kilometre first!

Exception: This rule applies to own car and hired taxi journeys. For journeys by auto-rickshaw (metered fare) or public bus, the actual fare paid is reimbursed without this deduction.

Exam Note: While the Department of Expenditure's 2017 O.M. does not explicitly restate the dead-km rule in its main text, it continues to be applied by most Ministries' Accounts sections as an inherited FR/SR practice. Verify with your Ministry's internal TA manual for departmental applicability.

Worked Example

Scenario: A Level 6 officer travels 40 km by own car in a state with no Directorate-of-Transport-notified rate, on official tour.
Given: The current road mileage rate is ₹30/km.
Step 1: Identify applicable rate — no state rate exists, so use the default.
Step 2: Distance × rate = 40 × ₹30 = ₹1,200.
Result: ₹1,200 payable as Mileage Allowance for that road journey.
Counter-example: If this journey were in Punjab (state RMA ₹15/km, illustrative defence table), the state-specific rate would override the default — always check for a state rate first.

Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Level 6, 40 km own car, no state rate → 40 × ₹30 = ₹1,200 (as the source’s worked example states it, without deducting the dead km); always check for a state rate first.

Q1. Default mileage rate for own car/taxi where no state rate is prescribed (as per the guide’s current 25%-enhanced rates)?
  1. ₹15/km
  2. ₹24/km
  3. ₹30/km
  4. ₹62.50/km

Answer: (c) ₹30/km — “₹30 per km (own car/taxi) and ₹15 per km (auto/own scooter etc.)”. Rule citation: ch04-004 (L1188–1201)

Q2. Which takes priority?
  1. Default ₹30/₹15 over state rate
  2. State Directorate of Transport rate over neighbouring-state rate over default
  3. Neighbouring-state rate over own-state rate
  4. All are alternatives

Answer: (b) State Directorate of Transport rate over neighbouring-state rate over default — Fact-Sheet: “State DoT rate > neighbouring-state rate > default ₹30/₹15”. Rule citation: ch04-008 (L1237–1246)

Q3. Applying the dead-kilometre rule, a 10 km own-car journey at ₹30/km yields:
  1. ₹300
  2. ₹270
  3. ₹150
  4. ₹285

Answer: (b) ₹270 — (10 − 1) × ₹30 = ₹270 per the micro-note. Rule citation: ch04-005 (L1202–1212)

Q4. The dead-km deduction applies to which journeys?
  1. Own car and hired taxi
  2. Metered auto-rickshaw
  3. Public bus
  4. All road journeys

Answer: (a) Own car and hired taxi — “This rule applies to own car and hired taxi journeys… auto-rickshaw (metered fare) or public bus, the actual fare paid is reimbursed without this deduction.” Rule citation: ch04-005
