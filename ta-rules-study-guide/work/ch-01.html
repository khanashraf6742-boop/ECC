Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • TA/DA ka poora system kaise chalta hai? Basically, Travelling Allowance ka system FR (Fundamental Rules) aur SR (Supplementary Rules) pe based hai — ye bohot purane rules hain jo British era se chale aa rahe hain. Inhi rules ko time-time pe Finance Ministry (Department of Expenditure) apne O.M. (Office Memorandum) ke through update karta rehta hai. To agar aapko TA/DA ka koi rule samajhna hai, to pehle FR/SR dekho, phir latest O.M. check karo.
  • 7th CPC ka "Master Order" kaunsa hai? 7th Pay Commission lagne ke baad, 13 July 2017 ko ek comprehensive O.M. nikla — No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV — jo TA Rules ka "Bible" ban gaya. Isi ek O.M. ne 6th CPC ke purane 2008 wale order (No. 19030/3/2008-E.IV, dated 23.09.2008) ko poori tarah replace (supersede) kar diya. Matlab ab 2008 wala order kisi kaam ka nahi — sirf 2017 wala O.M. aur uske baad ke amendments hi lagu hain.
  • Effective date ka confusion (Classic Trap): Ye O.M. issue to 13 July 2017 ko hua, lekin applicable 1 July 2017 se hai — yani 13 din pehle ki journey bhi isi naye rule ke hisaab se claim hogi. Exams mein ye bohot puchha jaata hai — "13.07.2017 ya 01.07.2017?" — answer hamesha 01.07.2017 (effective date) hai, issue date nahi. Socho jaise salary hike January se milta hai chahe order March mein nikle — same concept.
  • Pay Level kya hai? Grade Pay khatam kyun hua? Pehle (6th CPC mein) aapki TA/DA entitlement aapke Grade Pay (jaise ₹4200, ₹5400, ₹6600 etc.) se decide hoti thi. 7th CPC ne Grade Pay ka system poori tarah khatam kar diya aur uski jagah "Pay Level" (Level 1 se Level 18 tak) laaya. Ye Pay Level CCS (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016 ke Pay Matrix mein defined hai. Example: Agar aapka Grade Pay pehle ₹4200 tha, to ab aap Level 6 mein ho — aur aapki TA/DA entitlement Level 6 ke hisaab se hogi. Simple mapping: GP 1800→L1, GP 2800→L5, GP 4200→L6, GP 5400→L9/L10, GP 6600→L11, GP 7600→L12, GP 8700→L13, GP 10000→L14.
  • GFR aur TA Rules mein kya farq hai? (Exam Trap) Ye bohot important distinction hai. GFR (General Financial Rules) TA ke rates nahi batate — wo sirf procedure aur financial discipline batate hain. Example: ordinary Tour/Transfer/Training TA claims are due within 60 days after journey completion, whether or not an advance was drawn; retirement TA is 180 days under the later DoE O.M. dated 15.06.2021. These are submission clocks, not advance-adjustment deadlines. Lekin kitna paisa milega (kitna hotel, kitna food, kitna mileage) — ye GFR nahi batata, ye TA Rules (O.M. 13.07.2017) batate hain. Exam mein trap ye hota hai: "GFR Rule 290 prescribes ₹30/km mileage rate" — ye galat hai, GFR Rule 290 concerns ordinary TA claim submission; retirement TA and advance adjustment are separate questions.
  • Real-life example: Maan lo aap ek Section Officer (Level 7) ho Delhi mein. Aapko Mumbai 3 din ki training pe bheja gaya. Ab aapka TA claim kaise banega? (1) Aapka Pay Level 7 hai, to aap Economy class flight le sakte ho, AC-II train bhi, hotel ceiling ₹937.50/day hai, food lump sum ₹1,000/day milega. (2) Bill 60 din ke andar submit karna hai (GFR Rule 290). (3) Ye sab rules O.M. 13.07.2017 se aate hain, GFR se nahi. Bas yahi poora system hai!
Memory Hook

"2017 beats 2008, Pay Level beats Grade Pay, GFR-290 = ordinary TA claim clock."

Exam Angle / PYQ

Examiners love to test the exact O.M. number (19030/1/2017-E.IV) and date (13.07.2017), the superseded order (19030/3/2008-E.IV, 23.09.2008), and the effective date (01.07.2017 — note this is before the issue date, a classic trap). Also frequently tested: confusing the GFR 60-day general limit with the 180-day special limit for retirement TA claims (Chapter 6).

Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Base order = DoE O.M. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 13.07.2017 (supersedes 19030/3/2008-E.IV of 23.09.2008), applicable from 01.07.2017. Entitlement follows the Pay Level in the CCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2016 Pay Matrix; GFR gives procedure, not rates. Claim clocks: 60 days (tour/transfer/training) vs 180 days (retirement); advance adjustment is a separate issue.

Study aid · Memory hook

“2017 beats 2008” — new O.M. replaces old; “13 → issued, 01 → effective (07.2017)”: effective date comes BEFORE the issue date.

Official-text check · read from the official PDF on 26.11.2026

GFR Rule 290 (updated to 31.01.2026): the claim falls due on the date succeeding completion of the journey and must be submitted within sixty days of its becoming due, else it stands forfeited. See E13. Pay Level / “pay” definition: E14.

Four broad kinds of Travelling Allowance are recognised. Compare their purpose and main components:

Four broad TA categories
KindWhen it appliesMain elements / scope
TA on TourOfficial journey away from headquarters.Travel fare plus Daily Allowance (DA).
TA on TransferChange of headquarters.Travel fare, Composite Transfer Grant (CTG), and transportation of personal effects/conveyance, each subject to its own conditions.
TA on RetirementEligible one-way journey after retirement.Journey entitlement to the declared home town or place of settlement, subject to the applicable retirement rules.
Permanent / Fixed TA and Mileage AllowanceLocal journeys and journeys by own conveyance where no specific State rate is prescribed.Fixed-allowance or per-kilometre treatment, as applicable; do not assume a default rate where a state-specific rate applies.

Related component, not a fifth kind: DA on Tour is a lump-sum/reimbursement mechanism and a component of tour TA, covering hotel, local travel and food.

Simple Terms (Hinglish)
  • TA on Tour — Sabse common type: Jab bhi aap apne headquarters (HQ) se bahar kisi official kaam se jaate ho — chahe wo training ho, meeting ho, inspection ho, ya court mein deposition ho — to ye "Tour" kehlata hai. Isme aapko do cheezein milti hain: (1) Travel fare — yaani aane-jaane ka ticket ka paisa (train/air/bus), aur (2) Daily Allowance (DA) — yaani wahan rehne (hotel), khane (food lump sum), aur local ghoomne (auto/taxi) ka paisa. Example: Aap Delhi se Mumbai 3 din ki training pe gaye — to flight ka ticket + hotel ka paisa + khane ka lump sum + Mumbai mein auto ka paisa — sab milega. Tour tab shuru hota hai jab aap HQ chhodte ho, aur tab khatam hota hai jab aap wapas HQ pahunchte ho.
  • TA on Transfer — Jab posting badalti hai: Transfer TA ek alag journey category hai jisme self/family travel, CTG, personal effects aur conveyance jaise components ho sakte hain. Inki eligibility aur duration classification ko order, public-interest/fact pattern aur current SR 114 wording se confirm karo; “more than 180 days = transfer / otherwise tour” is guide mein verified universal rule nahi hai. DoE 13.07.2017 base rates checked hain, lekin har component/condition nahi.
  • TA on Retirement — Akhri journey ka paisa: Jab aap retire hote ho (superannuation, invalid pension, ya compensation pension pe), to aapko aur aapki poori family ko ek baar apne home town ya declared settlement place tak jaane ka TA milta hai. Isme bhi Transfer TA jaisa hi milta hai — fare + CTG + baggage + conveyance. Lekin dhyan rahe: (1) Ye sirf pension pe retire hone walon ko milta hai — resignation, dismissal ya removal pe nahi. (2) Journey 1 saal ke andar complete karni hai retirement date se. (3) Claim 180 din ke andar submit karna hai. (4) Agar settle hone ki jagah home town se door hai, to reimbursement home town tak ke fare pe cap ho jayega.
  • Mileage Allowance — Jab apni gaadi se jao: Agar aap official tour pe apni car, taxi, auto-rickshaw, ya scooter se travel karte ho, to aapko per kilometre ke hisaab se paisa milta hai. Current rates (2024-2026, DA≥50% enhanced): Car/Taxi = ₹30/km, Auto/Scooter = ₹15/km, On foot = ₹15/km. Lekin ek important rule: agar aapke state ke Transport Directorate ne koi specific rate notify kiya hai (jaise Defence mein PCDA ne state-wise rates fix kiye hain), to wo state-specific rate apply hoga — default ₹30 nahi. Aur ek aur baat: pehla kilometre "dead km" hota hai — uska paisa nahi milta (sirf own car/taxi pe apply hota hai).
  • Yaad rakhne ka formula: "T-T-R-M" — Tour (fare + DA), Transfer (fare + CTG + baggage + car), Retirement (same as Transfer but one-time), Mileage (per-km rate). Exam mein puchha jaata hai "TA ke kitne kinds hain?" — answer hamesha 4 hai.
Memory Hook

"T-T-R-M: Tour, Transfer, Retirement, Mileage — ye 4 pillars hain TA ke."

Study aid · Quick revision (not source text)

Four kinds: Tour (fare + DA), Transfer (fare + CTG + personal effects/conveyance), Retirement (eligible one-way journey to home town/settlement place), Permanent/Fixed TA & Mileage (local/own-conveyance journeys). DA on Tour is a component of tour TA, not a fifth kind.

Q1. From which date does DoE O.M. 19030/1/2017-E.IV (issued 13.07.2017) apply?
  1. 13.07.2017
  2. 01.07.2017
  3. 01.01.2017
  4. 23.09.2008

Answer: (b) 01.07.2017 — The Fact Card/Fact-Sheet give “applies from 01.07.2017”; the issue date is the trap. Rule citation: ch01-004 / ch01-007 (L743–776, L828–841)

Q2. Which order did the 13.07.2017 O.M. supersede?
  1. 19030/3/2008-E.IV dated 23.09.2008
  2. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 01.07.2017
  3. 12(1)/2016-E.II(A)
  4. 19030/3/2014-E.IV dated 23.06.2020

Answer: (a) 19030/3/2008-E.IV dated 23.09.2008 — Legal-basis table: “Superseded O.M. No. 19030/3/2008-E.IV dated 23.09.2008”. Rule citation: ch01-004 (L743–776)

Q3. How many broad kinds of Travelling Allowance does Chapter 1 recognise?
  1. Three
  2. Four
  3. Five
  4. Six

Answer: (b) Four — Tour, Transfer, Retirement, Permanent/Fixed TA & Mileage. DA on Tour is “not a fifth kind”. Rule citation: ch01-005 (L777–804)

Q4. The guide’s Pay-Level mapping places a Grade Pay ₹4200 officer at which level?
  1. Level 5
  2. Level 6
  3. Level 7
  4. Level 9

Answer: (b) Level 6 — Grade Pay → Pay Level table: GP 4200 → Level 6. Rule citation: ch01-006 (L805–827)
