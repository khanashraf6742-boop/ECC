### [ch02-004] card L872-897 Very High-Yield Entitlement table — Rail / Air / Steamer by Pay Level

Very High-Yield Entitlement table — Rail / Air / Steamer by Pay Level

Exact-Source Fact Card
Table A of the O.M. dated 13.07.2017 fixes travel entitlement by 5 pay-level bands. Level 13A (Grade Pay ₹8900, added later) was clarified vide O.M. of even number dated 04.09.2017 to carry the same entitlement as Level 13 .




Pay Level | Travel by Air | Travel by Train |

14 and above | Business/Club class | AC-I |

12 & 13 (incl. 13A) | Economy class | AC-I |

9 to 11 | Economy class | AC-II |

6 to 8 | Economy class | AC-II |

5 and below | Not entitled (except specific relaxations) | AC-III / AC Chair Car |


RED-FLAG TRAP Flag: "Levels 6–8 are too junior for air travel." Fix: Under the 7th CPC rules, Levels 6 to 11 are entitled to Economy Class air travel on tour — a major liberalisation from the 6th CPC regime. Fix-phrase: "6 through 13 all fly economy or better; only 5-and-below stays on the ground (by air)."

### [ch02-005] card L898-910 High-Yield Premium / Special trains & international travel

High-Yield Premium / Special trains & international travel

Exact-Source Fact Card
Officials may travel by Premium Trains/Premium Tatkal Trains/Suvidha Trains , with reimbursement of Premium Tatkal charges. Dynamic/Flexi-fare in Shatabdi/Rajdhani/Duronto trains is reimbursable while on official tour/training. Tatkal Seva Charges (fixed fare) continue to be allowed. The 12.09.2022 Tejas O.M. allows Tejas travel for tour, training, transfer and retirement , with class entitlement as for Shatabdi trains. For international air travel, the entitlement cited here is based on SR 48-B and remains outside this targeted source check; confirm the operative primary text before use.




Train type | Pay Level entitlement |

Rajdhani / Shatabdi / Duronto / Tejas (dynamic-fare) | All eligible levels, as per their base class entitlement (12 & above → Executive/AC-I; 6–11 → AC-II/Chair Car; 5 & below → AC-III/Chair Car) |


Analogy Think of "dynamic fare" trains like Uber's surge pricing — the government doesn't cap your reimbursement to the "normal" fare; it reimburses whatever the surge price was, since you had no choice on an official tour.


Exam Angle / PYQ Trap: Tatkal Seva Charge (fixed, small fee) vs Premium Tatkal (dynamic, can be large) — both are reimbursable, but examiners test whether you know both exist and are treated alike (reimbursable), unlike ordinary "convenience fee" add-ons which are not.
