### [ch07-008] card L1616-1642 Reference — status varies OM 3: DoE O.M. dated 01.02.2018 — Daily Allowance under Free Boarding and Lodging

Reference — status varies OM 3: DoE O.M. dated 01.02.2018 — Daily Allowance under Free Boarding and Lodging

Exact-Source Fact Card
Consequent upon the restructured Daily Allowance (DA) components under the 7th CPC (Hotel, Food, Local Travel), Department of Expenditure vide O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 01.02.2018 clarified the admissibility of Daily Allowance when free boarding and/or lodging is provided by the Government or host organization:


Component-wise Zeroing Rule: Since DA is purely a reimbursement of actual expenses, if any component is provided free, that specific claim is zeroed out:

Free stay (Lodging) provided: Hotel stay component is reduced to NIL.

Free meals (Boarding) provided: Food bill component is reduced to NIL.

Both stay and meals provided free: Both Hotel and Food components are reduced to NIL. No Daily Allowance for stay/meals can be claimed.




The 25% Flat DA Abolition: Under 5th & 6th CPC, if both boarding and lodging were free, employees were paid a flat 25% of the single-rate DA for out-of-pocket expenses. This O.M. completely abolished the 25% flat DA rule.

Local Travel Admissibility: Even if stay and meals are free, the employee is still entitled to claim actual or capped local travelling charges under Para 2E(i) & (iii) for local journeys actually performed on official duty (e.g. from airport to the guest house).

### [ch07-009] card L1643-1673 Reference — status varies OM 4: DoE O.M. dated 13.03.2018 & 15.06.2021 — TA Claim Time Limits

Reference — status varies OM 4: DoE O.M. dated 13.03.2018 & 15.06.2021 — TA Claim Time Limits

Exact-Source Fact Card
To align Travelling Allowance claims with the procedural discipline of the General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017, the submission timelines were drastically modified:


1. Tour, Transfer, and Training claims (O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 13.03.2018):

The ordinary tour/transfer/training claim window is 60 days succeeding the applicable trigger , whether or not an advance was drawn. For the journey itself the trigger is completion; para 2 specifies separate triggers for relevant family journeys and actual delivery/arrival of personal effects or conveyances.

This includes road mileage and the transport of personal effects/conveyances (which must be submitted within 60 days of their actual delivery/arrival at the destination).

Relinquishment / delayed-claim handling: The O.M. says an unpreferred claim is forfeited/deemed relinquished at the 60-day limit. Para 5 separately says that a TA claim allowed to remain in abeyance for more than one year should be investigated by the Head of Department; if genuineness and valid reasons for delay are established, it may be paid after usual checks. This is a limited stated procedure—not automatic or general condonation for any late claim.




2. TA on Retirement claims (O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 15.06.2021):

Recognizing the practical disruptions and settlement delays faced by retired employees and their families, the time limit for submitting Retirement TA claims was relaxed to 180 days (6 months) succeeding the date of completion of the journey.

This 180-day window applies only to retirement claims ; ordinary tour, transfer and training claims remain due within 60 days of the applicable trigger, whether or not an advance was drawn.

### [ch07-010] card L1674-1700 Reference — status varies OM 5: DoE O.M. dated 29.06.2018 — GST/Taxes on Hotel Entitlement

Reference — status varies OM 5: DoE O.M. dated 29.06.2018 — GST/Taxes on Hotel Entitlement

Exact-Source Fact Card
Following the introduction of the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in July 2017, numerous references were made to the Ministry of Finance regarding whether hotel tax is inclusive of or exclusive of the pay-level-based hotel accommodation ceilings.

The Rule (DoE O.M. No. 19030/2/2017-E.IV dated 29.06.2018):


GST is Exclusive: The hotel staying accommodation ceilings prescribed under the revised TA Rules (Para 2E(i) of the 13.07.2017 O.M.) are strictly exclusive of all taxes and GST.

Reimbursement Over and Above the Ceiling: The GST/taxes levied by hotels shall be reimbursed to the Government servant over and above the prescribed ceiling/entitlement!

The Proportionate Restriction: The reimbursement of GST/taxes is calculated only on the actual room tariff paid within their prescribed ceiling.

Case A (Within Ceiling): A PML 9 officer (entitled up to ₹2,812.50/day) stays in a room costing ₹2,500/night with ₹450 GST. Since ₹2,500 is within the ceiling, they get reimbursed the full ₹2,500 + ₹450 GST = ₹2,950 .

Case B (Exceeding Ceiling): The same officer stays in a room costing ₹3,500/night with ₹630 GST. Their room rent is restricted to the ceiling of ₹2,812.50, and their GST reimbursement is restricted to the GST rate calculated on ₹2,812.50 (e.g. ₹506.25 GST), resulting in ₹2,812.50 + ₹506.25 = ₹3,318.75 . The remaining excess tariff and excess GST must be borne personally.







[Hinglish block omitted from dump]
Exam Angle / PYQ A high-yield MCQ calculation trap! "A Level 9 officer stays in a hotel costing ₹2,500/night + ₹450 GST. Their entitlement ceiling is ₹2,812.50. How much is reimbursed?" — many candidates restrict the total to the ₹2,812.50 ceiling. The correct answer is ₹2,950 (₹2,500 actual rent + ₹450 GST), because GST is paid over and above the ceiling when the room tariff is within the limit.

### [ch07-013] card L1740-1761 Reference — status varies OM 8: DoE O.M. dated 06.01.2022 — CTG on Retirement

Reference — status varies OM 8: DoE O.M. dated 06.01.2022 — CTG on Retirement

Exact-Source Fact Card
Under the base O.M. dated 13.07.2017, retired employees settling down at their last station of duty, or within 20 km of it, were restricted to a reduced one-third (1/3rd) Composite Transfer Grant (CTG) .

The Revision (DoE O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 06.01.2022):


20 Km restriction abolished: The 20 km distance restriction for claiming full CTG on retirement is completely done away with / abolished.

Full CTG Admissible: Retiring Central Government employees who wish to settle down at their last station of duty, or within 20 km of it, are entitled to FULL CTG at the rate of 80% of their last month's Basic Pay , provided there is an actual change of residence involved.

Abolition of 1/3rd CTG: The old one-third (1/3rd) CTG reduction for retirement is completely defunct. If there is an actual change of residence, the retiree gets 100% of their eligible 80% basic pay CTG (e.g., if basic pay is ₹1,00,000, they get the full ₹80,000 CTG). If there is no change of residence, CTG is Nil.

### [ch07-014] card L1762-1796 Reference — status varies OM 9: DoE O.M. dated 24.01.2022 — TA for Re-employed Persons

Reference — status varies OM 9: DoE O.M. dated 24.01.2022 — TA for Re-employed Persons

Exact-Source Fact Card
To regulate Travelling Allowance (self/family fares, CTG, personal effects, and conveyance transport) for retired persons who are re-employed in Central Government service, Department of Expenditure vide O.M. No. 19030/4/2020-E.IV dated 24.01.2022 issued consolidated guidelines:

If they are subsequently re-employed at a station other than their place of settlement, or if the re-employment necessitates a change of residence:

They are allowed Transfer TA for joining the re-employed post, and also upon completion of their re-employment term.

Entitlement scale: Follows the pay scale of the re-employed post.




Scenario II: Pensioner who HAS NOT claimed TA on Retirement:
If they are re-employed within one year of retirement and have not yet claimed their retirement TA:

They are allowed TA for joining the re-employed post (if at a station other than their last station of duty, or if it necessitates a change of residence).

Who bears the joining cost?: The Travelling Allowance for joining is borne and paid by the organization from which they retired (with reference to the post held at retirement).

Who bears the reversion/completion cost?: Upon completion of the re-employment term, the return TA is paid by the re-employing organization.

### [ch07-019] card L1902-1941 Reference — status varies TA & Pay Responsibilities during Deputation, Foreign Service, and Joining Time under CCS Rules

Reference — status varies TA & Pay Responsibilities during Deputation, Foreign Service, and Joining Time under CCS Rules

Exact-Source Fact Card
Under the Central Civil Services (CCS) rules — specifically the CCS (Joining Time) Rules, 1979 (Rule 7) , standard DoPT Deputation Guidelines (OM No. 6/8/2009-Estt.(Pay-II)) , and Supplementary Rules (SR 206) — the financial responsibility for Travelling Allowance (TA), transit pay, and joining time pay is regulated with absolute procedural clarity:


1. During Deputation (Within Central Government / PSUs / Autonomous Bodies):

Onward Journey (Joining Deputation): The Transfer TA (including fares, CTG, and transportation of personal effects/conveyance) is borne and paid entirely by the borrowing department/organization .

Return Journey (Reversion to Parent Cadre): Upon repatriation/reversion, the return Transfer TA is also borne and paid entirely by the borrowing department/organization (the organization they are leaving).

On-Tour TA/DA: All Travelling Allowance and Daily Allowance for official tours performed during the deputation tenure are borne and sanctioned by the borrowing department/organization .

The Absorption Exception: If the employee is permanently absorbed into the borrowing organization, NO return TA is payable, as no repatriation journey takes place.




2. During Foreign Service (Transfer to State Govts / International Bodies / PSUs on Foreign Terms):

Onward & Return Journeys: Under SR 206, both the onward Transfer TA (to join) and the return Transfer TA (on reversion) are borne and paid entirely by the foreign employer .

On-Tour TA/DA: All TA/DA for official tours performed during the foreign service period are paid entirely by the foreign employer , governed by the foreign employer's rules (or parent-cadre rules if specified in the terms of deputation).




3. Joining Time Pay & Salary Responsibility (Rule 7, CCS (Joining Time) Rules, 1979):

Core Mandate: A Government servant on joining time is regarded as on duty and is entitled to Joining Time Pay (equal to the pay drawn in the old post before relinquishing charge, along with DA, HRA, and CCA appropriate to the old station).

Who Pays for Joining Time?: Under Rule 7, the joining time pay and allowances shall be paid for strictly by the new administrative authority (the borrowing department / new organization) where the Government servant joins on transfer.

On Reversion: Upon repatriation/reversion back to the parent cadre, the borrowing organization (the department being left) bears the joining time pay, allowances, and transit pay for the return journey.







[Hinglish block omitted from dump]
Memory Hook " The Guest Never Pays . Whether joining or returning from deputation/foreign service, the borrowing/host organization (guest office) bears the Transfer TA and the Joining Time Pay."


Exam Angle / PYQ Highly targeted exam questions: "Who is responsible for paying joining time pay under Rule 7?" — Option (a) Old office, (b) New office, (c) Shared equally, (d) Personal expense.
