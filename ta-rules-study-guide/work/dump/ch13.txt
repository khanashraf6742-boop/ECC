### [ch13-004] micronote L3345-3362 

Use this decision aid instead of the old yes/no chart:

1. First assess technical-resignation status under the applicable DoPT conditions (including proper-channel / prior-application rules where relevant).

2. Separately check whether the appointment is within Rule 4(4) as substituted in 2015, including its conditions and service-counting option.

3. Separately read DoPT O.M. para 3.6.3 and check for later binding clarification or department-specific instructions.

4. If the two provisions affect the case, obtain a written decision from the competent authority. This study aid does not decide an employee's TTA or Joining Time Pay.

### [ch13-005] recap L3363-3365 Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 13: keep both authorities visible
Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 13: keep both authorities visible

The 2015 Gazette substitutes CCS (Joining Time) Rule 4(4) with conditions for qualifying competitive appointments, including the option concerning past service; its substituted text contains no express three-year cutoff.
DoPT’s 24.11.2022 consolidation para 3.6.3 separately states a three-year condition for temporary Central Government employees’ TTA through Joining Time Pay.
Interaction unresolved: do not infer a categorical answer from confirmation, probation or years served alone; ask the competent authority to apply both texts to the actual facts.
Keep the Official Gazette text distinct from the Government-hosted NESTS reproduction; the direct DoPT record was not retrieved. This recap is not an individual ruling.
