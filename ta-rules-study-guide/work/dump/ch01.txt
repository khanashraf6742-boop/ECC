### [ch01-004] card L743-776 Very High-Yield Legal basis & supersession chain

Very High-Yield Legal basis & supersession chain

Exact-Source Fact Card
Use this source map to separate the governing framework, rate-setting orders and claim-processing clocks:


Legal framework, source role and boundary

Topic | Rule / authority | Effect and boundary |


TA framework | Fundamental Rules (FR) and Supplementary Rules (SR) , specifically SR 17–SR 246, as revised by Department of Expenditure (DoE), Ministry of Finance Office Memoranda. | The O.M.s revise and supplement the underlying rules; check the current instrument for the issue at hand. |

7th CPC base order | DoE O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 13.07.2017 . | Superseded O.M. No. 19030/3/2008-E.IV dated 23.09.2008 and applies from 01.07.2017 . |

Pay-Level basis | CCS (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016 Pay Matrix. | The Pay Level shown in that matrix determines TA/DA entitlement; the guide’s rate and entitlement tables are not all independently revalidated. |

GFR role | General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017; Rule 290 and DoE O.M. 13.03.2018. | GFR does not separately prescribe TA rates; its role here is procedural/financial-propriety. Rule 290 and the O.M. provide the ordinary claim-submission framework. |

Ordinary TA claim | Tour, transfer and training TA: 60 days succeeding the applicable trigger , whether or not an advance was drawn. | DoE O.M. 13.03.2018 para 2 provides separate triggers for relevant family journeys and delivered personal effects/conveyances. |

Retirement TA claim | 180 days under DoE O.M. 15.06.2021. | This is a separate claim-submission clock, not an advance-adjustment period. |

Claim kept in abeyance | DoE O.M. 13.03.2018 para 5. | Provides a specific review route where a claim is kept in abeyance for more than one year; it is not blanket condonation. |

Advance adjustment | Separate issue from the above submission clocks. | Do not infer an advance-adjustment deadline from the 60-day or 180-day claim-submission periods. |



Source boundary: This is a navigation summary, not a complete validation of current SR 114, SR 164, later amendments or advance-adjustment instructions.

Exam Angle / PYQ Examiners love to test the exact O.M. number ( 19030/1/2017-E.IV ) and date ( 13.07.2017 ), the superseded order ( 19030/3/2008-E.IV, 23.09.2008 ), and the effective date ( 01.07.2017 — note this is before the issue date, a classic trap). ### [ch01-005] card L777-804 High-Yield Kinds of Travelling Allowance

High-Yield Kinds of Travelling Allowance

Exact-Source Fact Card
Four broad kinds of Travelling Allowance are recognised. Compare their purpose and main components:


Four broad TA categories

Kind | When it applies | Main elements / scope |


TA on Tour | Official journey away from headquarters. | Travel fare plus Daily Allowance (DA). |

TA on Transfer | Change of headquarters. | Travel fare, Composite Transfer Grant (CTG), and transportation of personal effects/conveyance, each subject to its own conditions. |

TA on Retirement | Eligible one-way journey after retirement. | Journey entitlement to the declared home town or place of settlement, subject to the applicable retirement rules. |

Permanent / Fixed TA and Mileage Allowance | Local journeys and journeys by own conveyance where no specific State rate is prescribed. | Fixed-allowance or per-kilometre treatment, as applicable; do not assume a default rate where a state-specific rate applies. |



Related component, not a fifth kind: DA on Tour is a lump-sum/reimbursement mechanism and a component of tour TA, covering hotel, local travel and food.

Exam Trap: Examiners may ask "What is the TA entitlement of a GP 4200 officer?" — you must map this to Level 6 first, then look up the Level 6 entitlement (Economy air, AC-II train). The concept of "Grade Pay" no longer exists in the 7th CPC TA framework.

Review Protocol

If you can recall the O.M. number, both dates (issue vs effective), and the 4 kinds of TA without looking — you're solid. Remember: ordinary tour/transfer/training claim submission is 60 days whether or not an advance was drawn; retirement TA is a separate 180-day window. ### [ch01-009] recap L846-858 Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 1
Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 1


Master order: O.M. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 13.07.2017, effective 01.07.2017.

Supersedes the 2008 (6th CPC) O.M. No.19030/3/2008-E.IV.

Entitlement basis = "Pay Level" in the 2016 Pay Matrix, not Grade Pay.

GFR Rule 290 concerns the ordinary tour/transfer/training claim-submission clock: 60 days, whether or not an advance was drawn. Retirement TA has a separate 180-day order; advance adjustment is separate.

4 kinds of TA: Tour, Transfer, Retirement, Mileage/Fixed.

GFR ≠ TA rate-setting authority — common exam trap.

GP → Level mapping: GP 1800→L1, GP 2800→L5, GP 4200→L6, GP 5400→L10, GP 6600→L11, GP 7600→L12, GP 8700→L13, GP 8900→L13A, GP 10000→L14.

Scope: Civilian Ministries only — Defence/CAPF personnel governed by PCDA(Pune) orders.
