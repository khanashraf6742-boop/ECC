Important authority conflict — no individual ruling: The 2015 amendment, G.S.R. 229(E) dated 27.03.2015, substituted CCS (Joining Time) Rule 4(4) for qualifying competitive appointments. The substituted wording is conditional (including an option to count past service for all purposes in Central Government) and contains no express three-year cutoff. DoPT O.M. dated 24.11.2022, para 3.6.3, separately states that temporary Central Government employees with less than three years' regular continuous service are not entitled to TTA. Their interaction was not resolved in the targeted audit. Do not state categorically that confirmed employees always qualify or that temporary employees under three years never qualify without checking the operative text, conditions, and any later clarification. Technical-resignation status is a separate question from TTA / Joining Time Pay.
