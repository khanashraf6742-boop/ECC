### [ch11-006] card L2504-2555 Very High-Yield The Absolute Spousal Rules under CCS TA & LTC (When Both are Central Government Servants)

Very High-Yield The Absolute Spousal Rules under CCS TA & LTC (When Both are Central Government Servants)

Exact-Source Fact Card
Under the Central Civil Services (CCS) TA and LTC rules (governed by Supplementary Rules (SR) and DoPT guidelines), the entitlements when both husband and wife are Central Government servants are subject to highly specific restrictions and concessions to prevent double billing while protecting individual rights:


1. Transfer TA — Composite Transfer Grant (CTG) Split (The 60-day / 6-Month Rules):
If both spouses are transferred from the same old station to the same new station within six (6) months :

Transfers ordered more than 60 days apart: The spouse transferred earlier gets full CTG (80% of Basic Pay) , and the spouse transferred later gets half (50% of the normal grant, which equals 40% of Basic Pay) .

Transfers ordered within 60 days of each other: NO Composite Transfer Grant is admissible to the spouse transferred later! Only the first spouse receives the grant.

Note: If the transfers take place after a period of 6 months or more, both are treated as independent, and both get full CTG.




2. Transfer TA — Baggage & Family Fares Double-Dipping Ban:

The cost of travel for dependent family members and the transportation of personal effects can be claimed only once by either husband or wife.

Combined Weight Ceiling: If they are transferred at different times, they can transport their share, but the total weight of personal effects transported by both combined cannot exceed the maximum entitlement of the senior-most spouse (e.g., restricted to a maximum of 6,000 kg).

The Double Car Exception: If both spouses own a personal car separately (registered in their respective individual names), they are entitled to claim transportation charges for both cars if they travel separately!




3. Retirement TA — Settle at Same vs. Different Stations (SR 147):

Settle at the Same Place: They can claim fares for themselves separately. However, for family members, only one can claim, and the combined personal effects weight must be restricted to the senior spouse's limit (e.g. 6,000 kg).

Settle at Different Places: If they choose to settle at different stations, they can claim TA for their respective journeys and personal effects separately , up to their respective individual entitlements.




4. Leave Travel Concession (LTC) Spousal Rules:

Separate Home Towns: They can, at their option, declare separate Home Towns and claim LTC separately under the normal provisions of CCS (LTC) Rules.

The Dependent Double-Dipping Ban: A family member/child can be claimed as dependent only by one spouse in a particular block year. A child cannot avail of LTC twice in a block (once as the husband's dependent and once as the wife's).

Claiming Spouse as Dependent: A spouse can be claimed as a dependent family member by the other spouse, provided that the spouse being claimed does not claim LTC for themselves separately during that same block .

Leave Encashment (10 days): Both spouses are entitled to 10 days of earned leave encashment at the time of availing LTC separately, subject to a career maximum of 60 days each.

### [ch11-007] card L2556-2592 Very High-Yield TA to Family on Death-in-Service — Absolute Eligibility, Surety and Recovery Rules (SR 148)

Very High-Yield TA to Family on Death-in-Service — Absolute Eligibility, Surety and Recovery Rules (SR 148)

Exact-Source Fact Card
In the tragic event of a Government servant dying while in service, the concessions and advance of Travelling Allowance for the family to settle at their normal place of residence or home town are governed under SR 148 with very strict financial rules:


1. Absolute Eligibility Exclusions (Rule 6, SR 148):
The death-in-service TA concession is strictly NOT admissible to the families of:

(a) Employees who die while on leave preparatory to retirement .

(b) Retired employees who are re-employed in government service.

(c) Temporary employees who have not rendered at least three (3) years of regular continuous service .




2. Surety Requirement for TA Advance (GIO (3), SR 148):
The family can draw a TA advance up to three-fourths (75%) of the probable amount of travel expenses.
The Security Rule: To draw this advance, the family must provide a surety of a permanent Central Government employee of comparable or higher status! Only one family member can receive the advance on behalf of all, and an undertaking for recovery must be signed.

3. Strictly Enforced Journey & Recovery Timelines:
The journey of the family to the settlement place must be completed within one year (365 days) from the date of the employee's death .
The Adjustment Deadline (Bada Trap):

If the family travels in one batch: The final adjustment claim must be submitted within one month of the completion of the journey.

If the family travels in more than one batch: The final adjustment claim must be submitted within one month of the completion of the journey by the last batch .

If the account is not rendered within one month of the journey, the entire advance must be refunded immediately .

### [ch11-009] card L2620-2651 Very High-Yield Effect of free transport / boarding / lodging on Daily Allowance (7th CPC Reduction Rules)

Very High-Yield Effect of free transport / boarding / lodging on Daily Allowance (7th CPC Reduction Rules)

Exact-Source Fact Card
Under the 7th CPC (clarified via Department of Expenditure O.M. No. 19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 01.02.2018 ), the old concept of flat-rate Daily Allowance is replaced by three independent reimbursement components: (1) Hotel Accommodation, (2) Food Bills, and (3) Local Travel/Conveyance. Consequently, when an employee avails free facilities, the DA is scaled down component-wise:


1. Free Lodging Provided: If the employee is provided free stay (e.g. Government Guest House, Circuit House, or Host accommodation), the Hotel Accommodation component is reduced to NIL (no claim allowed).

2. Free Boarding Provided: If the employee is provided free meals (e.g. by the Government, Host Organization, or during residential training), the Food Bill component is reduced to NIL (no claim allowed).

3. Free Transport Provided: If the employee is provided free local transport (official vehicle/pool car), the Local Travel/Conveyance component is reduced to NIL .

4. When BOTH Free Boarding & Lodging are Provided:

Both Hotel and Food components are reduced to NIL .

The employee can only claim actual/capped local travel charges (as per Para 2E(i) & (iii) of the 13.07.2017 OM) for local journeys actually performed on official duty (e.g., travel from airport/railway station to guest house) which were not covered by free transport.

CRITICAL UPDATE: The old 6th CPC system of granting a flat 25% of the Daily Allowance when boarding and lodging were free has been completely discontinued . There is no flat percentage anymore; if stay and food are free, those components are zeroed out.

### [ch11-010] card L2652-2684 High-Yield DA admissibility for the day of departure/return on overnight journeys

High-Yield DA admissibility for the day of departure/return on overnight journeys

Exact-Source Fact Card
Under Para 2E(v) of the 13.07.2017 O.M., the lump-sum Daily Allowance (Food component) is regulated strictly based on the length of absence from headquarters , calculated on a calendar-day basis (midnight to midnight):


Length of Absence (on a calendar day) | Food DA Percentage Admissible |

Less than 6 hours (< 6 hrs) | 30% of the lump-sum ceiling |

Between 6 to 12 hours (6–12 hrs) | 70% of the lump-sum ceiling |

More than 12 hours (> 12 hrs) | 100% of the lump-sum ceiling |


For overnight train or air journeys, the day of departure and the day of return are calculated strictly based on these time-slab percentages according to the exact departure/arrival times:


Day of Departure (Overnight Journey): Since the journey starts on a calendar day, the absence starts from the actual departure time of the train/flight until midnight (24:00 hours) of that day.

Day of Return (Overnight Journey): The absence is counted from midnight (00:00 hours) of the return day until the actual arrival time of the train/flight at headquarters.




Worked Example
Scenario: A Level 11 Officer (Food DA ceiling: ₹1,125/day ) travels on official tour. They board an overnight train departing at 20:00 (8:00 PM) on Monday , spend all of Tuesday at the tour station, and arrive back at headquarters at 08:30 AM on Wednesday .
Step 1: Calculate Monday (Day of Departure)
Absence on Monday is from 20:00 to 24:00 (midnight) = 4 hours .
Since 4 hours is < 6 hours, Monday's DA is restricted to 30% of ₹1,125.
Monday DA = ₹1,125 × 30% = ₹337.50 .
Step 2: Calculate Tuesday (Full Day on Tour)
The officer is away for the entire 24 hours on Tuesday (> 12 hours) = 100% of ₹1,125.
Tuesday DA = ₹1,125.00 .
Step 3: Calculate Wednesday (Day of Return)
Absence on Wednesday is from midnight (00:00) to 08:30 AM = 8.5 hours .
Since 8.5 hours is between 6 and 12 hours, Wednesday's DA is 70% of ₹1,125.
Wednesday DA = ₹1,125 × 70% = ₹787.50 .
Total Food DA Claimed: ₹337.50 (Mon) + ₹1,125 (Tue) + ₹787.50 (Wed) = ₹2,250.00 .







### [ch11-011] card L2685-2717 High-Yield TA for departmental exams / LDCE & recruitment interviews

High-Yield TA for departmental exams / LDCE & recruitment interviews

Exact-Source Fact Card
Travel entitlements for appearing in examinations and recruitment interviews are distinctly categorized based on employment status:


1. Serving Government Employees appearing in Departmental Exams / LDCE (SR 110, SR 111):

Obligatory Exams (SR 110): If appearing in an obligatory departmental exam (mandatory for confirmation/promotion), the journey is treated as on duty. Full TA as on tour (travel fare per their Pay Level + DA for the journey days) is admissible.

Selection/Promotion Exams / LDCE (SR 111): If appearing in a competitive departmental exam (e.g. SO LDCE) with proper authorization, TA is also admissible as on tour.

CRITICAL LIMITATION (SR 113): No Daily Allowance (DA) is admissible for halts at the examination center. Only travel fare and DA for the journey days (days of transit) are paid.

Attempt Limit: TA is restricted to a maximum of two attempts for any particular examination. No TA is paid for third or subsequent attempts.




2. Candidates called for Recruitment Interviews (Outsiders/Unemployed):

Serving Employees: Sponsoring/serving employees summoned for interview by UPSC/SSC or other departments are treated as on duty and get TA as on tour (travel fare + journey and halt DA) paid by their parent department.

Unemployed / Outsider Candidates (especially SC/ST): Candidates called for written test/interview by UPSC, SSC, or Central Ministries are entitled to second-class / sleeper-class train fare (or bus fare) by the shortest route, provided the distance exceeds a specified limit (usually 80 km) and they are not already employed in government service. For Group-A recruitments, UPSC may allow AC 3-Tier rail fare to all called candidates.

### [ch11-015] card L2794-2800 Moderate-Yield Transfer TA for probationers/trainees on first appointment

Moderate-Yield Transfer TA for probationers/trainees on first appointment

Exact-Source Fact Card
A person joining Government service for the first time (e.g. a direct-recruit probationer reporting to their first place of training or posting) is NOT entitled to any Transfer TA , fares, or baggage transport allowance. The cost of reporting for the first appointment must be borne by the candidate themselves. ### [ch11-016] card L2801-2840 Very High-Yield TA on Retirement — family travel scale, settlement capping, and death before journey

Very High-Yield TA on Retirement — family travel scale, settlement capping, and death before journey

Exact-Source Fact Card
TA on Retirement is governed under SR 147 and provides the same entitlements as Transfer TA (Ch. 5), but is subject to unique capping and family rules:


Family Travel Scale: Eligible family members (as defined in Ch. 10) are entitled to travel fare reimbursement according to the retiree's Pay Matrix Level at the time of retirement, along with full CTG (80% of Basic Pay) and the transportation of personal effects/conveyance.

Settlement Location and Capping Rule: A retiring employee may choose to settle at their last headquarters, their declared home town, or any other place of their choice in India.

If settling at a place other than the last headquarters or home town, the reimbursement (fares + baggage/car transport) is strictly restricted/capped at what would have been admissible had they settled at their declared home town , or the actual expenditure incurred, whichever is less .




Time-Limit for Journey: The retirement journey and baggage transportation must be completed within one year (365 days) from the date of retirement. The final TA claim must be submitted within 180 days succeeding the date of completion of the journey.

Death after Retirement before Journey: If a retired Government servant dies after retirement but before actually performing the retirement journey :

Their eligible family members retain the full entitlement to draw TA on Retirement (fares, CTG, and transportation) to the declared home town or place of settlement.

The journey must be performed within the original one-year window from the retirement date.







[Hinglish block omitted from dump]
Memory Hook " Home Town is the Ceiling . You can settle in Paris or Chennai, but your cash limit stops at Patna (Home Town). If a retiree leaves us too soon, the family still gets the ticket to tune."


Worked Example
Scenario: A Level 12 officer retires from Delhi. Their declared home town is Srinagar (distance: 800 km). They decide to settle in Chennai (distance: 2,200 km). They incur an actual travel and baggage transportation cost of ₹95,000 for the Chennai journey. The calculated notional cost to settle in their home town (Srinagar) is ₹45,000.
Step 1: Apply the Capping Principle
Retirement TA is capped at the lesser of the actual cost incurred (Chennai, ₹95,000) or the notional cost to the declared home town (Srinagar, ₹45,000).
Step 2: Comparison
Notional Srinagar Cost (₹45,000) < Actual Chennai Cost (₹95,000).
Result: The officer's reimbursement is strictly restricted to ₹45,000 . The additional ₹50,000 must be borne personally by the retiree.







### [ch11-017] card L2841-2879 Source-status review Disability-related TA claims — separate verified and unresolved routes

Source-status review Disability-related TA claims — separate verified and unresolved routes

Exact-Source Fact Card
The DoE escort O.M. and the DoPT LTC exception address different benefits. Neither source supports an unrestricted local AC-taxi benefit or an extra baggage-weight allowance for transfer/retirement.


1. Attendant / Escort TA (O.M. No. 19030/3/2013-E.IV dated 17.02.2015):

The O.M. creates a conditional fare claim for an escort accompanying a Government servant with disabilities on tour/training, etc.; it is not a blanket right to a privately arranged escort in every case. Ordinarily, the destination office or institute is to provide an escort.

When a fare claim may arise: the destination office/institute (or Indian Mission for foreign travel) must officially express inability to provide the escort; the competent medical authority must certify that the employee compulsorily requires assistance for travel; the competent authority must approve the need, and the tour/training must meet the O.M.’s stated conditions.

Fare / DA: admissible escort travel is reimbursed at the same rate and in the same class as the employee, subject to concessions and applicable travel restrictions. No DA is admissible to the escort. For road conveyance, no separate escort mileage is payable except where travel is by public bus.




2. Local AC taxi during official tour/training:
The 2017 TA O.M. sets local-travel entitlements by pay level; the 22.12.2020 O.M. changes the receipt/voucher rule for Levels 9–11, not the vehicle class or level ceiling. The 17.02.2015 escort O.M. does not create an employee local-AC-taxi exemption. A general Divyang AC-taxi entitlement regardless of pay level was not verified in the Central primary sources checked; keep it on hold.

3. Additional baggage on transfer/retirement:
The claimed additional 500 kg for assistive devices has not been verified in a current Central primary source. Do not assert it or calculate it into a claim without the operative signed order. The 13.07.2017 DoE O.M. lists the ordinary personal-effects limits but does not establish this claimed supplement.

4. Double Transport Allowance (daily commute, not tour TA):
The official DoE O.M. No. 21/1/2018-E.II(B), 29.07.2025 substituted category B(1) of the 15.09.2022 compendium. ### [ch11-020] recap L2910-2927 Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 11
Rapid-Revision Recap — Chapter 11


TA advances: Do not apply a general 90% tour/transfer cap based on the inspected material. A 15-day tour-advance adjustment appears in the 2005 Compendium copy (current currency unverified); no general 30-day transfer deadline is established here.

Ordinary tour/transfer/training TA claim submission is due within 60 days whether or not an advance was drawn. Do not infer an advance-adjustment deadline from GFR Rule 290.

Free meals + stay on tour reduces Hotel and Food DA components to NIL . No flat 25% allowance exists anymore under 7th CPC. Only actual local travel is reimbursable.

Overnight journey DA is calendar-day-wise (midnight-to-midnight): <6h = 30% , 6-12h = 70% , >12h = 100% of the daily lump-sum food ceiling.

Departmental exams/LDCE give tour TA but no DA for halts at the exam station, and is capped at 2 attempts .

Same-city transfer CTG is Nil unless residence changes, which allows a reduced 1/3 CTG (26.67% of Basic Pay) .

Deputation TA (both onward and reversion) is borne and paid entirely by the borrowing department .

Probationers get Nil TA on first joining.

Retirement TA to an alternate settlement place is capped at the declared home-town distance and fare . Family of a retiree who dies before the journey remains fully eligible within 1 year of retirement.

Disability-related TA: the 17.02.2015 DoE O.M. provides a conditional escort-fare route, not an escort DA entitlement. A general local AC-taxi exemption for official tour/training and a 500 kg transfer/retirement baggage supplement were not established by the primary sources checked. Keep both claims unverified; the separate LTC own/hired-taxi exception has its own conditions.

SR 172: Non-Government candidates called for UPSC/SSC interviews get Second Class train fare if distance >80 km (SC/ST exempt from 80 km minimum). No DA for halts.

Transfer vs Tour: Apply the transfer order and current SR 114 conditions; do not infer category or CTG solely from duration.

TA bill revision: Supplementary/revised claims admissible within 3 years of original claim.
