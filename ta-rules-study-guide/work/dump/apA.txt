### [apA-004] card L2940-3024 

Master TA O.M. number and date?
19030/1/2017-E.IV, dated 13.07.2017 (effective 01.07.2017).

Which O.M. did it supersede?
19030/3/2008-E.IV dated 23.09.2008.

4 kinds of TA?
Tour, Transfer, Retirement, Mileage/Fixed TA.

Ordinary TA claim-submission time limit?
For tour/transfer/training, 60 days succeeding completion of journey under GFR Rule 290 and DoE O.M. 13.03.2018, whether or not an advance was drawn. This does not set an advance-adjustment deadline; retirement TA has a separate 180-day order.

What advance-adjustment deadlines were located?
The inspected 2005 Compendium Rule 51 says 15 days for a tour advance (currency unverified). Rule 43 for transfer advances gives no general 30-day deadline in the inspected copy. Verify current instructions.

Penal interest rate on overdue/unadjusted TA advances?
2% per annum over the GPF interest rate , calculated from the date of drawal to the date of recovery.

Lump-sum recovery rule for overdue TA advances?
The entire outstanding advance is recovered in a lump sum from the employee's salary of the immediate next month.

Retirement TA claim time-limit?
180 days succeeding the date of completion of the journey (clarified in O.M. dated 15.06.2021).

Level 14+ travel entitlement?
Business/Club Class air; AC-I train.

Which levels first got air entitlement (economy) under 7th CPC?
Levels 6 to 11 (a major liberalisation vs 6th CPC).

Level 5 and below — air entitlement?
None (ground travel only — AC-III/Chair Car by train).

3 components of Daily Allowance on tour?
Hotel stay reimbursement + Travel-within-city (local taxi/auto) + Food (lump sum, no voucher).

Self-certification threshold for Hotel/Travel DA?
Levels 8 and below may use the stated route up to the applicable ceiling; the Class-X ₹1,000/day hotel ceiling is voucher-backed. The interaction for claims within the ordinary ceiling is unresolved here.

Daily Allowance (Food component) timing scale?
<6h = 30%; 6–12h = 70%; >12h = 100% of daily lump sum.

Daily Allowance reduction rule if both free boarding and lodging are provided?
Hotel and Food DA components are reduced to NIL . No flat 25% DA is payable. Only actual local travel expenses under Para 2E are admissible.

What 8 km test does the signed local-journey clarification use?
DoE letter No. 904302/2018-E.IV dated 12.09.2018 says the temporary duty point must be beyond 8 km from the normal place of duty, whether travel starts from residence or that duty point. It does not settle all fares/vehicle treatment at or below 8 km; see Chapter 3.

Is there an age limit for a dependent son to be eligible for TA/LTC?
Yes, up to 25 years (or until marriage/earning, whichever is earliest). However, there is no age limit for disabled sons .

What is the dependency income ceiling for family members under 7th CPC?
Monthly income from all sources must not exceed ₹9,000/- plus Dearness Relief (DR) thereon .

Name the 8 Class-X cities under HRA/TA classification.
Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Pune.

What are the three ATAs for Government-account air-ticket booking?
IRCTC, Balmer Lawrie & Company Limited (BLCL), and Ashok Travels & Tours (ATT). Do not extend the air-ticket rule to all rail bookings.

Is AITTSL an authorized travel agent for Government-account air tickets?
No. Use the three ATAs for air tickets under the applicable orders; an airline’s identity as carrier does not itself authorize direct booking. An unavoidable exception needs an authorized relaxation.

What happens if a Government-account air ticket is booked outside the required channel?
The fare may be disallowed unless an authorized relaxation is granted for unavoidable circumstances under the applicable order. Check the exact order and competent authority; do not treat this as a rule for every rail booking.

Are cancellation charges reimbursable?
Yes, if the journey is cancelled/postponed strictly in public interest , supported by a Controlling Officer's certificate.

How is TA regulated if a longer/circuitous route is taken for personal convenience?
TA is strictly restricted to the fare of the shortest route , and extra travel time is treated as leave (not duty).

What are the steamer travel cabin-class entitlements for SCI island ships?
PML 9+: Deluxe; PML 6-8: First Class / 'A' Cabin; PML 4-5: Second Class / 'B' Cabin; PML 1-3: Bunk Class.

How is training DA calculated where boarding/lodging are provided, free, or compulsory at fixed rates?
No universal formula is verified. Secondary SR 164 transcriptions describe facility-specific schedules and a distinct fixed-rate special allowance; DoE O.M. 01.02.2018 separately addresses free boarding/lodging. Verify the current order and facts before calculating.

Default own-car mileage rate (Current)?
₹30/km (applicable today since DA &ge; 50%).

Default auto/scooter mileage (Current)?
₹15/km (applicable today since DA &ge; 50%).

CTG rate for transfer &ge;20 km?
80% of last month's basic pay.

CTG rate for A&N/Lakshadweep transfer?
100% of last month's basic pay.

CTG rate for transfer within the same city/station?
Nil if no change of residence; one-third (1/3) of the normal rate (26.67% of Basic Pay) if there is an actual change of residence.

Who bears the TA cost for an employee on deputation?
The borrowing department/organization bears and pays the Transfer TA for both the onward journey (joining) and the return journey (reversion).

Does a direct-recruit probationer get transfer TA on first joining?
No, first-time joining does not qualify for any Transfer TA (under SR 116).

Does a serving employee get DA for halts while appearing in a departmental exam (LDCE)?
No, under SR 113, no Daily Allowance is admissible for halts at the exam center. Only travel fare and transit DA are paid.

What caps a retiree's TA if they settle at a place other than home town?
Reimbursement is strictly capped at the notional TA/fare to their declared home town , or actual expenditure, whichever is less.

What happens to retirement TA if the employee dies after retirement but before performing the journey?
The eligible family members retain the full entitlement to perform the journey and claim TA within the original 1-year window.

Is pay-level-independent local AC taxi reimbursement for a Divyang employee on official tour verified here?
No. The primary sources checked establish ordinary level-based tour-local-travel rules, a 2020 voucher procedure change for Levels 9–11, and conditional escort fare; they do not establish a general disability-specific local AC-taxi exemption. Keep any separate signed order on file before relying on one.

Is an extra 500 kg baggage allowance for assistive devices on transfer/retirement verified?
No current Central primary source for that supplement was located in this review. Do not treat the claim as an established entitlement pending the operative signed order.

Current Level 14+ hotel ceiling?
₹9,375/day (applicable today).

Current Gratuity ceiling?
₹25 lakh (up from ₹20 lakh due to DA crossing 50%).

Attendant/Escort TA O.M. date?
17.02.2015 (O.M. 19030/3/2013-E.IV).

Tejas Express admissibility clarified when?
12.09.2022.

Death-in-service family TA advance cap?
75% of the likely TA claim.

O.M. date for "vouchers for travel-within-city DA component"?
22.12.2020 (O.M. 19030/2/2020-E.IV).

O.M. date for "Boarding Pass submission with TA bills"?
Originally 08.10.2014 (O.M. 19030/3/2014-E.IV), reiterated 23.06.2020.

What does DoPT F.No. G-14019/02/2026-Cash dated 16.07.2026 cover?
A Government-hosted DGT reproduction was retrieved. It is a DoPT Cash Section circular addressed to DoPT officers/officials. It reiterates the LTC-air 21-day booking encouragement and at-least-30-day advance request for air-entitled employees under the 29.08.2022 instructions, with the specified authorized-travel-agency webpage printout showing suitable flight/fare details. The 21 days are not a compulsory claim deadline. The circular is limited to DoPT staff and LTC air; it does not create a general TA or rail-booking rule. A direct DoPT-hosted copy was not obtained in this pass; the cited PDF is hosted by DGT.

Is a Pay Level 7 officer entitled to travel by air (Economy) on tour? What about on LTC?
On Tour: Yes , fully entitled to Air (Economy Class). On LTC: No , restricted to Rail (AC-II Tier), except under special dispensation schemes.

Who is responsible for paying Joining Time Pay under Rule 7 of CCS (Joining Time) Rules, 1979?
It is paid for strictly by the new administrative authority (the borrowing department / new organization) where the Government servant joins on transfer.

Is Daily Allowance (DA) admissible during Special Casual Leave (SCL) due to curfews or disturbances on tour?
Yes, fully admissible for the entire period of such SCL (SR 51, GIO (4)(b)).

What Food DA does the signed DoE local-journey letter state?
For qualifying journeys beyond 8 km from the normal place of duty, it states 50% of the tour food amount: 15% for <6 hours, 35% for 6–12 hours, and 50% for >12 hours. See letter No. 904302/2018-E.IV dated 12.09.2018; full current SR 71/GIO remains open.

Does the signed letter establish a taxi ban for a journey under 8 km from residence?
No such residence-based ban is established by the letter. It measures the qualifying local-journey threshold from the normal place of duty, regardless of origin. It does not decide public fares or taxi/auto/own-car reimbursement at or below 8 km; keep those claims open pending the current signed SR/GIO.

Does DA automatically reset when theory and practical training are held at different stations?
No general reset is verified. Secondary transcriptions of P&T 1982/1984 orders describe fresh-course treatment at the practical-training station for a limited in-service/refresher setting; the original letters, current status, and applicability to other courses/cadres remain unverified. Do not apply a new 180-day limit at every station change; see Chapter 11.

Is transportation of a personal car on transfer reimbursable if the car is registered in the spouse's name?
No strictly . Transport of personal conveyance is reimbursable ONLY if it is registered strictly in the name of the Government servant themselves .

What is the CTG split if both husband and wife are transferred from the same old station to the same new station within 6 months?
If transfers are ordered more than 60 days apart : earlier spouse gets full CTG (80% of Basic Pay) , later spouse gets half CTG (40% of Basic Pay) . If ordered within 60 days of each other : later spouse gets Nil (Zero) .

If both husband and wife are transferred separately, can they claim transport for both of their personal cars?
Yes , provided that both cars are registered separately in their respective individual names.

On retirement, if both spouses are Central Government servants and choose to settle at different stations, can they claim TA separately?
Yes, fully . They can claim Travelling Allowance for their respective journeys and personal effects separately, up to their respective individual entitlements.

Can husband and wife (both Central Government servants) declare separate Home Towns under LTC rules?
Yes , they are allowed to declare separate Home Towns and claim LTC separately under the normal provisions.

Is the family of a deceased temporary employee with 2 years of continuous service eligible for death-in-service TA concessions?
No . Temporary employees must have rendered at least three (3) years of regular continuous service for their family to be eligible (SR 148, GIO (1)).

What security requirement is mandatory to draw a death-in-service family TA advance?
The family must provide a surety of a permanent employee of comparable or higher status (SR 148, GIO (3)).

What is the adjustment timeline for a death-in-service family TA advance?
Within one month (30 days) of the completion of the journey (or of the last batch's journey if travelling in multiple batches).

What is the Immediate Relief Advance amount given to the family upon the demise of a Government servant while in service?
A fixed sum of ₹25,000 (under DoE O.M. No. 12(1)/2016-E.II(A) dated 20.06.2018).

Can the ₹25,000 Immediate Relief Advance on demise be paid in cash?
Yes . Under CGA O.M. dated 30.04.2021, DDOs are permitted to disburse this ₹25,000 in cash immediately from Imprest or other sources to prevent delays.

How is the ₹25,000 Immediate Relief Advance on demise adjusted/recovered?
It is strictly adjusted and recovered from the first terminal payments (Death Gratuity, GPF balance, leave salary, or pay arrears) due to the deceased employee.

Does a Government servant who quits service by resignation qualify for retirement TA concessions?
No strictly . Retirement TA is admissible only on retiring pension, superannuation, invalid, or compensation pension. It is strictly disallowed on resignation, dismissal, or removal.

How does road mileage scale on transfer for road journeys between places not connected by rail?
It scales by family count: 1 mileage for self/self+1; 2 mileages if 2 members accompany; 3 mileages if more than 2 accompany (SR 116 GIO (5)).

Are GST, Service Tax, and cess charged on personal effects transport bills reimbursable?
Yes, fully reimbursable up to the employee's admissible entitlement limit (SR 116 GIO (10)).

Does the employee have to prove that possessing a vehicle at the old/new station was in the public interest to claim transport costs on transfer?
No . Under SR 116, the possession of the conveyance at either station need not be in the public interest to claim reimbursement.

Who bears the Travelling Allowance expenses for an employee's return journey upon reversion from deputation?
The borrowing department/organization (the office they are leaving) bears and pays the return Transfer TA.

Who bears the Transfer TA during Foreign Service under SR 206?
Both the onward and return Transfer TA are borne and paid entirely by the foreign employer .

Are local conveyance or Daily Allowance reimbursable under LTC?
No. Daily Allowance, hotel charges at destination, and local travel/conveyance are not reimbursable under the cited LTC rule.

Can a Government servant travel by own car or hired taxi on normal LTC?
Normally no, subject to the specific exceptions. Verify the applicable Divyang provision or unconnected-stretch rule; the latter is limited to a maximum of 100 km each side under the cited order.

What are the LTC claim-submission timelines?
Normally, one month after completion of the return journey if an advance was drawn (Rule 15(vi)), and three months if no advance was drawn (Rule 14). Under DoPT O.M. 31011/18/2023-Estt.A-IV dated 21.12.2023, limited relaxation with FA concurrence may extend the no-advance case to six months, or the advance case to three months if the full advance plus penal interest is refunded in one lump sum. See the official DoPT 01.07.2025 FAQ, Q35.

What is the time limit for revision of a TA bill or filing a supplementary claim?
Three (3) years from the date of the original claim, as per GFR procedural provisions.

What determines whether a change of duty station is a "Transfer" vs a "Tour"?
Use the actual transfer/duty order and the applicable current rule. The official DoE 13.07.2017 O.M. preserves SR 114 admissibility conditions but does not reproduce them; duration alone is not treated here as a verified universal classification test. Check separate CTG, family-travel, baggage and conveyance conditions before deciding.

Is any mileage payable for the first kilometre of a road journey by own car/taxi?
No. Under the FR/SR rules (carry-over from 6th CPC), the first kilometre is a "dead kilometre" — no mileage is payable. Calculation begins from the 2nd kilometre onward.

What is the TA entitlement if a transfer involves movement within the same Urban Agglomeration (same city) with a change of residence?
A restricted CTG of 1/3rd of the normal rate (26.67% of Basic Pay), plus actual transportation charges for personal effects within the city. If no change of residence, CTG = Nil.

What TA/DA do retired employees (pensioners) get when summoned for a departmental inquiry or court deposition?
TA and DA as per their original pay level at the time of retirement for the duration of the visit. This is distinct from re-employment. No LTC is admissible.

What is SR 172 and who does it apply to?
SR 172 governs TA for non-Government candidates called for UPSC/SSC written tests or interviews. They get Second Class (Mail/Express) train fare by shortest route, provided the distance exceeds 80 km (SC/ST candidates are exempt from the 80 km minimum). No DA for halts.

How is DA calculated in a "split duty" scenario where an employee leaves HQ, returns mid-day, and goes out again?
The total absence from HQ within the single calendar day is aggregated . E.g., 4 hours morning + 4 hours afternoon = 8 hours total → falls in the 6–12 hours band → 70% DA . The two periods are NOT treated as separate journeys.

Can a Railway employee eligible for a Duty Pass claim TA if they choose not to use the Duty Pass?
No , unless a specific relaxation is granted by the competent authority. If eligible for a Duty Pass but choosing to travel otherwise, TA for that leg is not admissible.

Which Defence/Armed Forces personnel are governed by PCDA rules instead of the civilian DoE TA Rules?
Defence Personnel (Army, Navy, Air Force) and personnel of CISF, Assam Rifles, and other CAPFs are governed by separate PCDA (Pune) orders — not by the civilian DoE O.M. No.19030/1/2017-E.IV.

For an overnight train departing at 8:00 PM and arriving at 2:00 AM next day, what DA is payable for Day 2?
Day 2 absence is from midnight to 2:00 AM = 2 hours → falls in the <6 hours band → 30% of the daily food lump sum . Day 2 is NOT treated as a full travel day.
