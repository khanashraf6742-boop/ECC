### [ch05-004] card L1270-1284 Very High-Yield Composite Transfer Grant (CTG)

Very High-Yield Composite Transfer Grant (CTG)

Exact-Source Fact Card
Composite Transfer Grant is payable at 80% of the last month's basic pay for an eligible transfer involving a change of station located 20 km or more apart. For a transfer below 20 km or within the same city/station, the 13.07.2017 O.M. allows one-third of the normal CTG only if an actual change of residence is involved ; otherwise CTG is nil. For transfer to/from the Island territories of Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep , CTG is paid at the enhanced rate of 100% of last month's basic pay . Where both spouses (both Central Govt servants) are transferred from the same station to the same station within 6 months — but the second transfer is after 60 days of the first — full CTG is paid to the spouse transferred earlier and 50% CTG to the spouse transferred later. CTG on retirement was separately clarified admissible vide O.M. dated 06.01.2022 .




Scenario | CTG rate |

Transfer ≥ 20 km, general case | 80% of last month's basic pay |

Transfer < 20 km / same city | One-third of normal CTG if an actual residence change occurs; otherwise nil |

Transfer to/from A&N / Lakshadweep | 100% of last month's basic pay |

Both spouses transferred, same O-D, within 6 months (2nd after 60 days) | Full CTG to earlier-transferred spouse; 50% to later-transferred spouse |


RED-FLAG TRAP Flag: "CTG is always 80% regardless of distance." Fix: For an eligible transfer at 20 km or more , ordinary CTG is 80%; below 20 km or within the same city/station, one-third of normal CTG applies only if an actual residence change occurs , otherwise CTG is nil. Specified A&N/Lakshadweep transfers have the 100% rate. Fix-phrase: "Below 20 km: residence change matters; the islands have a separate 100% rate."






### [ch05-005] card L1285-1304 Very High-Yield — source status open Transfer vs Tour — apply the order and current SR 114 conditions

Very High-Yield — source status open Transfer vs Tour — apply the order and current SR 114 conditions

Primary-source boundary
Transfer TA and tour TA are distinct categories, but a duration-only “180-day switch” is not established by the primary text retrieved here. The official DoE O.M. dated 13.07.2017 says the general admissibility conditions prescribed in SR 114 continue to apply to transfer TA; it does not reproduce SR 114. The current complete Central SR 114 text, its applicable orders/amendments, and the exact transfer-order conditions remain unverified in this review.


When a transfer order is involved: Read its purpose, stated temporary/permanent nature and period, any extension/revision, station and residence facts, and the operative TA rule before deciding which components are admissible. If classed as a transfer, fare, CTG, personal-effects and conveyance components each retain their own conditions.

When duty is described as temporary or a tour: Do not infer the full TA package—or the absence of CTG/personal-effects transport—solely from the number of days. Apply the current rule and the order that actually authorized the movement.

Training is a separate question: Secondary transcriptions report training-specific options and schedules, but current SR 164/GIO text and their status are unresolved. Do not merge a training rule into a general SR 114 conclusion.

### [ch05-006] micronote L1305-1316 

Same City / Urban Agglomeration (UA) — Transfer Within the Same Station

What happens when an officer is moved from one office building to another within the same city (e.g., from North Block to South Block in Delhi, or from one campus to another in Bengaluru)?

When an officer is moved between offices in the same city or Urban Agglomeration (UA), keep the residence fact distinct from the station boundary:


No change of residence: If the move is within the same UA and does not involve an actual change of residence, the guide states no TA is payable (CTG nil; no fare reimbursement).

With change of residence within the same UA: The move is treated as a transfer within the same station. The cited rule gives one-third of normal CTG (1/3 × 80% = 26.67% of last basic pay), plus actual transportation charges for personal effects within the city.

Station boundary: The guide describes “station” for TA purposes as the entire Census-defined UA, not merely the municipal boundary; its example is Delhi UA including Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.


Exam illustration: A North Block-to-South Block move (3 km) in Delhi with a residence change is shown as one-third CTG, not nil or full CTG, because both offices are within the same UA. Verify the case facts and applicable order before applying.





### [ch05-007] card L1318-1349 Very High-Yield Transfer TA — Spousal Scaling, Multiple Road Mileages, and Tax Reimbursements (SR 116)

Very High-Yield Transfer TA — Spousal Scaling, Multiple Road Mileages, and Tax Reimbursements (SR 116)

Exact-Source Fact Card
Under Swamy's Handbook-2024 and GIOs below SR 116 , several critical administrative and financial nuances govern Travelling Allowance on Transfer:


1. Multiple Road Mileages for Road Travel (GIO (5), Para IV, SR 116):
If the transfer journey is performed between places not connected by rail otherwise than by public bus (e.g. by private car or hired vehicle), road mileage is payable at scaled rates based on the number of family members travelling:

One Road Mileage: For self, or self and one additional family member.

Two Road Mileages: If exactly two family members accompany the Government servant.

Three Road Mileages: If more than two family members accompany the Government servant.




2. Reimbursement of Service Tax, GST, and Cess (GIO (10), SR 116):
All elements of Service Tax, GST, and Education Cess charged by transport operators in the bill for the transportation of personal effects are fully reimbursable up to the employee's maximum entitlement ceiling.

3. Conveyance Transport "Public Interest" Exemption (Item V, GIO (1), SR 116):
For claiming the cost of transporting a personal vehicle (car/scooter) on transfer, the possession of the conveyance at the old or new station need not be in the public interest (reimbursable as long as it is owned strictly by the employee).

4. Department of Posts Choice Posting Concession (SR 114):
Under postal-specific GIOs, a posting to a place of choice on the completion of a full tenure is not treated as a transfer on own request . Consequently, such officials are fully entitled to Transfer TA and CTG under SR 114.

### [ch05-008] card L1350-1363 High-Yield Transportation of personal effects (packing grant) & conveyance

High-Yield Transportation of personal effects (packing grant) & conveyance

Exact-Source Fact Card
Transportation of personal effects by road is reimbursed at currently applicable (25%-enhanced) rates of ₹62.50/km (Level 6 and above) , ₹31.25/km (Level 5) , and ₹18.75/km (Level 4 and below) . By train/steamer, entitlement is by weight: Level 12 and above / Level 6–11 → 6,000 kg (by goods train/4-wheeler wagon/1 container — double container for Level 12+, single container for Level 6–11); Level 5 → 3,000 kg ; Level 4 and below → 1,500 kg . Separately, transportation of a conveyance (motor car, motor cycle, scooter, moped, bicycle) is reimbursed: Level 6 and above may transport 1 motor car/similar or 1 motor cycle/scooter; Level 5 and below may transport 1 motorcycle/scooter/moped/bicycle (SR 116). Possession of the conveyance at the old or new station need not be shown to be in the public interest.




Pay Level | By Train/Steamer | By Road (Current Rate) |

12 and above | 6,000 kg (double container) | ₹62.50/km |

6 to 11 | 6,000 kg (single container) | ₹62.50/km |

5 | 3,000 kg | ₹31.25/km |

4 and below | 1,500 kg | ₹18.75/km |


Analogy Think of the personal-effects entitlement like an airline baggage allowance that scales with "class of ticket" (Pay Level) — senior levels get a bigger "container," junior levels get a smaller weight cap.






### [ch05-009] card L1364-1371 Moderate-Yield Special-region transfers & TA entitlement basis on transfer

Moderate-Yield Special-region transfers & TA entitlement basis on transfer

Exact-Source Fact Card
In case of transfer from one post to another, TA entitlement is determined on the basis of the pay of the lower of the two posts/grades (i.e., old vs new). Special provisions for TA on transfer to/from the North-Eastern Region, Union Territories of Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep Islands and Ladakh were clarified/enhanced vide O.M. No.19030/1/2017-E.IV dated 16.06.2022 .



Memory Hook "On transfer, you're paid like the junior of your two posts — never the senior."
