### [ch16-009] card L3739-3755 Definition Q3. Conditions for Technical Resignation

Definition Q3. Conditions for Technical Resignation


Question: Which of the following is NOT a condition for treating a resignation as "technical resignation"?


(a) The employee applied through proper channel
(b) The employee was selected for a post in the same or another Government Department
(c) The employee must have completed 3 years of service
(d) The resignation was required for administrative reasons to take up the new post





✓ Answer: (c)

Reasoning: Technical-resignation status itself has no stated minimum-service condition in the cited provisions. The two issues must not be conflated.







### [ch16-011] card L3757-3773 Special Case Q4. Applied Before Joining Government Service

Special Case Q4. Applied Before Joining Government Service


Question: An employee applied for a competitive exam BEFORE joining Government service. On selection, he was required to resign from his current post. Under what conditions can this be treated as technical resignation?


(a) Never — since the application was not through proper channel
(b) Only if he had informed the new department about his current employment
(c) If he intimated the details on joining AND the authority is satisfied that the application would have been forwarded through proper channel had he been in service
(d) Only with special approval from the Secretary of the Department





✓ Answer: (c)

Reasoning: Under DoPT O.M. Para 2.5, this benefit IS available if: (i) the employee intimated the details immediately on joining, (ii) at resignation, specifically requested it be treated as technical, and (iii) the authority is satisfied that had the employee been in service on the application date, it would have been forwarded through proper channel.







### [ch16-013] card L3775-3791 Lien Rules Q5. Lien Retention Period

Lien Rules Q5. Lien Retention Period


Question: A permanent Government servant appointed to another Central Government department/office or State Government post tenders technical resignation. Under the cited lien rule, the normal reversion/lien period is:


(a) 1 year only
(b) 2 years normally, 3 years in exceptional cases
(c) 5 years
(d) Until retirement





✓ Answer: (b)

Reasoning: Under DoPT O.M. Para 3.1(i): "A permanent Government servant appointed in another Central Government Department/Office/State Government has to resign from his parent department unless he reverts within a period of 2 years, or 3 years in exceptional cases." The exceptional case (Para 3.1(ii)) is when the employee is not confirmed in the new department within 2 years.







### [ch16-015] card L3793-3809 Definition Q6. Word "Technical" Not Mentioned

Definition Q6. Word "Technical" Not Mentioned


Question: A Government servant submits a plain resignation (not mentioning "technical") to join another Government post for which he had applied through proper channel. This resignation:


(a) Will NOT be treated as technical resignation since the word was not used
(b) Will be treated as technical resignation if all conditions are met, regardless of whether the word "technical" was mentioned
(c) Requires a fresh application to convert it to technical resignation
(d) Is valid only if the new department specifically requests it





✓ Answer: (b)

Reasoning: Under DoPT O.M. Para 2.1: "The resignation will be treated as technical resignation if these conditions are met, even if the Government servant has not mentioned the word 'Technical' while submitting his resignation." This is a very commonly tested point.







### [ch16-017] card L3811-3827 Core Rule Q7. Benefits NOT Carried Forward

Core Rule Q7. Benefits NOT Carried Forward


Question: Which benefit is NOT carried forward on technical resignation?


(a) Earned Leave balance
(b) LTC entitlement
(c) Pay protection
(d) Minimum qualifying service for promotion in the old post (if employee later reverts)





✓ Answer: (d)

Reasoning (scope-limited): DoPT O.M. para 2.4 states that, if a Government servant rejoins the substantive parent post, service spent in the other department after technical resignation does not count toward minimum qualifying service for promotion in the higher post there. Among these options, (d) is the intended distinction, but do not say that every other benefit is carried forward on identical terms: LTC has a separate fresh-recruit rule for specified cases under para 2.3, and leave, pay protection, pension/GPF/NPS each follow their own conditions. This item and answer remain unverified as part of the full bank review.

### [ch16-023] card L3867-3883 LTC Rules Q10. LTC Entitlement on Technical Resignation

LTC Rules Q10. LTC Entitlement on Technical Resignation


Question: A Government servant resigns within 8 years of initial appointment and joins another post in Government after a valid technical resignation. Under the cited DoPT LTC instruction, the employee’s LTC entitlement is regulated as follows:


(a) Fresh LTC block starts from the date of joining the new department
(b) The employee is treated as a fresh recruit for 8 years from the date of initial appointment under Government
(c) LTC is completely forfeited on technical resignation
(d) The employee retains the same LTC block and entitlement without any modification





✓ Answer: (b)

Reasoning: Under DoPT O.M. Para 2.3: "In case of a Government Servant who resigns within 8 years of his appointment and joins another post in the Government after Technical Resignation, the Government Servant will be treated as a fresh recruit for a period of 8 years from the date of his initial appointment under Government." So if he has served 4 years already, he is treated as a fresh recruit for the remaining 4 years of the 8-year fresh recruit period.

### [ch16-027] card L3902-3918 Reversion Q12. Reverting to Old Department

Reversion Q12. Reverting to Old Department


Question: A confirmed employee joins a new post on technical resignation. After 6 months, he wants to revert to his old department. Which statement is correct?


(a) He cannot revert — technical resignation is final
(b) He can revert if his lien is still valid (within 2-3 years)
(c) He can revert only with special permission from DoPT
(d) He can revert but loses all service benefits in the new department





✓ Answer: (b)

Reasoning: If he retained lien on the old post (which is valid for 2 years, extendable to 3 in exceptional cases), he can revert. The service in the new department counts for pension, leave, and increment, but NOT for seniority or promotion eligibility in the old department (DoPT O.M. Para 2.4).
