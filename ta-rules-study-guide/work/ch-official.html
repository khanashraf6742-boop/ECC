  • (b) Only if they officially express inability does the claim for the attendant’s fare arise; for air, airlines are expected to assist, but the employee can take a personal attendant by Indian Railways even if one is officially provided at destination.

  • (d) Only if the competent Medical Authority certifies that the person compulsorily requires assistance for travel (certificate from a Government Civil Hospital designated for the disability, obtained through the Head of Department); the HoD must then satisfy himself of the need.
  • (e) On tour (domestic or foreign) the deputing authority must record in writing that the tour is necessary for the officer’s duties.
  • (f) On training only if recorded that the training is mandatory for career progression or induction training for probationers.
  • (g) No Daily Allowance to the attendant; mileage allowance at the same rate and class as the employee’s entitlement (subject to austerity instructions); no separate mileage for road conveyance except where travel is by public bus.
  • (h) Transport Allowance at double rate is for daily commuting; no TA is admissible for training at headquarters, so none for the attendant either.

S13 — DoPT LTC air-booking O.M.s of 29.08.2022 and October 2023 (read from the enclosures)

O.M.OrderProviso
Source: DoPT O.M. 31011/12/2022-Estt.A-IV, 29.08.2022; O.M. 31011/11/2023-Pers. Policy A-IV, October 2023
Official-text layer — not part of the original study guide
  • 29.08.2022: for LTC air journeys tickets only from the three ATAs (choice left to the office/employee; no agency charges); cheapest fare in the entitled class, preferably non-stop, in the desired 3-hour slot with a 10% optimisation band; keep the ATA webpage print-out; booking at least 21 days ahead is encouraged; cancellation under 24 hours needs a self-declared justification (ATAs to give nil cancellation charges; till then reimbursed where beyond the employee’s control); one ticket per leg; register the official e-mail; unauthorised booking — relaxation by FA / HoD not below Joint Secretary; no mileage points.

  • October 2023 modifications: the three ATAs display the cheapest flight and flights up to 10% dearer in the desired slot — booking on their websites is itself proof of the cheapest fare; tickets carry the word “LTC”; ATAs register employees without official e-mail on the office’s request; the railhead print-out rule for Special Dispensation travel continues.
